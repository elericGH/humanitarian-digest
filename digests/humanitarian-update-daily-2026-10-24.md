## 🌏 Tier 1 — Pacific & Asia-Pacific
**Pacific churches challenge Israel's PIF partner status.** RNZ Pacific reports the Pacific Conference of Churches, representing churches and national councils across 18 countries and territories, wants Israel's new partnership with the Pacific Islands Forum reviewed and all engagement suspended until Israel complies with international court rulings and UN resolutions. The call follows Israel's announcement this week that it was accepted as a second-tier PIF partner ([RNZ Pacific](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/pacific/2098703/test-of-regionalism-pacific-church-group-calls-for-review-of-israel-s-pif-role)).

**New Caledonia critical minerals deal boycotted.** Pro-independence members of New Caledonia's Congress boycotted the signing of a non-binding EU–New Caledonia memorandum of understanding on raw materials, citing a lack of local consultation. The MOU was signed on Tuesday ([RNZ Pacific](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/pacific/2096107/new-caledonia-pro-independence-leaders-boycott-eu-critical-minerals-deal)).

**Bougainville independence debate continues in PNG Parliament.** Every MP is being given a chance to speak on whether to ratify the 2019 non-binding referendum, in which 97.7 percent voted for independence. The debate resumes in November before an eventual vote ([RNZ Pacific](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/pacific/2085694/bougainville-scars-acknowledged-as-papua-new-guinea-mps-near-big-decision)).

**Other regional items (headline only).** The Diplomat: [Myanmar's Aung San Suu Kyi speaks with son for first time since military coup](https://thediplomat.com/2026/10/myanmars-aung-san-suu-kyi-speaks-with-son-for-first-time-since-military-coup/). ## 🌍 Tier 2 — Middle East & Africa
**Yemen: Ta'iz siege deepens.** At a Geneva briefing on Friday, OCHA spokesperson Jens Laerke said Ta'iz has been cut off for 20 days, with fighting making it "impossible for us to get in and impossible for people to get out". WFP says more than 800,000 people in Ta'iz are cut off from aid. Truck-delivered water costs have tripled and cooking gas is largely unavailable ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/23/time-running-out-to-reach-yemens-taiz-amid-deepening-crisis-un-says)). OCHA says more than 256,000 people have been displaced in Yemen since the start of August, and that 42 health facilities in Ta'iz city report acute shortages of fuel, oxygen and medicines ([OCHA](https://www.unocha.org/news/todays-top-news-yemen-ethiopia-democratic-republic-congo-ukraine-haiti)). Related (headline only): [UNICEF Djibouti flash update No. 4 on refugee influx from Yemen](https://reliefweb.int/report/djibouti/unicef-djibouti-humanitarian-flash-update-no-4-refugee-influx-yemen-19-october-2026).

**Ethiopia: drone strikes and Tigray fighting.** OCHA reports drone strikes affecting civilians and infrastructure in Tigray, including one that hit a local NGO's humanitarian warehouse on Wednesday. Multiple strikes between 15 and 21 October in eastern Amhara reportedly killed at least 15 civilians ([OCHA](https://www.unocha.org/news/todays-top-news-yemen-ethiopia-democratic-republic-congo-ukraine-haiti)). AP reports a Tigray caretaker administration official urged rebel forces to disarm, hours after a rebel commander told fighters to keep resisting; the TPLF withdrew from Mekelle in early October ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/23/tigray-official-urges-rebels-to-disarm)).

**DR Congo: Ebola in an eighth province, cholera spreading.** WFP says Ebola has spread to an eighth DRC province, that nearly 3.5 million people in Ebola-affected areas face Crisis-level hunger or worse (IPC 3+), and that it needs USD 214 million over six months ([WFP](https://www.wfp.org/news/ebola-spreads-eighth-drc-province-wfp-delivers-food-builds-treatment-centres-and-keeps)). OCHA reports more than 55,000 suspected cholera cases in the DRC between January and October; in Tanganyika Province all health zones are affected and the Kalemie treatment centre is overwhelmed ([OCHA](https://www.unocha.org/news/todays-top-news-yemen-ethiopia-democratic-republic-congo-ukraine-haiti)). Headline only: [Multi-Country Ebola Bundibugyo Outbreak Update n°23](https://reliefweb.int/report/democratic-republic-congo/multi-country-ebola-bundibugyo-outbreak-update-ndeg23).

**West Bank: movement restrictions.** Al Jazeera reports that Israeli forces have kept the four entrances to Madama village, south of Nablus, blocked with earthen barriers since 11 October, restricting more than 2,500 residents and disrupting schools and medical care ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/23/israeli-roadblocks-disrupt-schools-and-medical-care-in-west-bank-village)). Headline only: [Israel locks down occupied West Bank after Hebron shooting](https://www.aljazeera.com/video/newsfeed/2026/10/23/israel-locks-down-occupied-west-bank-after-hebron-shooting).

**Kenya: building collapse in Nairobi.** A multistorey building under construction collapsed on Friday around 1pm local time in Eastleigh. Interior Minister Kipchumba Murkomen said 67 workers were on site and 66 were unharmed or had escaped, while about 11 people in ground-floor shops are unaccounted for. Rescue efforts involve national and county government and the Red Cross ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/23/many-feared-trapped-after-multistorey-building-collapses-in-kenya)).

**Other items (headline only).** [Central African Republic: incidents affecting humanitarian workers (September 2026)](https://reliefweb.int/report/central-african-republic/central-african-republic-overview-incidents-affecting-humanitarian-workers-september-2026); [Lebanon Humanitarian Fund at a glance, October 2026](https://reliefweb.int/report/lebanon/lebanon-humanitarian-fund-lhf-glance-october-2026); [The New Humanitarian Cheat Sheet: El Fasher's dark anniversary, a worsening siege in Yemen](https://www.thenewhumanitarian.org/news/2026/10/23/el-fashers-siege-yemen-protests-paris-cheat-sheet) (headline only).

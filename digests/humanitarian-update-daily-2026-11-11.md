## 🌏 Tier 1 — Pacific & Asia-Pacific
**Pacific disease alerts:** The latest Pacific epidemic and emerging disease alert map (as of 10 November) reports that Marshall Islands' 18th dengue situation report (15 August–2 November) records 171 positive cases (Majuro 128, Ebeye 43), with 15 hospitalisations and no deaths; the red alert for DENV-1 remains in effect. In Samoa, 30 new dengue cases were reported for EpiWeek 43, down 43% on the previous week, with 20,510 clinically diagnosed cases and nine deaths since 1 January 2025; the blue alert remains. [ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/map/world/epidemic-and-emerging-disease-alerts-pacific-10-november-2026)

**El Niño and water security:** RNZ Pacific reports that one of the strongest El Niño events on record is straining Pacific water systems, with generally dry conditions through Melanesia and the Southern Cook Islands more vulnerable to scarcity while the Northern Cooks see heavier rainfall, according to the Pacific Community (SPC). [RNZ Pacific](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/pacific/2481612/the-pacific-s-water-security-challenge-during-a-searing-el-nino)

**COP31:** Pacific artists are presenting a 25-photograph climate exhibition at COP31 in Türkiye this week. [RNZ Pacific](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/pacific/2498963/cop31-opens-in-turkiye-as-global-temperature-flirts-with-1-point-5-degreesc-line)

## 🌍 Tier 2 — Middle East & Africa
**Gaza floods:** OCHA reports that after weekend heavy rain, over 200,000 people may be affected in Gaza; damage was reported to more than 40,000 family shelters, over 900 latrines and nine health facilities. Support had reached about 750 households (roughly 4,500 people), and partners say funding and Israeli approvals are needed to bring in heavy machinery, with restrictions behind the "Yellow Line" limiting relocation options. [OCHA](https://www.unocha.org/news/todays-top-news-mali-occupied-palestinian-territory-yemen-haiti) Medical Aid for Palestinians says an estimated 65% of Gaza's population lives in tents or makeshift shelters. [ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/occupied-palestinian-territory/gazas-winter-floods-expose-deadly-consequences-israels-restrictions-aid) Al Jazeera reports at least five deaths, including four children, from the floods. [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/10/f1-champion-lewis-hamilton-visits-gaza-border)

**Yemen — Ta'iz:** OCHA says supply routes into Ta'iz remain cut by fighting; the UN Human Rights Office has verified 284 civilian casualties since August (70 killed, 214 injured) and nearly 330,000 people have been displaced across Yemen since August. A vaccination campaign targeting more than 125,000 children began in Marib on 7 November. [OCHA](https://www.unocha.org/news/todays-top-news-mali-occupied-palestinian-territory-yemen-haiti)

**Mali:** UN relief chief Tom Fletcher is on a three-day visit; OCHA says more than 5 million people (1 in 5) need humanitarian assistance this year. [OCHA](https://www.unocha.org/news/todays-top-news-mali-occupied-palestinian-territory-yemen-haiti)

**DR Congo — Ebola:** IOM reports 71 Ebola alerts identified at surveillance points and a USD 59.8 million regional funding shortfall. WHO figures cited as of 31 October: 10,467 confirmed cases and 5,103 deaths across 67 health zones in eight provinces, with imported cases in Kenya and Nigeria. [ReliefWeb/IOM](https://reliefweb.int/report/democratic-republic-congo/71-ebola-cases-detected-drc-routes-funding-gap-risks-outbreak-remaining-out-control-iom-warns)

**Sudan and Somalia (headline only):** "Attacks on Health Care in Sudan, 16-31 October 2026" [ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/sudan/attacks-health-care-sudan-16-31-october-2026); "Security forces seized top court, Somalia's ex-chief justice says" [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/video/newsfeed/2026/11/10/security-forces-seized-top-court-somalias-ex-chief-justice-says).

## 🌐 Tier 3 — Rest of the World
**Haiti:** El Salvador's president said Salvadoran troops targeted the 400 Mawozo gang's headquarters near Port-au-Prince; the operation reportedly killed 20 people. Amnesty International urged both governments to publish the deployment agreement and ensure compliance with human rights law. [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/10/salvadoran-troops-target-haitian-gang-leaving-20-dead)

## 🇦🇺 Australian Priority Alerts

**ACFID calls for $10 million in new Australian humanitarian funding for El Niño preparedness.** In its latest edition of The Weekly, ACFID says it has continued efforts to raise awareness of the impacts of the "Super El Nino" on vulnerable communities, including a proposal calling on the Australian Government to commit $10 million in new and additional humanitarian funding to support El Niño preparedness across the Asia-Pacific. ACFID frames this as an opportunity to invest in early action with local communities and partners. The edition also notes that COP31 has started and that ACFID has published a blog post on what COP31 needs to deliver for the Pacific. Policy and advocacy workshops are planned in Melbourne (19 November) and Sydney (24 November). ([ACFID — The Weekly, 11/11/2026](https://acfid.asn.au/the_weekly/wednesday-11-11-2026/))

## 🌍 Tier 2 — Middle East & Africa

**Gaza: UN relief chief demands immediate entry of winter supplies.** In a statement on 11 November, Tom Fletcher said delays, blockages and funding cuts are having consequences, "lives lost, a population once again at great risk", and called for all crossings to open, NGO partners to be allowed to work and commercial markets to function. The needed items include durable shelter, warm clothes, blankets, bedding, safe heating, generators, fuel and equipment to pump out water. ([OCHA](https://www.unocha.org/news/no-more-delay-un-relief-chief-urges-immediate-entry-winter-supplies-gaza)) NRC reports winter storms flooding thousands of tents (headline only). ([ReliefWeb/NRC](https://reliefweb.int/report/occupied-palestinian-territory/gaza-winter-storms-flood-thousands-tents-nrc)) Al Jazeera reports an Israeli strike on tents in Gaza City's Sabra neighbourhood killed three people. ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/video/newsfeed/2026/11/11/scenes-show-aftermath-of-horrific-israeli-strike-on-gaza-tents))

**Lebanon: more than one million people face acute food shortages.** FAO, WFP and Lebanon's Ministry of Agriculture say nearly a quarter of the population is affected after two conflicts in under two years, and the number is expected to reach 1.15 million by mid-2027. About 300,000 people remain displaced, down from over one million. ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/11/one-million-people-in-lebanon-facing-acute-food-insecurity-un-says)) WFP also published a food insecurity analysis (headline only). ([ReliefWeb/WFP](https://reliefweb.int/report/lebanon/food-insecurity-persists-lebanon-fragile-conditions-stall-recovery-efforts-new-analy))

**DR Congo, Sudan, Chad, CAR (headlines only).** OCHA flash update #1 reports new armed violence causing displacement in South Kivu ([ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/democratic-republic-congo/rd-congo-sud-kivu-flash-update-1-de-nouvelles-violences-armees-en)). UNHCR published flash update #60 on a new influx of Sudanese refugees into Chad ([ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/chad/flash-updates-60-nouvel-afflux-de-refugies-soudanais-au-tchad)) and a Sudan Situation monthly update ([ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/sudan/sudan-situation-unhcr-monthly-external-update-123-september-2026)). CAR Situation Report No. 93 was issued ([ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/central-african-republic/central-african-republic-situation-report-no-93-10-november-2026)). OCHA's daily top news covers oPt, Mali, Somalia, DRC, Sudan and Haiti ([OCHA](https://www.unocha.org/news/todays-top-news-occupied-palestinian-territory-mali-somalia-democratic-republic-congo-sudan)).

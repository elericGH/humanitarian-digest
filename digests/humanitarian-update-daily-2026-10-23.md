## 🌏 Tier 1 — Pacific & Asia-Pacific
**Bougainville independence debate, Papua New Guinea.** RNZ Pacific reports that emotional scars from Bougainville's history surfaced as PNG's parliament debates whether to ratify the result of the 2019 non-binding referendum, in which 97.7 percent voted for independence. Every MP is being given a chance to speak; debate resumes in November before an eventual ratification vote. [RNZ Pacific](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/pacific/2085694/bougainville-scars-acknowledged-as-papua-new-guinea-mps-near-big-decision)

**Vanuatu government warns over "second republic" claims.** Prime Minister Jotham Napat voiced serious concern over claims by a group on Gaua Island, Torba province, of a "second republic" and a new flag; the government issued an official warning against any attempt to establish a separate republic or government. [RNZ Pacific](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/pacific/2083025/vanuatu-government-warns-against-claims-of-breakaway-second-republic)

**Bangladesh measles outbreak.** An IFRC DREF update says sustained transmission continues across all eight divisions: 208,570 suspected cases, 22,840 laboratory-confirmed cases and 185,390 measles-related hospital admissions between 15 March and 14 October 2026, with about 1,000 suspected admissions daily (peak 1,251 on 8 October) and 1,073 suspected deaths. [IFRC via ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/bangladesh/bangladesh-measles-dref-operation-update-mdrbd039-october-2026)

**Myanmar displacement data (headline only).** UNHCR published updated Myanmar IDP-number and refugee route-based snapshot products (as of 12 Oct and 30 Sep 2026). [UNHCR via ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/myanmar/number-internally-displaced-people-myanmar-12-oct-2026)

## 🌍 Tier 2 — Middle East & Africa
**Sudan: drone and air strikes on populated areas.** The ICRC says aerial attacks, mostly likely by armed drones, have caused a worrying increase in civilian harm in recent weeks, with hundreds reportedly killed in residential areas, markets and displacement sites. ICRC-supported hospitals recorded over 2,700 weapon-wounded patients in the first half of 2026. [ICRC](https://www.icrc.org/en/news-release/sudan-aerial-attacks-populated-areas-are-endangering-civilian-lives) OCHA reports an air strike in Tina locality, North Darfur reportedly killed five civilians including two Emergency Response Rooms volunteers; the Sudan response plan is just over 46 per cent funded ($1.32 billion of nearly $2.9 billion) and the Humanitarian Coordinator warned food and nutrition stocks could run out by early next year. [OCHA](https://www.unocha.org/news/todays-top-news-yemen-sudan-occupied-palestinian-territory-ukraine)

**Yemen: Ta'iz access and rising displacement.** OCHA says the UN Human Rights Office has verified 262 civilian casualties since August (66 killed, 196 injured); WFP switched to cash assistance in Ta'iz because access is disrupted, aiming for about 132,000 people. [OCHA](https://www.unocha.org/news/todays-top-news-yemen-sudan-occupied-palestinian-territory-ukraine) Amnesty International called on the Huthis to immediately open roads in and around Ta'iz as fighting nears densely populated areas. [Amnesty via ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/yemen/yemen-huthis-must-open-taiz-roads-immediately-thousands-civilians-lives-are-risk) International Medical Corps reports 251,125 people displaced across 10 governorates, up about 27% since its last report. [IMC via ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/yemen/yemen-emergency-response-situation-report-5-october-22-2026)

**Ebola (Bundibugyo strain) spreads to more countries.** WHO says the outbreak announced in DR Congo in mid-May, which has killed more than 4,000 people there, is now "more complex", with cases in Kenya and Nigeria; Nigeria confirmed its first case this week and investigators are examining whether the Nigerian and Kenyan patients crossed paths at Entebbe airport on 3 October. [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/22/who-warns-more-complex-ebola-outbreak-now-present-in-3-african-countries)

**Gaza and the West Bank.** Al Jazeera reports Israeli attacks killed four Palestinians in 24 hours, as a rights group warns of renewed famine risk from restrictions on incoming goods. [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/22/israeli-attacks-in-gaza-kill-four-including-a-young-girl) OCHA's Humanitarian Coordinator visited Jenin with diplomats to see the effect of settler attacks and access limits on the olive harvest. [OCHA](https://www.unocha.org/news/todays-top-news-yemen-sudan-occupied-palestinian-territory-ukraine)

[OCHA](https://www.unocha.org/news/aid-community-gearing-deeper-food-insecurity)

**WFP cyber-attack (headline only).** The New Humanitarian podcast episode "Why the WFP cyber-attack matters" discusses the WFP Gaza data breach. [The New Humanitarian](https://www.thenewhumanitarian.org/podcasts/2026/10/22/rethinking-humanitarianism-why-wfp-cyber-attack-matters)

**Mozambique (headline only).** OCHA story: "We found nothing when we came back": Mozambican families rebuild after displacement. [OCHA](https://www.unocha.org/news/we-found-nothing-when-we-came-back-mozambican-families-rebuild-after-displacement)

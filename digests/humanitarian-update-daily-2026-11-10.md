## 🌏 Tier 1 — Pacific & Asia-Pacific
Taiwan's Foreign Ministry has confirmed it is terminating "all technical cooperation and aid programs in Papua New Guinea", less than four months after PNG's government announced the closure of Taiwan's mission in Port Moresby, according to [RNZ Pacific](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/pacific/2457099/taiwan-shuts-papua-new-guinea-office-cuts-all-aid-and-halts-lng-purchases).

Health officials in Vanuatu have declared an influenza A outbreak on Tanna island: 163 flu-like illness cases reported, rising over the past month, two people severely ill and none hospitalised, per the Vanuatu Daily Post as relayed by [RNZ Pacific news in brief, 9 November](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/pacific/2471182/pacific-news-in-brief-for-9-november).

RNZ reports Pacific island countries face tougher water-security challenges as drought and other El Niño impacts worsen across the region ([RNZ Pacific](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/pacific/2481612/the-pacific-s-water-security-challenge-during-a-searing-el-nino)). World Vision is calling for child-centred climate financing ahead of COP 31 (headline only: [RNZ Pacific](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/pacific/2464300/world-vision-calls-for-child-centered-climate-financing-ahead-of-cop-31)).

In Nepal, LIFE for Relief and Development cites Nepalese authorities and the UN as reporting a death toll above 1,450, more than 5,200 missing and about 352,000 displaced after severe floods; this is an NGO release, so figures are as quoted ([ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/nepal/life-relief-and-development-nepal-urgent-needs-humanitarian-assistance-particularly-women-older-people-and-children)). ASEAN's weekly disaster update for 2–8 November was also published (headline only: [ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/indonesia/asean-weekly-disaster-update-week-45-2-8-november-2026)).

## 🌍 Tier 2 — Middle East & Africa
OCHA reports the UN and partners are supporting families hit by the first heavy rains in Gaza over the weekend, including reports of an infant who drowned and of three children and at least one woman killed when a sand dune collapsed onto tents. Thousands of families lost shelters and belongings, and the Humanitarian Coordinator urged lifting restrictions on pumps, tents and other supplies ([OCHA](https://www.unocha.org/news/todays-top-news-occupied-palestinian-territory-ukraine-democratic-republic-congo-syria)).

More than 50,000 people have been displaced by deadly clashes on the Ethiopia–Kenya border, according to Kenya Red Cross's regional head as quoted by Al Jazeera; arrivals in Moyale, Kenya report little food or shelter, and diarrhoea is spreading ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/9/more-than-50000-displaced-by-deadly-ethiopia-border-clashes)).

Yemen's government forces claim new gains in Lahj and al-Jawf while Houthi cross-border attacks resume; the claims could not be independently verified, and the Houthis are reported to have besieged Taiz for over a month ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/9/yemen-government-forces-claim-new-advances-as-houthi-border-attacks-resume)). UNHCR published a map of new displacement from the escalation on Yemen's west coast (headline only: [ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/map/yemen/map-new-displacement-due-escalation-west-coast-yemen-situation-07-november-2026)).

UNHCR and IOM warn millions in Sudan risk losing emergency shelter for lack of funding (headline only: [ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/sudan/au-soudan-des-millions-de-personnes-risquent-de-perdre-leur-abri-durgence)). In DR Congo, the Humanitarian Coordinator expressed concern over violence in Ituri's Djugu territory and its effect on civilians and the Ebola response (headline only: [ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/democratic-republic-congo/rd-congo-ituri-le-coordonnateur-humanitaire-preoccupe-par-les-violences-dans-le-territoire-de-djugu-et-leurs-consequences-sur-les-civils-et-la-riposte-lepidemie-debola-novembre-2026)).

## 🌐 Tier 3 — Rest of the World
Luke J. Lindberg assumed office as Executive Director of the World Food Programme, calling on donors to help sustain its work; WFP says 266 million people faced acute hunger last year ([WFP](https://www.wfp.org/news/luke-j-lindberg-takes-helm-executive-director-world-food-programme-and-calls-action-addressing)). At COP31, WFP urged innovative finance to stop climate shocks becoming hunger emergencies ([WFP via ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/world/cop31-world-food-programme-urges-use-innovative-finance-prevent-climate-shocks-becoming-hunger)).

OCHA's deputy relief chief briefed the Security Council on Ukraine, urging that Oleshky be built on to expand humanitarian access ([OCHA](https://www.unocha.org/news/build-oleshky-expand-humanitarian-access-ukraine-un-deputy-relief-chief-urges-security-council)).

An explosion at the HABAS steel plant in Aliaga, near Izmir, Türkiye, killed at least 16 people and injured 13 after an overhead crane fell into a furnace, according to the Izmir governor as reported by Al Jazeera; the death toll may rise ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/9/explosion-at-turkish-steel-plant-kills-at-least-16-people)).

In Haiti, OCHA-linked tracking reports on responses to displacement from armed violence in Gros-Morne, Croix-des-Bouquets, Arcahaie and Artibonite were published (headline only: [ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/haiti/haiti-ett-113-suivi-des-reponses-humanitaires-suite-aux-deplacements-lies-aux-violences-armees-croix-des-bouquets-departement-de-louest)). The New Humanitarian carries an opinion piece on Venezuela's nearly 9 million people left behind (opinion, headline only: [TNH](https://www.thenewhumanitarian.org/opinion/2026/11/09/venezuela-cannot-rebuild-while-leaving-nearly-9-million-people-behind)).

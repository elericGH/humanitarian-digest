## 🌏 Tier 1 — Pacific & Asia-Pacific
**Papua New Guinea – West New Britain:** Police confirmed 12 men died after an alleged armed robbery at a tradestore in Kimbe on Monday; the Police Commissioner said the group's vehicle rolled while fleeing and survivors opened fire on police, and one suspected ringleader is in custody. Governor Sasindran Muthuvel told RNZ Pacific the public is frustrated by repeated armed robberies by "raskol" groups, with innocent people also being hurt and killed. ([RNZ Pacific](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/pacific/2197945/people-are-being-hurt-west-new-britain-governor-speaks-out-as-12-gunmen-killed-in-kimbe-shootout))

**Northern Marianas – Typhoon In-fa:** The US National Weather Service in Guam said In-fa was a Category 3 typhoon (about 201 km/h) on Tuesday and could become a super typhoon by Thursday, passing near Pagan Island late Friday. Saipan sits just outside the forecast cone, and no watches or warnings were in effect for Guam or the CNMI at the time of the report. ([RNZ Pacific](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/pacific/2183425/typhoon-in-fa-could-become-super-typhoon-before-reaching-northern-marianas))

**Cambodia – flooding:** A ReliefWeb flood report (published 27 Oct) says flooding continued across Cambodia in October after heavy September rain; per figures cited as of 5 October, 109,985 families across 10 provinces were affected and 9,027 families evacuated. ([ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/cambodia/khm-flood-10-2026-heavy-rainfall-caused-flooding-2026-10-26))

**Myanmar–Bangladesh border:** The Arakan Army is preparing to relocate residents of some border villages to areas of Maungdaw district in Rakhine State, while fighting the Myanmar military's renewed attempts to retake territory. ([The Diplomat](https://thediplomat.com/2026/10/arakan-army-firms-up-relocation-plan-for-villages-on-bangladesh-myanmar-border/))

**Other headlines (headline only):** [Rain brings temporary relief as Southeast Asia battles fires and toxic haze](https://www.aljazeera.com/video/newsfeed/2026/10/27/indonesia-wildfires-rain-toxic-haze-southeast-asia) (Al Jazeera); [Fire engulfs Seoul's last shanty-town, forcing hundreds to evacuate](https://www.aljazeera.com/video/newsfeed/2026/10/27/fire-engulfs-seouls-last-shanty-town-forcing-hundreds-to-evacuate) (Al Jazeera); [Epidemic and emerging disease alerts in the Pacific as of 27 October 2026](https://reliefweb.int/map/world/epidemic-and-emerging-disease-alerts-pacific-27-october-2026) (ReliefWeb map).

## 🌍 Tier 2 — Middle East & Africa
**Occupied Palestinian Territory – Gaza:** OCHA says the UN and partners keep bringing limited supplies into Gaza despite restrictions and funding shortfalls, though transport was suspended today because Israel closed the Kerem Shalom crossing for election day. A shelter assessment found about 950,000 people in displacement sites at medium-to-high risk of flooding as a fourth winter approaches. ([OCHA](https://www.unocha.org/news/todays-top-news-occupied-palestinian-territory-ukraine-ethiopia-sudan-south-sudan-kenya))

**Occupied Palestinian Territory – Hebron:** The oPt Protection Cluster says Israeli forces declared parts of Hebron closed military areas on the night of 23 October and ordered residents out, with roughly 397 households (2,456 people) reportedly displaced and further displacement feared. The cluster raised concerns about collective punishment. ([ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/occupied-palestinian-territory/call-action-ongoing-mass-forced-displacement-and-collective-punishment-hebron))

**DR Congo – Ebola:** OCHA says more than 10,000 people have been affected by Ebola in the DRC and nearly half have died, with the outbreak still growing amid active conflict and Ituri Province as the epicentre. Separately, the Senior Ebola Coordinator condemned the 24 October killing of a response nurse in Bafwasende, Tshopo Province, and looting of a health centre in Kisangani. ([OCHA](https://www.unocha.org/news/responding-ebola-four-things-know); [ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/democratic-republic-congo/west-and-central-africa-latest-events-glance-20-26-october-2026))

**Nigeria – Taraba State:** The death toll from Friday's attack on Binnari village rose to at least 62, according to the state emergency agency, which said thousands were displaced. Amnesty International Nigeria said authorities' protection of rural communities is inadequate. ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/27/death-toll-from-attack-by-gunmen-in-northeastern-nigeria-rises-to-62))

**Republic of the Congo and Niger:** Flooding has affected nearly 43,000 people in northern Likouala, and a fire on 21 October affected about 100 displaced households at an IDP site in N'Guigmi, Diffa Region. ([ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/democratic-republic-congo/west-and-central-africa-latest-events-glance-20-26-october-2026))

**Yemen:** Human Rights Watch says the Houthi armed group used explosives on 18 October to severely damage the Hayjat al-Abd road, the main entry for food, medicine and fuel into besieged Taizz. ([ReliefWeb/HRW](https://reliefweb.int/report/yemen/yemen-houthi-attack-taizz-road-threatens-lives-welfare-enar)) Headline only: [Map of New Displacement due to Escalation in the West Coast – Yemen Situation, 25 Oct 2026](https://reliefweb.int/map/yemen/map-new-displacement-due-escalation-west-coast-yemen-situation-25-oct-2026) (UNHCR via ReliefWeb); [Diary of a day in Taiz: Life under blockade](https://www.thenewhumanitarian.org/opinion/first-person/2026/10/27/diary-day-taiz-life-under-blockade) (The New Humanitarian).

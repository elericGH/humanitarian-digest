## 🌏 Tier 1 — Pacific & Asia-Pacific
The Pacific Public Health Surveillance Network's epidemic alert map as of 3 November records 1,064 laboratory-confirmed dengue cases in American Samoa since the outbreak began in 2025, with only four new cases over the last three epi-weeks and the blue alert still in effect. In the Marshall Islands, the 16th outbreak report (15 August–22 October) counts 165 positive cases (127 in Majuro, 38 in Ebeye), 15 hospitalisations and no deaths, with only DENV-1 identified. [ReliefWeb map](https://reliefweb.int/map/world/epidemic-and-emerging-disease-alerts-pacific-03-november-2026)

## 🌍 Tier 2 — Middle East & Africa
OCHA's daily briefing says the closure of all roads into Ta'iz city, Yemen, is blocking aid delivery. Nearly 300,000 people have been displaced since August, and partners have reached nearly 260,000 but needs are outpacing the response. Emergency Relief Coordinator Tom Fletcher condemned the death of a UN Human Rights colleague in Houthi custody; at least 70 UN staff remain detained. [OCHA](https://www.unocha.org/news/todays-top-news-yemen-ukraine-ethiopia-occupied-palestinian-territory)

UNHCR warns that clashes on the Ethiopian side of the border have pushed thousands into Moyale, Kenya, with up to an estimated 50,000 people crossing since 22 October. Food is the most urgent need, followed by water, shelter, sanitation and health care; UNHCR is mobilising relief supplies for nearly 12,000 people. [UNHCR via ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/kenya/unhcr-warns-growing-humanitarian-needs-thousands-flee-ethiopia-kenya)

WFP warns nearly 125,000 refugees in Burundi, mostly fleeing conflict in DR Congo, risk losing food assistance from January 2027 without new funding; US and Belgian contributions have sustained assistance through the end of 2026. [WFP via ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/burundi/125000-refugees-burundi-risk-losing-food-assistance-without-urgent-funding-world-food-programme)

Al Jazeera features Zamzam camp survivors in Darfur, Sudan, vowing to document those killed in the RSF attack on the camp. [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/features/2026/11/3/darfur-survivors-vow-to-tell-story-of-those-killed-after-sudan-camp-attacks) ReliefWeb also lists new Ebola-related school situation updates for Ituri, DR Congo (headline/attachment only). [ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/democratic-republic-congo/mise-jour-ebola-en-milieu-scolaire-proved-ituri-2-novembre)

## 🌍 Tier 2 — Middle East & Africa
Uganda has ordered citizens returning from the Democratic Republic of the Congo to quarantine for 21 days (at their own cost), Health Minister Chris Baryomunsi announced on Sunday 25 Oct. The measure follows Ebola cases in Kenya and Nigeria. Uganda says it has no active infections after declaring its Bundibugyo outbreak over on 28 July, and has tightened checks at Entebbe airport and land crossings. ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/25/uganda-orders-21-day-ebola-quarantine-for-citizens-returning-from-dr-congo))

At least eight Palestinians were killed in Israeli attacks across Gaza on 25 Oct, including three security guards killed in an air strike on the Islamic University campus in Gaza City, the second attack on the institution within a few hours. ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/25/seven-killed-in-gaza-as-israel-targets-university-campus-and-other-sites))

Thousands attended funerals for eight of the nine victims of a shooting at a funeral at St George's Greek Orthodox Church in Hamoud, Karak province, Jordan; three days of national mourning were declared and the hunt for the gunmen continues. ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/25/thousands-mourn-victims-of-deadly-jordan-church-attack))

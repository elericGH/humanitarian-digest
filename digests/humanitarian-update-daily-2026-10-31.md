## 🌏 Tier 1 — Pacific & Asia-Pacific

Vanuatu and the United Nations launched a government-led Anticipatory Action Framework for Tropical Cyclones in Port Vila on 30 October. It brings together cyclone forecasts, pre-agreed actions and financing arrangements so assistance can reach communities before a severe cyclone hits ([OCHA via ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/vanuatu/vanuatu-and-united-nations-launch-anticipatory-action-framework-tropical-cyclones)).

Super Typhoon In-fa has regained strength, with maximum sustained winds of 150 miles per hour, as it approaches the Northern Islands of the Commonwealth of the Northern Mariana Islands (CNMI). RNZ describes it as the fifth major storm the Marianas has braced for ([RNZ Pacific](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/pacific/2235442/super-typhoon-in-fa-marianas-braces-for-fifth-major-storm)).

Solomon Islands Prime Minister Matthew Wale has asked the public for prayers as he recovers in Australia. He was admitted to the National Referral Hospital last weekend and doctors referred him for further treatment in Australia on Wednesday ([RNZ Pacific](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/pacific/2239750/solomon-islands-pm-wale-asks-public-for-prayers-as-he-recovers-in-australia)).

An ASEAN Special Envoy concluded a two-day humanitarian mission to Myanmar. Philippine Foreign Secretary Ma. Theresa Lazaro oversaw the distribution of humanitarian aid and met Myanmar's foreign minister ([The Diplomat](https://thediplomat.com/2026/10/asean-special-envoy-concludes-humanitarian-mission-to-myanmar/)). Separately, an Amnesty International report links Cambodian security companies to scam compounds (headline summary only) ([The Diplomat](https://thediplomat.com/2026/10/rights-groups-links-cambodian-security-companies-to-scam-compounds/)).

OCHA and the UN Resident Coordinator in the Philippines published an El Niño impact and response snapshot as of 30 October (infographic only; no further detail in the feed) ([ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/philippines/philippines-el-nino-snapshot-impact-and-response-actions-30-october-2026)). An Oceania epidemic and emerging disease alerts map dated 27 October, covering Australia, Fiji, Papua New Guinea, Samoa and others, was also published (map only) ([ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/map/world/carte-des-alertes-de-maladies-epidemiques-et-emergentes-en-oceanie-27-octobre-2026)).

## 🌍 Tier 2 — Middle East & Africa

**Yemen:** OCHA's Situation Update No. 6 (as of 25 October) reports more than 281,000 people displaced, mostly fleeing fighting in Ta'iz and along the West Coast, with a new surge from Lahj in mid-October. As of 27 October OHCHR had documented 263 civilian casualties, including 67 killed; children account for 97 of the casualties and about half of those killed ([OCHA via ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/yemen/situation-update-no-6-escalation-hostilities-and-displacement-across-yemen-update-no-6-25-october-2026-reporting-period-1-august)). UNHCR also published a report on refugees in Yemen trapped between conflict, displacement and dangerous journeys ([UNHCR via ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/yemen/refugees-yemen-trapped-between-conflict-displacement-and-dangerous-journeys)). Al Jazeera reports Houthi missiles striking Aden, with residents stockpiling food and preparing escape plans ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/30/need-a-plan-b-yemenis-in-aden-prepare-for-another-war-as-missiles-fall)).

**Gaza / Palestine:** WFP's Situation Report #99 says funding shortages forced it to cut food assistance by around one-third, from 1.6 million to 1.1 million people a month, leaving some 500,000 vulnerable people without critical assistance as winter approaches. Hot meals fell from 250,000 to 150,000 a day, and 1.4 million people (67 per cent of Gaza's population) still face acute food insecurity ([WFP via ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/occupied-palestinian-territory/wfp-palestine-emergency-response-external-situation-report-99-29-october-2026-reporting-period-1-15-october-2026)). Al Jazeera reports the UN human rights office (OHCHR) warning of ethnic cleansing fears as Israel squeezes space in Gaza, with the "Yellow Line" encroaching westward and treated as "a kill zone" ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/30/ethnic-cleansing-fears-rise-as-israel-squeezes-space-in-gaza-un)). Al Jazeera also reports Israel demolishing homes of Palestinians blamed for an attack on soldiers, with nearly 5,000 remaining displaced from Hebron neighbourhoods ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/30/israel-demolishes-homes-of-palestinians-blamed-for-attack-on-soldiers)).

**DR Congo Ebola and hunger:** An IOM update reports 9,964 confirmed Bundibugyo virus disease cases, including 4,841 deaths, as of 24 October, up 412 cases and 239 deaths since 18 October. Ituri accounts for 72 per cent of cumulative cases ([IOM via ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/democratic-republic-congo/multi-country-ebola-bundibugyo-outbreak-update-ndeg24)). New IPC analysis shows 28.8 million people in DRC acutely food insecure from September to December 2026, with 30.3 million projected by mid-2027 ([Save the Children via ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/democratic-republic-congo/drc-over-30-million-people-projected-face-hunger-conflict-and-ebola-compound-crisis-children)).

**Sudan:** Al Jazeera reports Port Sudan families struggling to afford water as shortages persist despite efforts to restore damaged infrastructure ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/30/port-sudan-families-can-barely-afford-water-as-shortages-persist)).

IGAD ministers adopted the Mombasa Ministerial Declaration on Anticipatory Action on 30 October, committing to national anticipatory action protocols, early warning systems and sustainable financing ([IGAD via ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/world/mombasa-ministerial-declaration-anticipatory-action-2026)).

## 🌐 Tier 3 — Rest of the World

**Ukraine:** UNHCR launched its annual winter appeal, saying at least 9 million people across Ukraine, Syria, Lebanon and Afghanistan need additional assistance this winter. It is aiming to raise at least $50 million ([UNHCR via ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/ukraine/unhcr-winter-appeal-9m-people-urgently-need-winter-aid-resources-dwindle-and-temperatures-drop)). Al Jazeera reports a humanitarian convoy with both Russian and Ukrainian officials entering the besieged city of Oleshky (headline only) ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/video/newsfeed/2026/10/30/russian-ukrainian-humanitarian-officials-enter-oleshky)).

**Haiti:** OCHA Flash Update No. 2 (as of 29 October) says the flood alert level was lowered from orange to green. Eight deaths were recorded, 59,420 people are affected and 13,649 houses are flooded ([OCHA via ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/haiti/heavy-rains-and-flooding-grand-sud-and-west-flash-update-no-2-29-october-2026)). A new IPC analysis projects 6.35 million Haitians facing acute food insecurity by next year (headline only) ([CARE via ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/haiti/new-ipc-analysis-635-million-haitians-are-projected-face-acute-food-insecurity-next-year)).

**Cuba:** OCHA's feature "24 hours in Havana" describes people getting through the day without reliable water, light or phone signal ([OCHA](https://www.unocha.org/news/24-hours-havana-where-people-are-getting)).

**Afghanistan:** REACH and WFP published an assessment of conflict impacts on markets and supply chains (July–August 2026) ([ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/afghanistan/impacts-conflict-markets-and-supply-chains-afghanistan-july-august-2026)).

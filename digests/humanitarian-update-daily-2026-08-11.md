# ⚡ Daily Humanitarian Update — Tuesday, 11 August 2026
*Produced Tuesday, 11 August 2026 | Australian Interest Priority Edition*

---

## 🇦🇺 Australian Priority Alerts

Australia will invest $5.2 billion (US$2.67 billion) in official development assistance in 2026–27, an increase of $112 million on the previous year, with more than three-quarters of that funding directed to the Pacific ($2.2 billion) and Southeast Asia ($1.4 billion). Assistant Minister Andrew Leigh confirmed the allocation while launching the ACFID State of the Sector Report 2026, noting that Australia "remains a reliable and responsive partner" even as aid budgets shrink elsewhere. The report also flagged mounting pressure on the sector, including a roughly 20% real decline in public donations since 2015 and a one-third fall in NGO staffing between 2017 and 2022. [Read more (PINA)](https://pina.com.fj/2026/08/10/australia-maintains-5-2b-aid-programme-as-pacific-takes-priority/)

## 🌏 Tier 1 — Pacific & Asia-Pacific

Relief operations continue in Kumamoto Prefecture, southern Japan, following the magnitude-7.1 earthquake of 28 July that killed at least 39 people. AMDA's fifth emergency relief sitrep, filed 10 August, tracks the ongoing NGO response as thousands remain displaced across the prefecture. [Read more (ReliefWeb/AMDA)](https://reliefweb.int/report/japan/amda-emergency-relief-5-kumamoto-earthquake-japan-10-august-2026)

## 🌍 Tier 2 — Middle East & Africa

OCHA warned on 10 August that intensifying fighting in Sudan is deepening humanitarian needs and driving fresh waves of displacement, with 33.7 million people now requiring assistance nationwide — the highest caseload of any country in the world — and repeated calls for stronger civilian protection. [Read more (UN News)](https://news.un.org/en/story/2026/08/1168109)

In the Democratic Republic of the Congo, UNHCR reported that the Ebola Bundibugyo outbreak has now reached displacement camps in Ituri province, infecting 19 people and killing five, with cases confirmed in at least five of the province's 69 camps — compounding a crisis that has already displaced 6.9 million people internally. [Read more (UN News)](https://news.un.org/en/story/2026/08/1168092)

## 🌐 Tier 3 — Rest of the World

A UN-run warehouse in Dnipro holding emergency health supplies bound for frontline facilities in Ukraine was struck and destroyed, OCHA reported, the fourth humanitarian warehouse hit nationwide in the space of a month. Humanitarian needs remain acute, with the six-month Flash Appeal for US$639 million less than half funded. [Read more (UN News)](https://news.un.org/en/story/2026/08/1168102)

UNHCR's Romania office issued its 208th weekly update on the Ukraine refugee situation on 10 August, continuing its regular tracking of displacement and cross-border movements linked to the war. [Read more (ReliefWeb/UNHCR)](https://reliefweb.int/report/romania/unhcr-romania-ukraine-refugee-situation-weekly-update-208-10-august-2026)

## 🚨 Sudden Onset Hazards

Recovery from Japan's magnitude-7.1 Kumamoto earthquake continues amid ongoing aftershocks (more than 100 recorded since the 28 July mainshock). As of 10 August, roughly 6,200 people remained in evacuation centres, with over 21,000 homes damaged and 1,744 destroyed or severely damaged. US Forces Japan airlifted around 33,000 bottles of drinking water to affected communities over the weekend, and authorities are preparing a secondary evacuation programme moving residents from shelters into hotels while repairs continue.

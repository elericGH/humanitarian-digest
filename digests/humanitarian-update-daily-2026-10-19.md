## 🌏 Tier 1 — Pacific & Asia-Pacific
**Tropical Cyclone Wati (Cook Islands).** The Category 2 cyclone was forecast to pass over Aitutaki on Sunday local time, tracking over Mauke and Mangaia. The Cook Islands Meteorological Service said storm-force winds of 54–76 knots (100–141 km/h) were likely, with heavy rain, damaging swells and a risk of coastal inundation. Winds of 78–113 km/h were blowing on Aitutaki as of 8am Sunday local time. Aitutaki's mayor reported a few homes without power and a coconut tree down on a roof, with one outage restored. The island council had activated its emergency operations centre. Sources: [RNZ Pacific – Warnings in place as Wati bears down](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/pacific/2014316/warnings-in-place-as-wati-bears-down-on-southern-cook-islands); [RNZ Pacific – Aitutaki braces for Tropical Cyclone Wati](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/pacific/1994834/aitutaki-braces-for-tropical-cyclone-wati).

**Typhoon Champi — Guam and Northern Mariana Islands.** The US President approved emergency declarations for Guam and the CNMI after Super Typhoon Champi narrowly missed Guam. Champi passed about 10 miles south of Guam early Sunday, with warnings of damaging winds and life-threatening conditions. The declaration authorises FEMA to coordinate assistance; the CNMI declaration covers Saipan, Tinian and Rota, with the federal government covering 75 percent of eligible emergency protective measures. The CNMI governor said the help comes after successive storms this year. Sources: [RNZ Pacific – Trump approves emergency declarations for Guam, CNMI](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/pacific/2011143/trump-approves-emergency-declarations-for-guam-cnmi-as-champi-moves-away); [RNZ Pacific – Category 4 Typhoon Champi narrowly misses Guam](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/pacific/1987119/category-4-typhoon-champi-narrowly-misses-guam).

**Myanmar (headline only).** Al Jazeera reports on US talks with Myanmar's military ruler: [Why the US is holding secret talks with Myanmar's military ruler](https://www.aljazeera.com/video/newsfeed/2026/10/18/why-the-us-is-holding-secret-talks-with-myanmars-military-ruler).

## 🌍 Tier 2 — Middle East & Africa
**Yemen / Saudi Arabia.** The Gulf Cooperation Council condemned repeated Houthi attacks on Saudi Arabia after a foreign ministers' meeting in Manama. The Saudi-led coalition reported shooting down two drones near Riyadh and in the Eastern Province early Sunday. Al Jazeera's summary says fighting is displacing more than 233,000 people across Yemen. Ministers urged tighter enforcement of the UN arms embargo and welcomed a 15 October UN Security Council statement condemning Houthi strikes and threats to Red Sea shipping. Source: [Al Jazeera – GCC condemns Houthi attacks on Saudi Arabia as Yemen fighting intensifies](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/18/gcc-condemns-houthi-attacks-on-saudi-arabia-as-yemen-fighting-intensifies).

**Occupied West Bank.** The EU condemned an attack by masked Israeli settlers on a convoy of European diplomats near Taybeh, east of Ramallah, and called for "full accountability" and measures to prevent further violence against Palestinians and journalists. The attack happened during the olive harvest, when attacks on Palestinian farmers are said to be escalating. Diplomats from France, Belgium and Poland were in the delegation; a Belgian vehicle window was smashed and the French mayor of Aulnoye-Aymeries said he was kicked and beaten. Sources: [Al Jazeera – EU condemns Israeli settler attack on diplomats](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/18/eu-condemns-israeli-settler-attack-on-diplomats-in-occupied-west-bank); [Al Jazeera – A timeline of West Bank attacks](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/18/a-timeline-of-west-bank-attacks-on-palestinians-and-foreign-officials) (headline only).

**Ethiopia – Tigray.** Al Jazeera video reporting says renewed fighting in Tigray has raised fears of a wider regional conflict; residents of Adigrat, near the Eritrean border, recall recent drone strikes by Ethiopian forces against Eritrean troops. Source: [Al Jazeera – Residents recount drone strikes in Ethiopia's northern Tigray region](https://www.aljazeera.com/video/newsfeed/2026/10/18/residents-recount-drone-strikes-in-ethiopias-northern-tigray-region).

**Syria (headline only).** ReliefWeb lists the Health Cluster's biweekly update for 1–15 October 2026: [Health Sector Syria update](https://reliefweb.int/report/syrian-arab-republic/health-sector-syria-update-biweekly-national-and-sub-national-health-sector-coordination-1-15-october-2026).

## 🌐 Tier 3 — Rest of the World
**Albania.** Al Jazeera reports a sixth day of student-led anti-government protests in Tirana, with police using rubber bullets, water cannon and tear gas, and offices at the Supreme Court set ablaze; the prime minister has refused to resign. Sources: [Tirana sees sixth day of violent protests](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/18/chaos-in-tirana-demonstrators-determined-to-not-give-up-so-easily); [Fire engulfs Albania's Supreme Court](https://www.aljazeera.com/video/newsfeed/2026/10/18/albania-protests-supreme-court-fire).

**France.** President Macron denounced nationwide rioting and looting, promising more security but stopping short of a state of emergency or curfew. Al Jazeera ties the protests to anger over school conditions and living costs. Sources: [Macron denounces France's nationwide rioting](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/18/macron-denounces-frances-nationwide-rioting-looting); [Why have France's school protests become a wider political crisis?](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/18/why-have-frances-school-protests-become-a-wider-political-crisis).

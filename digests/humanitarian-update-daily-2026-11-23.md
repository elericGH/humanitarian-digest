# ⚡ Daily Humanitarian Update — Monday, 23 November 2026
*Produced Monday, 23 November 2026 | Australian Interest Priority Edition*

## 🇦🇺 Australian Priority Alerts

**COP31 climate talks collapse; Australia holds the negotiations presidency.** UN climate talks ended on Sunday without a deal after the EU and others rejected a final draft put forward by COP31 presidents Turkey and Australia as too weak. Australia's COP31 President of Negotiations, Chris Bowen, called the outcome "very disappointing" and said disagreements over fuels and climate finance were the dealbreakers. It was the longest COP on record and the first failure to adopt a deal by all parties since Copenhagen in 2009 ([RNZ](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/world/2737626/cop31-climate-talks-collapse-as-eu-and-others-reject-deal); also [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/22/countries-react-after-cop31-climate-talks-end-in-collapse)).

## 🌏 Tier 1 — Pacific & Asia-Pacific

**Marshall Islands: derelict ore carrier at risk of sinking in Majuro lagoon.** A US Coast Guard structural assessment of the MV Futun Creek reportedly found it not fit for ocean travel and possibly not fit to stay afloat much longer. The vessel, carrying iron ore with a crew of about 30, has been stranded in Majuro since January. More than 10 other derelict vessels already threaten the lagoon ([RNZ Pacific](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/pacific/2739583/derelict-cargo-ship-laden-with-iron-ore-at-risk-of-sinking-in-majuro-lagoon)).

**Bangladesh: unrest after rejection of Hasina's return.** Paramilitary troops have been deployed after a week of violence following protests by supporters of ousted PM Sheikh Hasina. At least four people have been killed, and police stations, banks and the ancestral home of Muhammad Yunus were attacked ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/22/why-bangladesh-descended-into-violence-ahead-of-hasinas-planned-return)).

**Rohingya in Cox's Bazar (documentary).** Al Jazeera World reports Rohingya families facing hunger, loss and an uncertain future (headline/summary only) ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/video/al-jazeera-world/2026/11/22/coxs-bazar-rohingya-life-in-exile)).

## 🌍 Tier 2 — Middle East & Africa

**Sudan: RSF drone strikes on Khartoum area kill four, damage UN building.** Drones blamed on the RSF struck residential areas early Sunday; three were killed in northern Omdurman and a child south of the capital. The UN Resident and Humanitarian Coordinator Denise Brown said a UN building was damaged while staff were inside. Air defences reportedly responded to drones targeting government buildings and Khartoum airport ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/22/rsf-drones-kill-four-in-khartoum); [UN statement via ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/sudan/statement-united-nations-resident-and-humanitarian-coordinator-sudan-denise-brown-drone-strikes-khartoum)).

**West Bank: settler attacks.** Al Jazeera reports Israeli settlers attacked several Palestinian towns and villages on Sunday (headline/summary only) ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/video/newsfeed/2026/11/22/israeli-settlers-attack-multiple-palestinian-villages-as-pogroms-surge)).

**Nigeria: boat capsizes.** At least 24 people were killed when a boat carrying mourners to a funeral capsized (summary only) ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/video/newsfeed/2026/11/22/dozens-drown-as-boat-capsizes-in-nigeria-en-route-to-funeral)).

**Zanzibar: hotel fire.** A fire tore through six hotels and dozens of shops in Nungwi (summary only) ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/video/newsfeed/2026/11/22/fire-breaks-out-at-popular-tourist-hotspot-in-zanzibar)).

## 🌐 Tier 3 — Rest of the World

**Ukraine: attacks and health-system pressure.** Three Russian attacks killed at least six people near Kyiv and shelling killed one in Zaporizhzhia, per Al Jazeera ([link](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/22/zelenskyy-urges-us-to-release-patriot-missiles)). The Ukraine Health Cluster published documents on relocating health facility equipment from frontline areas ([ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/ukraine/ukraine-health-cluster-safeguarding-health-facility-equipment-and-supplies-through-relocation-frontline-areas-ukraine-november-2026)) and partner response to attacks on health care as of 31 October ([ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/ukraine/ukraine-health-cluster-partner-emergency-response-attacks-31-october-2026)) (headlines only).

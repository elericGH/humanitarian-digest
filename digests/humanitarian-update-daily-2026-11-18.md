## 🇦🇺 Australian Priority Alerts

**Australia joins call for unimpeded Gaza aid as floods worsen.** Plan International (17 Nov) welcomed the renewed calls by the Foreign Ministers of Australia, Canada, Italy and the United Kingdom for immediate, rapid and unimpeded humanitarian access throughout Gaza, and urged that restrictions on essential supplies, including tents and shelter materials, be lifted. It cites a UNICEF estimate that 950,000 displaced people live in flood-exposed areas, including 400,000 children. ## 🌏 Tier 1 — Pacific & Asia-Pacific

**Severe Tropical Cyclone Xavier (Category 5) batters the Northern Cook Islands.** RNZ (18 Nov, 7:24am NZDT) reports maximum sustained winds of about 220 km/h near the centre with gusts up to 315 km/h, moving west-southwest at about 7 km/h per the Fiji Meteorological Service. Pukapuka and Nassau are under a gale warning, Manihiki had a strong wind warning, and Emergency Management Cook Islands urged residents to remain in safe shelter and away from coastal areas. On Manihiki, wharves are damaged and power supply is a major concern. See [RNZ – Category 5 Cyclone Xavier](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/pacific/2648447/category-5-cyclone-xavier-batters-northern-cook-islands-with-gusts-up-to-315km-h). Earlier RNZ reports tracked it intensifying: [Category 4 over Northern Cook Islands](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/pacific/2644431/cyclone-xavier-intensifies-to-category-4-over-northern-cook-islands) and [Manihiki residents 'don't wait for warnings'](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/pacific/2637624/manihiki-residents-don-t-wait-for-warnings-as-xavier-intensifies-into-category-3-system) (headline only).

**Pacific epidemic alerts update.** The Pacific Community's map as of 17 Nov lists Marshall Islands dengue: the 20th situation report (15 Aug–12 Nov) records 173 positive cases (Majuro 128, Ebeye 45) and 18 hospitalisations, all children, with no deaths. All new cases since 29 October came from Ebeye, and only DENV-1 has been identified. The map also covers Australia, PNG, Samoa, Tonga, Vanuatu, New Caledonia and others. [ReliefWeb – Epidemic and emerging disease alerts in the Pacific, 17 November 2026](https://reliefweb.int/map/world/epidemic-and-emerging-disease-alerts-pacific-17-november-2026)

**Timor-Leste school feeding.** Timor-Leste, the Republic of Korea and WFP launched a US$7.2 million home-grown school feeding initiative (headline only). [ReliefWeb – WFP](https://reliefweb.int/report/timor-leste/timor-leste-republic-korea-and-wfp-launch-us72-million-home-grown-school-feeding-initiative)

**Rohingya case at the ICJ.** The Diplomat analysis says each possible outcome of the case has different legal consequences and implications for survivors, but none would erase what was inflicted on the Rohingya. [The Diplomat](https://thediplomat.com/2026/11/the-rohingya-genocide-case-at-the-icj-international-justice-at-a-crossroad/)

## 🌍 Tier 2 — Middle East & Africa

**Somalia: fighting displaces over 18,000 around Baidoa as El Niño looms.** The ICRC (17 Nov) cites UNHCR's Q3 2026 report: recent fighting in and around Baidoa has displaced more than 18,000 people, and the Bay region hosts more than half a million displaced people. Nearly 26,000 displaced people in Baidoa could face severe food insecurity by late December per IPC experts. Somali Red Crescent teams transported over 500 wounded to Bay Regional Hospital, and 10,500 sandbags and 14 flood-proofed water points were provided ahead of expected floods. [ICRC](https://www.icrc.org/en/news-release/somalia-baidoa-communities-bear-cost-renewed-fighting-el-nino-looms)

**Gaza: flooding and winter shelter shortages.** See the Plan International statement above ([ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/occupied-palestinian-territory/humanitarian-access-urgently-needed-gaza-floods-worsen)). The New Humanitarian's feature "Gaza aid in limbo: One year after the Board of Peace" says the Trump-led entity was supposed to facilitate humanitarian access, but there isn't enough engine oil for ambulances. [The New Humanitarian](https://www.thenewhumanitarian.org/feature/2026/11/17/gaza-aid-limbo-one-year-after-board-peace) Al Jazeera reports the Palestinian Health Ministry says a 72-year-old woman, Fadda Mohammed Fheidat, was shot dead by Israeli settlers, with 10 others wounded per the headline. [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/17/israeli-settler-pogrom-kills-palestinian-woman-wounds-10-people?traffic_source=rss)

**South Sudan: Ebola readiness gaps.** The UN Humanitarian Coordinator called for stronger preparedness for a potential Ebola outbreak after an October simulation exercise. The exercise showed the system can quickly detect simulated cases, but significant gaps remain in alert management, rapid response teams, health worker remuneration, ambulances, laboratories, PPE supplies and contact tracing. [ReliefWeb – OCHA](https://reliefweb.int/report/south-sudan/humanitarian-coordinator-calls-strengthened-ebola-readiness-south-sudan)

**Yemen.** Al Jazeera reports Yemen's parliament approved a law designating the Houthis as "terrorists"; the law targets the group's financing and activities as the Houthis make advances in southwestern Yemen. [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/17/yemeni-parliament-approves-law-designating-houthis-as-terrorists?traffic_source=rss) OCHA's daily top news covers Yemen, the occupied Palestinian territory, DR Congo, Somalia, Ukraine, Afghanistan and Pakistan floods/El Niño (headline only). [OCHA](https://www.unocha.org/news/todays-top-news-yemen-occupied-palestinian-territory-democratic-republic-congo-somalia-ukraine)

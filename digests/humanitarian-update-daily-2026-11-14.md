# ⚡ Daily Humanitarian Update — Saturday, 14 November 2026
*Produced Saturday, 14 November 2026 | Australian Interest Priority Edition*

## 🇦🇺 Australian Priority Alerts

**ACFID and PIANGO report on reimagining NGO partnerships for climate finance.** ACFID published (13 Nov) a report of the Pre-COP31 stakeholder session held in Nadi, Fiji on 6 October 2026, organised by ACFID and the Pacific Islands Association of Non-governmental Organisations (PIANGO). The Talanoa-style session, attended by more than 50 participants from the Pacific and Australia, examined how Australian and Pacific NGOs can partner on climate finance, underpinned by Pacific and Indigenous knowledge and the locally-led development agenda. COP31 runs 9–20 November 2026 in Antalya, Türkiye. [ACFID resource](https://acfid.asn.au/acfid_resource/reimagining-ngo-partnerships-for-climate-finance/)

## 🌏 Tier 1 — Pacific & Asia-Pacific

**Australia–Philippines defence ties deepen.** Indo-Pacific Defense Forum reports eight Australia-supported defence infrastructure projects are underway across Philippine military bases while the two countries finalise a defence cooperation arrangement. [IPDF](https://ipdefenseforum.com/2026/11/australia-philippines-connect-defense-infrastructure-training-operations/)

**Emergency fuel stockpiles in Southeast Asia.** Indonesia, Thailand and the Philippines will establish joint emergency fuel stockpiles under a Japan-backed ticket-system scheme similar to the EU's. [The Diplomat](https://thediplomat.com/2026/11/indonesia-thailand-philippines-to-establish-joint-emergency-fuel-stockpiles/)

**Bus–truck crash in Assam, India.** At least 24 people were killed and 11 injured when a container truck collided with a bus in Golaghat district, near Kaziranga National Park, police said. [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/13/truck-and-bus-crash-in-indias-assam-state-kills-at-least-24-people)

**WFP country briefs and El Niño.** New WFP country briefs for Nepal and Bhutan were published (headline only): [Nepal](https://reliefweb.int/report/nepal/wfp-nepal-country-brief-november-2026), [Bhutan](https://reliefweb.int/report/bhutan/wfp-bhutan-country-brief-november-2026). FAO published "Asia and the Pacific: Snapshot of El Niño & Middle East Crisis (as of 12 November 2026)" (headline only): [ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/world/asia-and-pacific-snapshot-el-nino-middle-east-crisis-12-november-2026).

## 🌍 Tier 2 — Middle East & Africa

**Gaza: deadly rains expose shelter crisis.** The Humanitarian Country Team says rainstorms have killed children and adults, suspended critical services and flooded or destroyed thousands of shelters. It says the devastation was preventable: more than 150,000 tents remain outside Gaza, and restrictions and funding shortfalls have hampered the winter plan. [OCHA](https://www.unocha.org/news/todays-top-news-occupied-palestinian-territory-ukraine-democratic-republic-congo-haiti-0) Al Jazeera separately reports a baby died from cold in Gaza as 53 countries urged Israel to allow aid (headline/summary only): [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/video/newsfeed/2026/11/13/jerusalem-daily-baby-dies-from-cold-in-gaza?traffic_source=rss).

**Sudan: drone strikes among deadliest for civilians.** The Sudan INGO Forum issued its sixth alarm on drones: strikes killed at least 300 civilians in the first three weeks of October, including a 26 October market attack in Nyala, South Darfur that killed and injured more than 150. Strikes hit infrastructure in at least 12 states. [ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/sudan/sudan-ingos-issue-6th-alarm-drone-strikes-october-marks-one-deadliest-months-civilians)

**DR Congo: record hunger and Ebola.** WFP, FAO, UNICEF and WHO report a projected record 30.3 million people in IPC Phase 3+ between January and June 2027, up from 26.5 million a year earlier, with 4.2 million in Emergency. [ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/democratic-republic-congo/conflict-and-disease-outbreaks-deepen-dr-congos-hunger-crisis-record-numbers-are-projected-face-acute-food-insecurity) The UN Senior Ebola Coordinator said progress has been made but significant transmission continues, including into new areas, and that ADF violence has recently killed more people than Ebola. [OCHA](https://www.unocha.org/news/todays-top-news-occupied-palestinian-territory-ukraine-democratic-republic-congo-haiti-0)

**Yemen: displacement tops 340,000.** IOM says more than 340,000 people have fled, mostly from Taiz (224,819), Lahj and Al Hodeidah, while restrictions on key transport routes threaten aid delivery. [ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/yemen/displacement-tops-340000-yemen-restrictions-hamper-humanitarian-response)

**Nigeria ends Ebola contact monitoring.** Nigeria's NCDC says none of 62 contacts of its 20 October case developed Ebola; 13 reported symptoms but tested negative. [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/13/nigeria-ends-21-day-ebola-contact-monitoring-with-no-secondary-transmission)

**Other.** At least 10 killed in Guinea clashes over a gold-mining ban (headline only): [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/13/at-least-10-killed-in-guinea-in-crackdown-on-gold-mining?traffic_source=rss). OCHA published footage of Emergency Relief Coordinator Fletcher's Mali visit, 12 November (headline only): [OCHA](https://www.unocha.org/media-centre/mali-fletcher-visit-12-november-2026). UNHCR published a CAR Sudan Situation update as of 10 November (headline only): [ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/central-african-republic/central-african-republic-sudan-situation-november-10-2026).

## 🌐 Tier 3 — Rest of the World

**Haiti: violence escalates in Port-au-Prince.** OCHA warns of heavy weapons, attack helicopters and armed drones in dense neighbourhoods; violence in Croix-des-Bouquets has displaced about 2,000 people since mid-October, per IOM. [OCHA](https://www.unocha.org/news/todays-top-news-occupied-palestinian-territory-ukraine-democratic-republic-congo-haiti-0)

**Ukraine: attacks kill 10 civilians.** OCHA cites local authorities: 10 civilians killed and 60 injured, a Kyiv hospital damaged and power disrupted in 13 regions. [OCHA](https://www.unocha.org/news/todays-top-news-occupied-palestinian-territory-ukraine-democratic-republic-congo-haiti-0)

**Malawi: WFP forced to prioritise refugees.** From November WFP will give monthly cash (USD 8 per person) to 35,000 of the most vulnerable of 65,000+ refugees in Dzaleka camp, covering 50% of food needs. [ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/malawi/funding-shortages-force-wfp-prioritise-food-assistance-refugees-malawi)

**ICRC on autonomous weapons.** ICRC President Mirjana Spoljaric urged states, ahead of next week's CCW review conference in Geneva, to ensure the future of warfare is not handed to machines. [ICRC](https://www.icrc.org/en/statement/icrc-president-states-must-act-ensure-future-warfare-not-handed-over-machines)

**Other.** More than 7,400 migrants reached Spain's Canary Islands in the first 10 days of November (headline summary): [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/video/newsfeed/2026/11/13/migrants-risk-deadly-route-to-spains-canary-islands?traffic_source=rss). The Cheat Sheet weekly roundup covers Gaza's winter, a new DR Congo rebel offensive and killer robots (headline only): [The New Humanitarian](https://www.thenewhumanitarian.org/news/2026/11/13/gaza-deadly-winter-new-rebel-offensive-dr-congo-looming-killer-robot-cheat-sheet).

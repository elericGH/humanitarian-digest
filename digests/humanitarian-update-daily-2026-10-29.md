## 🌍 Tier 2 — Middle East & Africa

About 50,000 people have crossed into Moyale town in Kenya because of renewed intercommunal fighting in neighbouring Ethiopia, as of 28 October, according to an OCHA flash update. Needs are rising quickly, with food, WASH, health and protection the most urgent. Kenya Red Cross and authorities are scaling up, and a rapid joint multisectoral assessment is under way ([OCHA via ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/kenya/kenya-humanitarian-situation-displaced-people-moyale-marsabit-cou)).

Save the Children reports that 57% of children assessed in Central Jebel Marra, Sudan, are chronically malnourished (stunted) and 15% (nearly one in six) were acutely malnourished. It links this to conflict and the El Niño weather event ([Save the Children via ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/sudan/sudan-more-half-children-assessed-jebel-marra-stunted-drought-wor)). Al Jazeera also ran "Sudan war and inflation leave families struggling to survive in Khartoum" (headline only; [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/features/2026/10/28/sudan-war-and-inflation-leave-families-struggling-to-s)).

OCHA's daily top-news briefing covers Yemen, the occupied Palestinian territory, Ethiopia/Kenya, Sudan, DR Congo and Haiti (headline only; [OCHA](https://www.unocha.org/news/todays-top-news-yemen-occupied-palestinian-territory-ethiopiakenya-sudan)). The Red Cross published a Yemen population-movement Emergency Appeal operational strategy, MDRYE018 (headline only; [ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/yemen/yemen-mena-population-movement-emergency-appeal-operational-strat)). UNHCR published its Lebanon operational update for September 2026 (headline only; [ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/lebanon/unhcr-lebanon-operational-update-september-2026)).

## 🌐 Tier 3 — Rest of the World

An OCHA flash update on heavy rains and flooding in Haiti's Grand Sud (as of 28 October) reports 4 deaths (3 in Nippes, 1 in South-East) and 2 fishermen missing in Abricots. In Nippes, 1,920 families are affected and 1,767 houses are flooded, with 48 small houses destroyed. In Grand'Anse, 630 houses are flooded and 3 critical facilities are flooded, including the Corail community hospital. In Les Cayes, 200 houses are flooded after sections of the Ravine du Sud dykes collapsed ([OCHA via ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/haiti/haiti-heavy-rains-and-flooding-grand-sud-flash-update-no-1-28-oct)).

A report titled "Global food insecurity: why action cannot wait" was published on ReliefWeb (headline only; [ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/world/global-food-insecurity-why-action-cannot-wait)).

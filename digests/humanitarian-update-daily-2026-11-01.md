## 🌏 Tier 1 — Pacific & Asia-Pacific
Japan and Sweden are expanding defence and technology cooperation, with both governments increasingly viewing Euro-Atlantic and Indo-Pacific security as inseparable. The Japanese and Swedish defence ministers discussed regional security and bilateral cooperation at a July 2026 meeting at the NATO Summit in Ankara, according to [Indo-Pacific Defense Forum](https://ipdefenseforum.com/2026/10/japan-sweden-deepen-defense-ties-as-security-challenges-connect-europe-indo-pacific/).

## 🌍 Tier 2 — Middle East & Africa
**Gaza:** At least four Palestinians were killed on Saturday as Israeli attacks continued, including shelling of the Nuseirat refugee camp. Two girls also died from injuries sustained when their tent caught fire at a displacement camp in al-Mawasi, Khan Younis; the cause is unclear. The Gaza Health Ministry says 1,580 people have been killed since the October 2025 ceasefire, per [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/31/at-least-four-palestinians-killed-in-gaza-as-israeli-attacks-continue).

**South Sudan:** The OCHA humanitarian update (as of 30 October) reports more than 5,100 refugees and returnees from the DR Congo needing urgent support, insecurity and displacement in Jonglei and Upper Nile, and drone strikes reportedly killing dozens in Abyei. It also notes a national simulation exercise identified gaps in Ebola preparedness, worsening food insecurity, and access impeded by insecurity and bureaucratic constraints ([ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/south-sudan/south-sudan-humanitarian-update-30-october-2026)).

**DR Congo:** The Protection Cluster's September 2026 highlights describe a protection situation that remained of concern, marked by armed conflict, forced displacement, rights violations and access constraints. North Kivu, South Kivu and Ituri remained most affected, and the Ebola outbreak continued to limit access to essential services and humanitarian interventions in some areas ([ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/democratic-republic-congo/cluster-protection-rdc-points-saillants-de-la-situation-de-protection-en-republique-democratique-du-congo-du-mois-de-septembre-2026)).

## 🌐 Tier 3 — Rest of the World
**Pakistan:** WFP's weekly market monitor (week ending 29 October) says staple food prices remain 23 percent above pre-Middle East conflict levels, with wheat flour 30 percent above. The basic food basket for a six-person household costs PKR 18,669 per month, and petrol and diesel are 46 and 45 percent above pre-conflict levels ([ReliefWeb/WFP](https://reliefweb.int/report/pakistan/pakistan-weekly-market-monitor-week-ending-29-october-2026)).

**Ukraine:** Residents who spent years trapped in Russian-occupied Oleshky have reached Ukrainian-controlled territory, evacuated during a US-backed local ceasefire (headline and short summary only; [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/video/newsfeed/2026/10/31/aje-onl-nf_evacuees-from-oleshky-reunite-w-families-in-ukraine-311026)).

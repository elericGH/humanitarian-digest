## 🇦🇺 Australian Priority Alerts

**ACFID on COP31 and the Pacific.** ACFID members have called for stronger climate action and increased, accessible climate finance for vulnerable communities and Pacific Island countries as COP31 gets underway this month. Australia, as President of Negotiations at COP31, is being urged to build international momentum for climate finance and accelerated action amid rising geopolitical tensions and significant reductions in global ODA. ([ACFID, 5 Nov 2026](https://acfid.asn.au/from-promises-to-progress-what-cop31-must-deliver-for-the-pacific/))

**ACFID on El Niño preparedness (headline only).** "Scaling up Australia's support for Asia-Pacific Preparedness and Response to El Niño" was published on 5 Nov. ([ACFID](https://acfid.asn.au/scaling-up-australias-support-for-asia-pacific-preparedness-and-response-to-el-nino/))

## 🌏 Tier 1 — Pacific & Asia-Pacific

**Fiji drought and El Niño.** Fiji's Prime Minister Sitiveni Rabuka downplayed the impact of El Niño-driven drought ("Are people dying? No. We can manage"). The Fiji Met Service says El Niño is likely to reach very strong intensity and persist through the rest of 2026 and early 2027, with below-normal rainfall forecast. The Meteorological Services Minister told parliament that 19 of 44 Water Authority of Fiji sources were at critical levels as of 1 November, and that the government is "working towards declaring a national emergency", which would first need Cabinet approval. ([RNZ Pacific](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/pacific/2355926/fiji-pm-rabuka-downplays-el-nino-impact-are-people-dying-no-we-can-manage))

## 🌍 Tier 2 — Middle East & Africa

**Yemen: Ta'iz.** OCHA reports nearly 310,000 people displaced since the start of August, with about 135,000 still displaced within Ta'iz governorate. The ICRC delivered four trucks of medical supplies, including surgical kits, to Ta'iz hospitals, and WFP launched an emergency meals initiative aiming to reach 2,000 families initially. A missile strike near Qa'tabah reportedly injured civilians and led to suspended deliveries. ([OCHA](https://www.unocha.org/news/todays-top-news-yemen-sudan-ukraine-occupied-palestinian-territory); [ICRC via ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/yemen/yemen-icrc-delivers-shipment-vital-medical-supplies-health-facilities-taiz-city-enar))

**Sudan.** The Humanitarian Coordinator warned that drone strikes continue to hit power stations and fuel facilities. OCHA cites local sources reporting strikes on Ed Damazine (Blue Nile) that caused power outages and killed two civilians, and air strikes in Um Bader (North Kordofan) that killed two women and damaged water stations. The 2026 appeal of nearly $2.9 billion is funded at $1.34 billion. ([OCHA](https://www.unocha.org/news/todays-top-news-yemen-sudan-ukraine-occupied-palestinian-territory))

**Kenya/Ethiopia: Moyale.** Intercommunal fighting that began on 18 October has displaced people inside Ethiopia. An estimated 50,000 people had crossed into Moyale, Marsabit County, Kenya by 3 November. The UN in Kenya appealed for US$8.9 million for the next three months. ([OCHA flash update via ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/kenya/kenya-humanitarian-situation-displaced-people-moyale-marsabit-county-flash-update-no-2-5-november))

**South Sudan border clinic.** IOM warns 100,000 people risk losing healthcare in Wunthou if funding shortages close the only clinic at the Sudan–South Sudan crossing, possibly by December. ([IOM via ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/south-sudan/100000-risk-losing-healthcare-key-south-sudan-border-clinic-nears-closure-warns-iom))

## 🌐 Tier 3 — Rest of the World

**Ukraine.** OCHA reports more than 100 drone-related attacks on humanitarian operations this year across seven front-line regions, including a strike on an evacuation vehicle in Kramatorsk. WHO has verified 597 attacks on health care this year, up 15 per cent. ([OCHA](https://www.unocha.org/news/todays-top-news-yemen-sudan-ukraine-occupied-palestinian-territory))

**West Bank.** OCHA reports dozens of Palestinians were displaced in Isteih (Jericho) after settlers took over homes, and a "closed military zone" blocked partners from reaching them. ([OCHA](https://www.unocha.org/news/todays-top-news-yemen-sudan-ukraine-occupied-palestinian-territory))

**Panama (headline only).** Earthquake in Azuero & Veraguas, Situation Report No. 08 as of 4 November. ([ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/panama/panama-earthquake-azuero-veraguas-situation-report-no-08-4-november-2026-1230))

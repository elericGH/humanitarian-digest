## 🌏 Tier 1 — Pacific & Asia-Pacific
The Cook Islands Prime Minister Mark Brown has declared a state of emergency for Nassau Island ahead of Tropical Cyclone Xavier. The Fiji Meteorological Service's latest track shows the storm passing south of the island this weekend as a category 3 or 2 storm. Nassau is remote, more than 1100km north-west of Rarotonga, with fewer than 100 residents. New Zealand's Rapid Response Team has sent two personnel to assist ([RNZ Pacific](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/pacific/2705528/cook-islands-declares-state-of-emergency-for-nassau-as-cyclone-xavier-approaches)).

The International Court of Justice has delivered a verdict finding Myanmar guilty of genocide against the Rohingya ([The Diplomat](https://thediplomat.com/2026/11/icj-delivers-historic-verdict-myanmar-guilty-of-genocide-against-the-rohingya/)). The New Humanitarian carries an interview in which state crime expert Penny Green says the ruling is largely symbolic without political action to back it ([TNH](https://www.thenewhumanitarian.org/interview/2026/11/20/why-icj-ruling-wont-stop-rohingya-genocide)). The Diplomat also asks what comes next ([The Diplomat](https://thediplomat.com/2026/11/the-icjs-myanmar-genocide-ruling-what-comes-next/)).

Pacific leaders gave a stark warning over fossil fuels at COP31 (headline only) ([RNZ Pacific](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/pacific/2709824/stark-warning-over-fossil-fuels-from-pacific-at-cop31)).

A DG ECHO daily map covers Tropical Storm Akvan affecting India and Bangladesh, dated 20/11/2026 (map only, no text detail) ([ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/map/bangladesh/india-bangladesh-tropical-storm-akvan-dg-echo-daily-map-20112026)). A joint IOM/UNHCR report covers border monitoring of inflows into Pakistan from Iran, 1 Mar – 19 Nov 2026 (headline only) ([ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/pakistan/pakistan-joint-iom-and-unhcr-border-monitoring-report-inflows-islamic-re)). Australian, New Zealand and US navies are boosting mine warfare capabilities in Exercise Dugong (headline only) ([IPDF](https://ipdefenseforum.com/2026/11/australia-new-zealand-u-s-navies-boost-mine-warfare-capabilities-in-exercis)).

## 🌍 Tier 2 — Middle East & Africa
OCHA's daily briefing for 20 Nov covers Ukraine, Yemen, the Occupied Palestinian Territory and the Democratic Republic of the Congo (headline only; the feed body showed only the Ukraine section heading) ([OCHA](https://www.unocha.org/news/todays-top-news-ukraine-yemen-occupied-palestinian-territory-democratic-republic-congo)).

Al Jazeera reports Israeli drone attacks killed seven Palestinians in central Gaza, with drones causing "fear and panic" as attacks continue despite the "ceasefire" ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/20/israeli-drone-attacks-kill-seven-palestinians-in-central-gaza)). A separate Al Jazeera item says almost one in three reported violations against children worldwide in 2025 occurred in Palestine ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/video/newsfeed/2026/11/20/one-third-of-attacks-on-children-worldwide-recorded-in-pal)). Al Jazeera also reports Sudan's war has closed schools and rehabilitation centres, leaving children with disabilities without education ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/20/sudans-war-leaves-disabled-children-missing-out-on-education)).

Africa CDC published a six-month situation and response report on the Bundibugyo Ebola outbreak (declared 15 May 2026). It finds substantial response capacity was mobilised, but persistent transmission and mortality indicate that capacity did not consistently translate into early detection, timely access to care and reliable local services in DRC ([ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/democratic-republic-congo/bundibugyo-ebola-outbreak-africa-six-month-situation-an)). OCHA's DRC Ebola situation overview for week 46 (to 15 Nov) was also published (infographic only) ([ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/democratic-republic-congo/republique-democratique-du-congo-epidemie-de-maladie-vi)).

UNHCR's Sudan population movements update for 16 Nov 2026 was published (headline only) ([ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/sudan/sudan-population-movements-sudan-16-november-2026)). The ICRC says explosive remnants of war continue to kill and maim civilians long after fighting ends, with children a large proportion of victims in 2025 per the Landmine and Cluster Munitions Monitor ([ReliefWeb/ICRC](https://reliefweb.int/report/world/icrc-explosive-remnants-war-continue-kill-and-maim-civilians-long-after-fig)).

## 🌐 Tier 3 — Rest of the World
WFP launched its 2027 Global Outlook, planning to assist 91 million people at a cost of just over USD 12 billion. WFP cites up to 266 million people facing acute hunger in 2025 (39 million at emergency levels or worse) and warns of a possible third major global food crisis in six years. About 72 percent of 2027 requirements are for crisis response and 28 percent for resilience ([WFP](https://www.wfp.org/news/world-food-programme-sets-out-2027-plan-assist-91-million-people-need-food-crisis-looms)).

IOM's Hurricane Melissa Situation Report #20 (Jamaica, Nov 2025 – Oct 2026) says emergency activities have concluded while shelter recovery continues, with 4,105 households receiving repair assistance and four community centres repaired ([ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/jamaica/iom-hurricane-melissa-situation-report-20)).

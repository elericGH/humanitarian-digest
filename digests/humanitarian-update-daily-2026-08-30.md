# ⚡ Daily Humanitarian Update — Saturday, 29 August 2026
*Produced Saturday, 29 August 2026 | Australian Interest Priority Edition*

---

## 🇦🇺 Australian Priority Alerts

The Nepal-Tibet floods dominate the Australian humanitarian agenda. At least 41 Australians remain missing after Wednesday's flash flood tore through Rasuwa, Nuwakot and Dhading districts, with Foreign Minister Penny Wong warning the number could still rise and describing the search as "a very difficult situation" in "a remote location." DFAT has around 20 staff on the ground in Nepal working to confirm the welfare of unaccounted-for Australians ([ABC News](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-08-29/death-toll-rises-as-rescuers-work-through-flood-devastation/107092742)).

The Australian Government has announced a $5 million humanitarian assistance package for Nepal, delivered through established partners including $2 million to the World Food Programme for food supplies and logistics support, alongside emergency shelter, clean water and sanitation assistance ([DFAT media release](https://www.dfat.gov.au/news/media/Pages/australia-to-help-flood-affected-communities-in-nepal)).

Fourteen Australian charities have joined the Emergency Action Alliance's Nepal Flood Disaster Appeal, mobilising donations for emergency shelter, food, clean water, hygiene supplies and medical care for affected communities ([Emergency Action Alliance](https://emergencyaction.org.au/current-appeals); [ACFID](https://acfid.asn.au/nepal-and-tibet-floods-appeal/)).

ACFID's newly launched State of the Sector Report 2026 shows member agencies raised $750 million from more than 793,000 Australians in 2023-24, underscoring the scale of public support the sector is now drawing on for this and other concurrent appeals ([ACFID](https://acfid.asn.au/)).

## 🌏 Tier 1 — Pacific & Asia-Pacific

Nearly 2,000 people remain missing in Nepal following Wednesday's flash flood, with at least 579 confirmed dead in Nepal and five in Tibet. Satellite imagery indicates the disaster was triggered by a large chunk of ice breaking from a Himalayan glacier, sending ice, rock and mud into the valley below. At least 4,451 people have been injured and rescued, including 129 foreign nationals, with 8,380 police personnel deployed; Nepal has declined offers of international search-and-rescue assistance, saying it is managing the response itself ([ABC News](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-08-29/death-toll-rises-as-rescuers-work-through-flood-devastation/107092742); [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/27/nepal-tibet-floods-what-happened-what-caused-them-and-who-is-missing)).

Monsoon rain intensified by a low-pressure system has suspended government offices and classes across Metro Manila and 15 Philippine provinces, highlighting continued weather-driven disruption across the Asia-Pacific region this week ([Manila Times](https://www.manilatimes.net/2026/08/29/podcasts/headlines-new-flood-risks-slow-nepal-tibet-rescue-august-29-2026/2414133)).

## 🌍 Tier 2 — Middle East & Africa

The World Food Programme has warned that global conflict and disruption to fuel and trade routes are deepening Sudan's hunger crisis, with basic food costs up almost 40 per cent since February. Acting Executive Director Carl Skau called Sudan the world's worst hunger crisis, with almost 19.5 million people facing crisis-level hunger and 9 million displaced; WFP says it needs US$579 million to sustain operations through October 2026 ([WFP](https://www.wfp.org/news/wfp-warns-more-displacement-sudan-fighting-rages-darfurs-and-kordofans-amidst-shrinking); [allAfrica](https://allafrica.com/stories/202608270007.html)).

UNHCR has confirmed it is cutting its 2027 budget by 16 per cent and reducing its workforce by roughly 3,500 positions in response to funding shortfalls, even as global displacement is forecast to reach a record high next year. Some member states have raised concerns about the impact on major host countries such as Uganda and Bangladesh ([US News](https://www.usnews.com/news/world/articles/2026-08-28/un-refugee-agency-scales-back-posts-and-budget-for-2027-documents-show)).

## 🌐 Tier 3 — Rest of the World

Humanitarian needs continue to escalate in earthquake-hit Venezuela, where over 304,000 people have now accessed assistance through the coordinated multi-sector response following June's magnitude 7.2 and 7.5 quakes. World Central Kitchen continues serving thousands of meals daily near La Guaira, and the American Red Cross has lifted its contribution to the IFRC emergency appeal to $4 million ([Red Cross](https://www.redcross.org/about-us/news-and-events/news/2026/red-cross-movement-responds-to-venezuela-earthquakes.html)).

## 🚨 Sudden Onset Hazards

The Nepal-Tibet glacial-lake flood event remains the dominant active sudden-onset hazard, with search and rescue ongoing and flood risk still elevated in the region as authorities warn of further landslides hampering access ([ABC News](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-08-29/death-toll-rises-as-rescuers-work-through-flood-devastation/107092742)). No active tropical cyclone warnings were identified for the Pacific at time of writing.

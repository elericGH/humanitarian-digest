## 🌍 Tier 2 — Middle East & Africa
The Education Cluster recorded 185 education-related incidents in the West Bank (incl. East Jerusalem) in Q3 2026, affecting 149 schools, 26,475 students and 1,512 education personnel — a 99% rise in incidents on Q3 2025. Access-related incidents rose from 37 to 134 and settler violence from six to 31 incidents — [ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/occupied-palestinian-territory/education-related-incidents-west-bank-3rd-quarter-2026-july-september-2026).

A passenger train travelling from Cairo to Aswan derailed at al-Ayyat, Giza; Al Jazeera reports seven killed and dozens injured (its video report says 112 injured) — [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/8/train-crash-kills-four-injures-dozens-near-egypts-cairo-city).

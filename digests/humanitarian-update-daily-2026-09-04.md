## 🇦🇺 Australian Priority Alerts

Australia has ramped up its response to the Nepal-Tibet flood disaster, deploying a 15-person team of specialist drone operators (drawn from NSW Fire and Rescue and the Queensland Fire Department) to support search-and-rescue efforts as the confirmed death toll reached around 1,200, with roughly 4,200 people still missing. Foreign Minister Penny Wong said Nepali authorities were searching sites identified by Australia, including two hotels where missing Australians were last known to be, and that "we have grave concerns" for the 38 Australians still unaccounted for ([ABC News](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-09-03/australia-deploys-drones-to-nepal-as-death-toll-passes-1200/107111120)).

Prime Minister Anthony Albanese, speaking from the Pacific Islands Forum in Palau, announced a further A$3 million in humanitarian assistance for Nepal, bringing Australia's total humanitarian package to around A$11 million and total personnel deployed to 34. This follows an RAAF C-17A Globemaster delivery of relief supplies (1,440 hygiene kits, 576 menstrual hygiene kits, 2,200 solar lights and 432 shelter tool kits) that landed in Nepal on 2 September ([ABC News](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-09-03/australia-deploys-drones-to-nepal-as-death-toll-passes-1200/107111120)).

Emergency Action Alliance, the coalition of 15 Australian aid agencies including ADRA Australia, has an active Nepal Flood Disaster Appeal open for public donations, describing "catastrophic flooding on the Nepal-China border" that has cut off communities and left thousands needing shelter, clean water, food and medical care ([Emergency Action Alliance](https://emergencyaction.org.au/nepal-flood-appeal/)).

## 🌏 Tier 1 — Pacific & Asia-Pacific

The 55th Pacific Islands Forum Leaders Meeting, running in Palau from 30 August to 4 September, has been marked by intensifying China–Taiwan competition for influence, with Beijing's special envoy reportedly confronting Palau's president over Taiwan's presence at the Forum Troika meeting and warning of retaliation, while Taiwan's foreign minister met Palau's president to strengthen ties ([The Diplomat](https://thediplomat.com/2026/09/how-great-power-rivalry-is-fracturing-the-pacific-islands-forum/)).

Pacific leaders at the Palau summit are pressing to convert major powers' geopolitical interest in the region into concrete climate finance, with low-lying communities such as Palau's Melekeok state describing homes already being flooded by rising seas and residents unable to afford to relocate inland ([AP via WSLS](https://www.wsls.com/news/world/2026/09/03/pacific-island-nations-seek-to-turn-geopolitics-into-funding-to-offset-climate-change-harm/)).

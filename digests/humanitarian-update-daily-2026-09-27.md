## 🌍 Tier 2 — Middle East & Africa
The Next Century Foundation's weekly Gaza Information Index, updated 26 September 2026, reports that UN partners completed repairs on 21 September to a damaged sewage pumping station in Jabalya in north Gaza, restoring operations via a locally procured generator. The update notes 19 water and sewage facilities have been repaired between June and August, and around 1 million of more than 60 million metric tonnes of rubble have been cleared since last year, though OCHA says the repair effort remains capacity-constrained because Israel continues to restrict entry of generators, heavy machinery and spare parts. ([Next Century Foundation](https://www.nextcenturyfoundation.org/gaza-information-index-26-september-2026/))

The same update reports that roughly 330,000 children are now attending 200 UNICEF-supported learning centres following Gaza schools' return from the summer break, while this year's US$4 billion OPT humanitarian response plan remains under 40 percent funded. Truck movement into Gaza via Kerem Shalom fell sharply over the week of 18–24 September, from 1,109 to 822 trucks, driven by fewer commercial loads and a near-halving of aid trucks (244 to 185), according to the Gaza Crossings and Borders Authority's weekly bulletin cited in the same report. ([Next Century Foundation](https://www.nextcenturyfoundation.org/gaza-information-index-26-september-2026/))

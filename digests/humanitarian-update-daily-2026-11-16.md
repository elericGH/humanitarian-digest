## 🌏 Tier 1 — Pacific & Asia-Pacific
Tropical Cyclone Xavier intensified into a category 1 cyclone on Sunday night, and the Cook Islands Meteorological Service has a storm warning in force for Manihiki and Rakahanga in the Northern Cooks (the warning for Penrhyn has been cancelled). Damaging gale-force winds of up to 85km/h with gusts to 120km/h are expected, rising to 95km/h with gusts to 130km/h later on Monday night, along with heavy rain and thunderstorms. Residents are advised to secure loose items and fishermen to stay off the sea. [RNZ Pacific](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/pacific/2620097/tropical-cyclone-xavier-to-bring-damaging-winds-to-northern-cook-islands)

El Niño conditions could persist until May 2027, raising drought, water-shortage and wildfire concerns for the Northern Mariana Islands and Guam. A US National Weather Service outlook issued 13 November gave a greater than 79 percent probability of a strong-to-very strong El Niño continuing through January–March 2027, with peak conditions expected around December–January. The Marianas have already experienced several major typhoons in 2026. [RNZ Pacific](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/pacific/2619342/el-nino-could-last-until-may-2027-raising-drought-fears-in-northern-marianas-and-guam)

## 🌍 Tier 2 — Middle East & Africa
Africa CDC says the Ebola (Bundibugyo strain) outbreak in the Democratic Republic of the Congo is "still not under control" six months after it was declared on 15 May. Government data show more than 11,200 cases and 5,500 deaths; Africa CDC's director-general said over 80 percent of new cases emerge outside contact lists and over 65 percent of deaths occur in the community. Eight of the DRC's 26 provinces are affected and cases have been reported in East, Central and West Africa. [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/15/africa-cdc-says-ebola-outbreak-in-dr-congo-still-not-under-control)

A joint statement by Africa CDC, UNFPA, UNICEF, WFP and WHO (dated 15 November) says 400 to 500 new cases are still reported each week across 67 health zones in eight provinces, contact follow-up remains below target, and delays in paying frontline responders slow the response. Children account for more than a quarter of confirmed cases and a third of deaths, and recent imported cases in Kenya and Nigeria underscore the risk of cross-border spread. [WFP](https://www.wfp.org/news/joint-statement-six-months-accessing-hard-reach-communities-key-halting-ebola-outbreak)

In Yemen, forces allied with the internationally recognised government say they are redeploying from Dhubab airport and the Bab al-Mandeb area to defend Aden from the Houthis, and to reinforce efforts to end the Houthi siege of Taiz, where aid supplies are reportedly blocked and civilians cannot leave. Al Jazeera reports about 300,000 people displaced since fighting surged this summer. [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/15/yemeni-government-forces-redeploy-from-bab-al-mandeb-to-defend-aden)

An OCHA Situation Update (No.7, as of 10 November) records 329,117 people displaced in Yemen since 1 August per IOM DTM, about 48,000 more than the previous update, mostly from Lahj and Ta'iz; fighting in Lahj is driving new displacement towards Tuban and Aden is expected to receive more. [ReliefWeb/OCHA](https://reliefweb.int/report/yemen/situation-update-no7-escalation-hostilities-and-displacement-across-yemen-10-november-2026) Separately, the ICRC supported the release of 600 conflict-related detainees by authorities in Sana'a, in a release initiated by the National Committee for Prisoners Affairs and outside the Stockholm Agreement. [ICRC](https://www.icrc.org/en/news-release/yemen-600-conflict-related-detainees-released-sanaa)

A fire destroyed all 59 tents at a Syrian refugee camp in Bar Elias, eastern Lebanon, leaving nearly 500 people homeless, according to Anadolu Agency as cited by Al Jazeera; the cause is unknown. [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/video/newsfeed/2026/11/15/flames-engulf-syrian-refugee-camp-in-eastern-lebanon)

In Gaza, recovery teams have retrieved 70 bodies from the rubble in 35 days; at least 8,000 more are believed to remain trapped. [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/video/newsfeed/2026/11/15/70-bodies-recovered-from-gaza-rubble-thousands-still-missing)

## 🌐 Tier 3 — Rest of the World
Spain has declared a migration emergency in the Canary Islands after more than 9,000 arrivals since the start of November. Maritime Rescue rescued 1,488 migrants aboard 14 boats, mostly arriving at El Hierro from West Africa on Saturday, breaking the previous one-day record of 1,461 set in November 2020. [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/15/record-number-of-migrants-arrive-in-spains-canary-islands)

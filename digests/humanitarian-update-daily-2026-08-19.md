## 🇦🇺 Australian Priority Alerts
No new items in the last 24 hours from ACFID, Emergency Action Alliance, or DFAT. The most recent Australian-angle developments remain the ACFID State of the Sector Report 2026 launch (6 August) and the government's confirmed $5.2 billion 2026–27 ODA envelope, with $2.2 billion directed to the Pacific ([ACFID](https://acfid.asn.au/a-new-era-australian-aid-agencies-adapt-partner-and-maintain-accountability/), [PINA](https://pina.com.fj/2026/08/10/australia-maintains-5-2b-aid-programme-as-pacific-takes-priority/)); neither falls inside today's 24-hour window.

## 🌏 Tier 1 — Pacific & Asia-Pacific
Flooding and landslides driven by the enhanced southwest monsoon combined with tropical cyclones Luis and Maymay have killed at least 26 people and affected an estimated 5.1 million Filipinos, with roughly 3,600 people still in Manila evacuation centres as of 18 August. Masinloc, Zambales, was placed under a state of calamity after coastal flooding damaged homes and agriculture ([BusinessWorld](https://bworldonline.com/the-nation/2026/08/18/771023/death-toll-from-storms-monsoon-rises-to-26-5-1m-filipinos-affected/), [Manila Times](https://www.manilatimes.net/2026/08/18/regions/masinloc-placed-under-state-of-calamity-due-to-flooding/2407242)).

Indonesia's response to the magnitude-7.7 Flores earthquake continues, with nearly 1,500 military and police personnel and 10 aircraft delivering roughly 275 tonnes of aid under a 14-day provincial state of emergency; the death toll stands near 70 following a fatal aftershock on 17 August ([ANTARA](https://bali.antaranews.com/berita/411577/indonesia-mobilizes-swift-response-after-massive-quake-in-flores), [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/indonesia-earthquake-death-toll-flores-island-independence-day/)).

## 🌍 Tier 2 — Middle East & Africa
IOM is appealing for USD 19 million to scale up its emergency response in Lebanon, where the number of people forced to flee their homes has surged; more than 240,000 displaced people have been assisted with food and cash to date across the wider Lebanon–Syria response ([ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/updates)).

Renewed fighting around El Fasher, North Darfur, continues to push families into overcrowded camps with famine conditions confirmed in areas including Zamzam; more than 21 million people face acute food insecurity across Sudan in 2026 ([IRC](https://www.rescue.org/article/crisis-sudan-what-happening-and-how-help)).

## 🌐 Tier 3 — Rest of the World
The UN marks World Humanitarian Day 2026 today, 19 August, under the theme "Families of Fallen Humanitarians," honouring more than 380 humanitarian workers killed in 2024 alone and renewing calls for the protection of health care and aid personnel ([WHO](https://www.who.int/news-room/events/detail/2026/08/19/default-calendar/world-humanitarian-day-2026--honoring-fallen-humanitarians-and-calling-for-greater-protection-of-health-care), [UN](https://www.un.org/en/observances/humanitarian-day)).

Across Latin America, ReliefWeb reports roughly 16,000 people affected by rains and flooding in Peru, alongside an estimated 2–3 million people expected to need food support in Honduras and Guatemala between June and August ([ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/updates)).

## 🚨 Sudden Onset Hazards
Indonesia remains in a declared 14-day state of emergency in East Nusa Tenggara following the 15 August magnitude-7.7 Flores earthquake and a deadly magnitude 5.7–5.8 aftershock on 17 August that killed two more people in Ngada Regency; tsunami warnings issued immediately after the main shock have since been lifted ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/15/at-least-two-killed-as-magnitude-7-7-quake-hits-indonesia), [Wikipedia/2026 Flores earthquake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Flores_earthquake)).

The Philippines remains under active flood and landslide response from the combined southwest monsoon and tropical cyclones Luis and Maymay, with PAGASA continuing to track flood-prone river systems across Luzon ([Philstar](https://www.philstar.com/headlines/2026/08/18/2550028/metro-inundated-monsoon-rains-persist)).

Tropical Storm Lala remains active in the Central Pacific basin, tracked roughly 440 miles from Maro Reef, with advisories current as of 18 August ([search aggregation of NHC/CPHC reporting](https://www.nhc.noaa.gov/)).

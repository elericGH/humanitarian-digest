# ⚡ Daily Humanitarian Update — Friday, 9 October 2026
*Produced Friday, 9 October 2026 | Australian Interest Priority Edition*

## 🇦🇺 Australian Priority Alerts

Australia announced an AUD 7.29 million (about US$5.1 million) renewable energy package for the Pacific, including support for Vanuatu's transition to renewable energy, plus a further AUD 3 million to protect existing Pacific infrastructure, at the Pre-COP31 meeting in Nadi, Fiji. The commitments sit alongside Fiji's US$10 million for the Pacific Resilience Facility and Papua New Guinea's 25 million kina for Tuvalu's land reclamation ([PACNEWS via Islands Business](https://islandsbusiness.com/pacnews/pacnews-three-08-october-2026/)).

COP31 President Murat Kurum used the Nadi Pre-COP briefing to note that Australia is leading the COP31 negotiations, with Türkiye presiding and the Pacific shaping the process. He called for climate finance to reach the most affected countries "without a lot of red tape" ahead of the Antalya summit (9 to 20 November) ([PACNEWS via Islands Business](https://islandsbusiness.com/pacnews/pacnews-three-08-october-2026/)).

## 🌏 Tier 1 — Pacific & Asia-Pacific

**Vanuatu earthquake (M6.3).** A magnitude 6.3 earthquake struck central Vanuatu at 09:00 UTC on 8 October (20:00 local). GDACS issued an **Orange** alert for a medium potential humanitarian impact, with an estimated 10,000 people exposed to strong shaking (MMI VII or higher) and only a minor modelled tsunami wave (0.09 m) ([GDACS](https://gdacs.org/report.aspx?eventid=1570077&episodeid=1738390&eventtype=EQ)). The USGS placed the epicentre about 102 km northeast of Norsup in Malampa province, and there were no immediate reports of damage ([Anadolu Agency](https://www.aa.com.tr/en/asia-pacific/63-magnitude-earthquake-strikes-off-vanuatu/4082027)). Partners with a presence in Vanuatu should watch for NDMO assessments, especially in Penama and Sanma, which were hit by the M7.3 earthquake in March.

**Pacific climate commitments at Pre-COP31.** Fiji says the Nadi Pre-COP turned Pacific priorities into concrete pledges for resilience, renewable energy and infrastructure. Fiji also released a five-year costed carbon market plan and a National Action Plan for Coral Reef Conservation 2026–2032 ([PACNEWS via Islands Business](https://islandsbusiness.com/pacnews/pacnews-three-08-october-2026/)).

**Other regional hazards.** On 8 October GDACS recorded an M5.5 earthquake in the Philippines, new activity at Taal volcano (Philippines), and continuing flood events in Vietnam. It also listed large forest fires in Indonesia (more than 10,000 ha each) ([GDACS](https://gdacs.org/)).

## 🌍 Tier 2 — Middle East & Africa

**Ethiopia, Tigray.** The New Humanitarian reports that resumed fighting has made a severe food crisis worse. 3.9 million people, more than half of Tigray's population, need emergency food aid, 140 districts face catastrophic conditions and more than 252,500 ha of crops have failed. Looters ransacked the depots of the Tigray Disaster Risk Management Commission and the offices of the NGO OSSHD in Mekelle during a power vacuum on 4 October. About 3.2 million people in need are now without food assistance. The Mekelle–Semera corridor (via Afar) was still functional as of 4 October, and calls are growing for a humanitarian pause ([The New Humanitarian](https://www.thenewhumanitarian.org/analysis/2026/10/08/tigray-faces-double-catastrophe-war-and-worsening-hunger)). Separately, Eritrean troops are reportedly moving into Tigray, raising fears of a wider conflict ([allAfrica](https://allafrica.com/stories/202610080109.html)).

**Gaza.** Islamic Relief says Israeli attacks have killed or wounded a Palestinian in Gaza every 90 minutes on average since the ceasefire was announced a year ago, with at least 1,454 killed (including 320 children) and more than 5,000 wounded. Shelter materials, fuel and medical supplies remain blocked, about 82% of structures are damaged or destroyed, and more than 20,000 severely ill patients are waiting for permission to leave for treatment ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/8/israel-kills-or-wounds-palestinian-every-90-minutes-in-gaza-since-ceasefire)).

**Kenya, Ebola.** After Kenya's first confirmed Ebola case (a traveller from DR Congo who died in Nairobi), 10 contacts are in quarantine and 57 others are being traced. More than 650,000 travellers have been screened, and four hospitals have been designated to manage suspected cases ([allAfrica](https://allafrica.com/stories/202610080109.html)).

## 🌐 Tier 3 — Rest of the World

**CERF allocation.** The UN allocated US$160 million from the Central Emergency Response Fund to 12 underfunded crises. US$150 million goes to responses in Sudan, South Sudan, Somalia, Chad, Burkina Faso, Mali, Niger, Cameroon, Afghanistan, the occupied Palestinian territory, Cuba and Yemen, and US$10 million is set aside for disability-inclusive assistance ([allAfrica](https://allafrica.com/stories/202610080109.html)).

**Mexico, Tropical Cyclone Simon.** GDACS has raised an **Orange** alert for SIMON-26, now a Category 4 system (213 km/h) in the eastern Pacific. About 80,000 people are exposed to Category 1 winds or stronger, and models show up to 1,149 mm of rainfall ([GDACS](https://gdacs.org/report.aspx?eventid=1001335&episodeid=6&eventtype=TC)).

## 🚨 Sudden Onset Hazards

- **Earthquake, Vanuatu (M6.3, 8 Oct, Orange):** about 10,000 people exposed to MMI VII or higher; no tsunami threat of note; no damage reported so far ([GDACS](https://gdacs.org/report.aspx?eventid=1570077&episodeid=1738390&eventtype=EQ)).
- **Tropical Cyclone SIMON-26 (Cat 4, Orange):** eastern Pacific off Mexico, with a medium humanitarian impact expected ([GDACS](https://gdacs.org/report.aspx?eventid=1001335&episodeid=6&eventtype=TC)).
- **Typhoon KOGUMA-26 (Cat 4, Green):** offshore in the Western Pacific; no exposed population at Category 1 or higher ([GDACS](https://gdacs.org/report.aspx?eventid=1001333&episodeid=16&eventtype=TC)).
- **Floods:** new or ongoing GDACS flood events in Spain (8 Oct), Nigeria and Vietnam (7 Oct), and Senegal (6 Oct) ([GDACS](https://gdacs.org/)).

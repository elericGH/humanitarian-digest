## 🇦🇺 Australian Priority Alerts
**Oceania disease alerts include Australian influenza data.** A ReliefWeb "epidemic and emerging disease alerts in Oceania" map (13 October edition, published 19 Oct, French-language) reports that Australia's national influenza circulation remains high, with 31,571 cases notified between 21 September and 4 October 2026, down 3% on the previous fortnight. Influenza A(H1N1) has intensified in Tasmania and Western Australia; sentinel-lab positivity rose from 9.6% to 15.2% and sentinel ICU admissions for severe acute respiratory infection rose from 234 to 312, mostly influenza. [ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/map/world/alertes-de-maladies-epidemiques-et-emergentes-en-oceanie-le-13-octobre-2026)

## 🌏 Tier 1 — Pacific & Asia-Pacific
**Cyclone Wati damages Aitutaki harbour, Cook Islands.** RNZ Pacific reports Aitutaki's primary port was partially destroyed as Tropical Cyclone Wati swept through the Southern Cook Islands. Mayor Nick Henry said the new Arutanga Harbour and Marina causeway, which had received more than $7 million of investment, was found underwater. The storm has passed Aitutaki heading towards other outer islands; the Cook Islands Meteorological Office had forecast storm-force winds of 54–76 knots and a high probability of coastal inundation. [RNZ Pacific](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/pacific/2021856/aitutaki-marina-causeway-underwater-harbour-damaged-as-cyclone-wati-sweeps-south)

**Wildfires burn about one-third of Lou Island, PNG.** Villagers in Manus Province appeal for international help after a week of wildfires on Lou Island that they say have scorched at least a third of the island, including the forest used for sawn timber. RNZ Pacific notes bushfires are affecting communities across PNG as winter turns to spring under El Niño drought conditions; locals have had only improvised equipment and say the spread is out of their control. [RNZ Pacific](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/pacific/2020352/wildfires-have-destroyed-one-third-of-small-png-island-responders-say)

**Guam begins recovery after Super Typhoon Champi.** Government offices reopened Monday and cargo is partly moving, but widespread power outages persist: the Guam Power Authority reports electricity demand is being met at only 52% of the island's normal peak, with 70% of feeders back online. All schools remain closed on Guam; the CNMI declared all clear and schools resume. US emergency declarations have been approved for both. [RNZ Pacific](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/pacific/2031677/guam-begins-recovery-from-champi-as-cnmi-declares-all-clear)

**Dengue outbreaks in Marshall Islands and Vanuatu.** The Oceania alerts map reports 137 confirmed dengue cases (DENV-1) in Majuro and Ebeye for 15 August–5 October with no deaths but rising hospitalisations (12); the red dengue alert is maintained. Vanuatu recorded 38 new confirmed cases in epi week 40, a weekly record, bringing the total to 209; an outbreak on Tanna was declared on 2 October. [ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/map/world/alertes-de-maladies-epidemiques-et-emergentes-en-oceanie-le-13-octobre-2026)

**Pacific Partnership 2026 (headline only).** "Palau harbor cleanup among Pacific Partnership 2026 achievements." [IPDF](https://ipdefenseforum.com/2026/10/palau-harbor-cleanup-among-pacific-partnership-2026-achievements/)

## 🌍 Tier 2 — Middle East & Africa
**Yemen: Ta'iz increasingly cut off.** OCHA says more than a quarter of a million people have been displaced since early August, over 82,000 this month. The UN Human Rights Office has verified 240 civilian casualties, half of them children. Humanitarian partners distributed the last available food packages last week, market prices are up at least 50%, and OCHA warns of cholera risk in crowded displacement sites; partners cannot move between Ta'iz city and southern districts since 8 October. [OCHA](https://www.unocha.org/news/todays-top-news-occupied-palestinian-territory-yemen-sudan-ukraine-haiti)

**Gaza: WFP cuts food assistance.** WFP says funding shortfalls force it to reduce support from 1.6 million to 1.1 million people a month; rations are halved (one food parcel instead of two) and hot meals fall from 250,000 to 150,000 a day. An estimated 1.4 million people (67%) face IPC Phase 3 or worse. WFP also halved West Bank assistance from 400,000 to 200,000 people in September. [WFP via ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/occupied-palestinian-territory/funding-cuts-force-wfp-drastically-reduce-lifesaving-assistance-gaza)

**Sudan: drone strikes continue.** OCHA reports drones struck locations in Khartoum including near the airport; a strike in Bir Saliba, West Darfur, reportedly killed 11 people including nine children; strikes also hit Almawashi market in Nyala (at least four killed) and Abu Zabad, North Kordofan (at least four civilians killed). [OCHA](https://www.unocha.org/news/todays-top-news-occupied-palestinian-territory-yemen-sudan-ukraine-haiti)

**ICRC facilitates release of 159 detainees in Sana'a, Yemen (headline only).** [ReliefWeb/ICRC](https://reliefweb.int/report/yemen/yemen-icrc-supports-release-159-detainees-sanaa-enar)

**Mauritius lifts curfew (summary only).** Al Jazeera reports the curfew imposed amid violent protests has been lifted; unrest followed a police shooting of a woman at a rally over rising costs and water shortages.

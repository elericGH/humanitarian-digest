# ⚡ Daily Humanitarian Update — Thursday, 1 October 2026
*Produced Thursday, 1 October 2026 | Australian Interest Priority Edition*

---

## 🇦🇺 Australian Priority Alerts

Australia has committed an additional AUD3 million and deployed specialist drone operators to support Nepal's recovery from the August flood disaster, bringing its total contribution to AUD11 million, according to the Development Policy Centre's September aid roundup published 30 September. Nepal estimates reconstruction at USD4–5 billion and has requested USD20 million from the new global loss and damage facility, which has yet to disburse funds. The Emergency Action Alliance's Nepal Flood Disaster Appeal remains open. ([Devpolicy](https://devpolicy.org/sept-2026-aid-news-20260930/); [Emergency Action Alliance](https://emergencyaction.org.au/nepal-flood-appeal/))

The same roundup reports the government has rejected a Senate committee recommendation to give parliament regular updates on Australia's response to the Myanmar conflict, including its humanitarian programs. This comes as the UN condemned a 28 September airstrike near a market in Kyauktaw, Rakhine State, that reportedly killed 50 people (see Tier 1). ([Devpolicy](https://devpolicy.org/sept-2026-aid-news-20260930/))

Australia will reportedly tie AUD980 million over ten years to finalising a bilateral treaty with Solomon Islands, including AUD142 million over two years for Prime Minister Matthew Wale's free education program. Separately, Solomon Islands told its UN Universal Periodic Review it will deliver free universal education by 2027. ([Devpolicy](https://devpolicy.org/sept-2026-aid-news-20260930/); [RNZ](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/pacific/1667533/solomon-islands-pledges-free-universal-education-by-2027))

## 🌏 Tier 1 — Pacific & Asia-Pacific

**Myanmar / Malaysia:** The UN Secretary-General condemned an airstrike near a market in Kyauktaw, Rakhine State, on 28 September that reportedly killed 50 people and injured nearly 60, and called for unimpeded humanitarian access. The UN also urged Malaysia to halt the reported return of about 1,500 people to Myanmar, saying conditions do not permit safe, voluntary and dignified returns. ([UN Noon Briefing, 29 Sep](https://press.un.org/en/2026/db260929.doc.htm))

**Fiji HIV emergency:** Fiji has declared its HIV outbreak a national emergency. About one in 60 adults are now living with HIV and reported cases have risen 27% in a year, while rates are also climbing in Solomon Islands and PNG. ([Devpolicy](https://devpolicy.org/sept-2026-aid-news-20260930/))

**Pacific climate advocacy:** Leaders from Melanesia, Micronesia and Polynesia used the UN General Assembly to press for far stronger climate action, following Pacific Islands Forum pledges including almost AUD30 million from Australia, GBP10 million from the UK to the Pacific Resilience Facility, and USD150 million from the US. ([RNZ](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/pacific/1659474/pacific-leaders-unite-on-climate-at-unga-think-of-the-children); [Devpolicy](https://devpolicy.org/sept-2026-aid-news-20260930/))

## 🌍 Tier 2 — Middle East & Africa

**Sudan (Darfur):** At least 30 children have died of malnutrition-related complications in the Suni area of eastern Jebel Marra, and more than 100 cases of severe acute malnutrition have been documented in an area with no functioning medical facilities, a displaced persons' coordination group said on 30 September. ([Sudan Tribune](https://sudantribune.com/article/319441))

**Sudan (Nuba Mountains):** An Otoro community leader says fighting with SPLM-N forces around Kauda, South Kordofan, has killed more than 500 people over six months and displaced over 25,000. Many are sheltering in caves and bush with no humanitarian relief. Separately, OCHA warns that funding gaps threaten water trucking for 150,000 people in Tawila, North Darfur. The USD2.9 billion 2026 Sudan plan is less than 43% funded. ([Sudan Tribune](https://sudantribune.com/article/319411); [UN Noon Briefing](https://press.un.org/en/2026/db260929.doc.htm))

**Yemen:** A Houthi offensive that began on 3 September has displaced nearly 134,000 people, including about 73,000 children (UNICEF). WHO reports 838 people killed since early August. More than 18.3 million Yemenis face acute food insecurity in 2026. ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/9/30/we-no-longer-eat-three-meals-yemens-displaced-face-daily-fight-for-food))

**Anticipatory action for El Niño drought:** The Emergency Relief Coordinator has released CERF funds ahead of drought impacts: USD6 million for South Sudan, USD4 million for Zimbabwe and USD3.6 million for Chad (reaching 350,000 people). ([UN Noon Briefing](https://press.un.org/en/2026/db260929.doc.htm))

**DRC and Gaza:** In Ituri, violence reportedly killed at least four displaced people and forced more than 3,000 to flee. In North Kivu, two Ebola responders were reportedly killed in Lubero. The DRC plan is only half funded. In Gaza, OCHA reports Israeli fire near UN facilities and humanitarian convoys on several occasions in recent days, with no casualties reported. ([UN Noon Briefing](https://press.un.org/en/2026/db260929.doc.htm))

## 🌐 Tier 3 — Rest of the World

**Refugee funding crisis:** A new UNHCR report says nearly 8.3 million refugees and other forcibly displaced and stateless people are on the brink of losing vital assistance. UNHCR funding fell from USD5.2 billion to USD3.9 billion between 2024 and 2025, and only 32% of its USD8.5 billion 2026 requirement had been received by end-July. ([UN Noon Briefing](https://press.un.org/en/2026/db260929.doc.htm))

**Sector-wide contraction:** ALNAP's State of the Humanitarian System 2026 finds international humanitarian funding fell by 30% from its 2022 peak just as needs peaked, and that barriers to channelling aid through local actors persist. ([Devpolicy](https://devpolicy.org/sept-2026-aid-news-20260930/))

**Haiti and Ukraine:** The Security Council renewed the Gang Suppression Force mandate. Nearly 1.5 million displaced Haitians are exposed to hurricane-season flooding, and the USD880 million appeal is less than half funded. The Secretary-General also said he is alarmed by escalating Russian attacks on Kyiv and other Ukrainian cities, which are reportedly causing record civilian casualties. ([UN Noon Briefing](https://press.un.org/en/2026/db260929.doc.htm))

## 🚨 Sudden Onset Hazards

**Hurricane Polo (Mexico), GDACS Red alert:** Polo peaked at Category 5 (287 km/h). It made landfall in Baja California Sur on 29 September as a Category 2 hurricane, then made a second landfall in Sonora. GDACS estimated 220,000 people exposed. About 700 people remained in shelters and no serious damage or deaths were immediately reported. Remnant rainfall is driving flash-flood risk across the US Southwest and southern Plains. ([GDACS](https://www.gdacs.org/report.aspx?eventtype=TC&amp=&eventid=1001325); [PBS/AP](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/science/hurricane-polo-makes-landfall-in-baja-california-toppling-trees-and-flooding-coastal-communities))

**Hurricane Nolo (Central Pacific):** Nolo remained a Category 3 hurricane moving away from Hawaii after drenching parts of the Big Island. Forecasters note above-average Pacific storm activity consistent with El Niño. No M6+ earthquakes were reported in the last 24 hours. An M5.1 event occurred near East New Britain, PNG, with no reported impacts. ([PBS/AP](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/science/hurricane-polo-makes-landfall-in-baja-california-toppling-trees-and-flooding-coastal-communities); [QuakeMap24](https://quakemap24.com/en/event/95379ca15e1d74c7469fa102155b0daad644d6b5/earthquake-m5-1-east-new-britain-2026-09-30))

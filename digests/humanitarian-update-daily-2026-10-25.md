## 🌍 Tier 2 — Middle East & Africa
**Hebron: about 2,000 displaced during Israeli raids and lockdown.** Al Jazeera reports Israel imposed a lockdown and displaced thousands of Palestinians from their homes in Hebron as the military conducts raids across the occupied West Bank after two Israeli soldiers were killed in Friday's attack on a reserve base. Hebron's mayor said 2,200 people had been forced from their homes, and Israel's defence minister confirmed "about 2,000" displaced who "will not be allowed to return" until operations conclude. The Palestinian Authority called for international pressure to protect residents, who said evacuation orders were announced by loudspeaker late at night ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/24/more-than-2000-forced-from-homes-in-hebron-amid-israels-west-bank-raids)).

**Sudan: fuel shortages and rising prices deepen cost-of-living crisis.** Al Jazeera reports fuel queues of five hours or more in Port Sudan, with a gallon of petrol costing 35,000 Sudanese pounds in Port Sudan and 40,000 in Gazira State, forcing transport workers to lose working days queuing ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2026/10/24/sudans-soaring-fuel-prices-makes-getting-by-harder-for-workers)).

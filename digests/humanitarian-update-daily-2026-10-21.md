**PALM scheme expansion sought by Solomon Islands.** Prime Minister Matthew Wale called for more job opportunities for Solomon Islanders through Australia's Pacific Australia Labour Mobility (PALM) scheme, giving the keynote at the inaugural PALM Approved Employer Expo in Honiara on Tuesday. ## 🌏 Tier 1 — Pacific & Asia-Pacific

**Papua New Guinea: wildfires on Lou Island.** Wildfires worsened overnight on Lou Island in the Admiralty Islands, breaking through ridgelines towards Rei village, with local authorities saying at least half of the island's forests are gone. A local official said drought and warm winds, plus a lack of equipment, mean "it's practically impossible" to put the fire out, and called for helicopters. Responders are using buckets, knives, axes and spades. The article links the conditions to El Niño. [RNZ Pacific, 20 Oct 11:54 AEDT](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/pacific/2038624/i-don-t-know-what-we-can-do-anymore-lou-island-pleads-for-help-to-fight-wildfires)

**Pacific dengue outbreaks continue.** A ReliefWeb Pacific epidemic alerts map notes that Marshall Islands (Majuro and Ebeye) has recorded 156 confirmed and probable dengue cases for 15 Aug–15 Oct, up 21 from 5 Oct. Samoa reported 64 new dengue cases in EpiWeek 40, up 73% on the prior week. Vanuatu's Ministry of Health declared a dengue outbreak on Malekula Island on 15 October. [ReliefWeb, 20 Oct 16:10 AEDT](https://reliefweb.int/map/world/epidemic-and-emerging-disease-alerts-pacific-20-october-2026)

**Bougainville independence timetable.** A Diplomat opinion piece says Kokoda constituency declared itself "independence ready" in September and that similar ceremonies are being held across Bougainville. It argues Australia can push for a concrete timetable for PNG's independence vote. [The Diplomat, 21 Oct 01:06 AEDT](https://thediplomat.com/2026/10/bougainville-needs-a-credible-timetable-for-pngs-independence-vote-australia-can-help/)

## 🌍 Tier 2 — Middle East & Africa

**Ebola spreads to Nigeria.** Nigeria confirmed its first Ebola case in Abuja, in a woman who travelled from Uganda. She developed symptoms on 7 October and was admitted to a private hospital on 11 October. Health Minister Muhammad Pate said authorities are tracing 44 contacts. Kenya confirmed its ninth case, a 24-year-old already in quarantine. Kenya's health ministry said all nine belong to one cluster and 134 of 135 contacts have been traced. [Al Jazeera, 21 Oct 04:31 AEDT](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/20/kenya-confirms-ninth-ebola-case) The EU's DG ECHO daily map covers the Bundibugyo virus outbreak across DRC, Kenya and Uganda (map only). [ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/map/democratic-republic-congo/democratic-republic-congo-kenya-uganda-ebola-bundibugyo-virus-disease-outbreak-and-eu-response-dg-echo-daily-map-20102026) OCHA also published "The CUBE that helps heal: Response to Ebola in the DRC" (headline only). [OCHA](https://www.unocha.org/news/cube-helps-heal-response-ebola-democratic-republic-congo)

**Ethiopia: renewed hostilities in the north.** The ICRC says it facilitated the evacuation of 161 people from Mekelle to Semera on 28 September and delivered medical supplies to hospitals in Tigray on 13–16 October. It also pre-positioned 6,000 household kits in Tigray and 2,000 in Amhara. [ICRC](https://www.icrc.org/en/article/ethiopia-icrc-responds-humanitarian-needs-amid-renewed-hostilities-north-ethiopia) OCHA says only 16 of the 57 Mobile Health and Nutrition Teams needed in Amhara are operating, and 10 of those 16 are expected to stop by month's end. Partners are preparing food assistance for about 100,000 people in Wag Hamra and North Wollo. [OCHA](https://www.unocha.org/news/todays-top-news-ethiopia-yemen-occupied-palestinian-territory-ukraine-sudan-haiti)

**Yemen: Ta'iz deteriorating.** Humanitarian Coordinator Laurent Bukera said road closures and insecurity are blocking fuel and supplies into Ta'iz, the health system is close to collapse, food and fuel prices have more than doubled, and water production has halved. UNHCR reports more than 4,000 people have fled to Djibouti and Somalia. A boat travelling from Djibouti to Yemen capsized on 8 October, with 139 bodies recovered and 82 survivors. [OCHA](https://www.unocha.org/news/todays-top-news-ethiopia-yemen-occupied-palestinian-territory-ukraine-sudan-haiti)

**Gaza: winter flood risk and food aid cuts.** OCHA says displacement sites hosting over 650 families in Khan Younis are at risk of flooding, including 160 families in a low-lying pit. Shelter partners reached about 37,500 households in the first two weeks of October. [OCHA](https://www.unocha.org/news/todays-top-news-ethiopia-yemen-occupied-palestinian-territory-ukraine-sudan-haiti) Al Jazeera reports funding shortages threaten food aid for half a million Palestinians (headline and summary only). [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/video/newsfeed/2026/10/20/jerusalem-daily-half-a-million-palestinians-lose-food-aid)

**Other items (headline only).** Jordan Operational Update, Jul–Sept 2026 (UNHCR) [ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/jordan/jordan-operational-update-jul-sept-2026); Sudan Nutrition Partners Presence Map, September 2026 [ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/sudan/nutrition-partners-presence-map-september-2026); West and Central Africa: Latest Events at a glance, 13–19 October 2026 (OCHA) [ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/cameroon/west-and-central-africa-latest-events-glance-13-19-october-2026).

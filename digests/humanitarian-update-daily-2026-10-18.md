## 🌏 Tier 1 — Pacific & Asia-Pacific
**Typhoon Champi (Guam / Marianas).** Guam remains under its highest typhoon alert as Category 4 Typhoon Champi is expected to pass just south of or over southern Guam from late Saturday into Sunday morning, with sustained winds forecast up to about 225 km/h. Residents were ordered to stay indoors, six evacuation centres were open with more than 730 people sheltering as of 5pm Saturday, and the Guam Power Authority warned of outages. [RNZ Pacific](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/pacific/1974263/category-4-typhoon-champi-bears-down-on-guam). Earlier RNZ reports tracked its rapid intensification and port closures: [RNZ](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/pacific/1969884/champi-threatens-marianas-as-guam-braces-for-destructive-winds), [RNZ](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/pacific/1972209/guam-on-high-alert-as-typhoon-champi-strengthens-ports-shut).

**Tropical Cyclone Wati (Cook Islands).** Wati strengthened to Category 2 over the Northern Cook Islands, with maximum sustained winds of 100 km/h and gusts to 140 km/h at 8am local time. It is expected to move southeast and reach Category 3 for a time on Sunday over open water. The Cook Islands Meteorological Service has issued alerts across the whole group. [RNZ Pacific](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/pacific/1974118/tc-wati-strengthens-to-category-2); see also [Aitutaki residents urged to heed updates](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/pacific/1972158/aitutaki-residents-urged-to-heed-updates-as-wati-approaches).

**Indo-Pacific security (not humanitarian).** Japan Coast Guard says unauthorised Chinese ship surveys in Japan's EEZ have more than doubled in 2026 compared with last year. [Indo-Pacific Defense Forum](https://ipdefenseforum.com/2026/10/chinas-intrusions-into-japanese-waters-surged-in-2026/)

## 🌍 Tier 2 — Middle East & Africa
**Kenya — Ebola.** Kenya confirmed four more Ebola Bundibugyo cases, bringing the total to seven. Three are health workers who treated the first patient, and the fourth is a relative who had been quarantined since 6 October. These are the first known infections passed on inside Kenya. 128 of the first patient's 130 contacts have been quarantined, and the ministry says there is no evidence of community spread. The cases are linked to the DRC epidemic, reported at more than 9,000 cases and more than 4,300 deaths. [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/17/kenya-confirms-four-more-ebola-cases-bringing-total-to-seven)

**Gaza.** Israeli drone attacks killed at least six Palestinians across Gaza on Saturday, per medical workers. Gaza's Health Ministry says 1,511 Palestinians have been killed since the 10 October 2025 "ceasefire". Al Jazeera notes UNICEF reports more than 340 children killed since the ceasefire began. [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/17/israeli-drone-attacks-kill-six-palestinians-across-gaza-despite-ceasefire)

**Yemen.** The ICRC supported the unilateral release of 159 people detained in connection with the conflict by authorities in Sanaa. [ICRC](https://www.icrc.org/en/news-release/yemen-icrc-supports-release-154-detainees-sanaa)

**Hormuz / Gulf (headline and summary only).** Iranian state TV aired footage said to show an oil tanker exploding after striking a naval mine in Hormuz. [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/video/newsfeed/2026/10/17/iranian-tv-airs-video-said-to-show-tanker-explosion-in-hormuz). Separately, Egypt's president pledged "any measure" to defend Saudi Arabia amid Houthi attacks (headline only). [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2026/10/17/egypts-sisi-seeks-authorisation-for-defence-assistance-to-saudi-arabia)

**WFP country briefs.** WFP published October country briefs for [Iran](https://reliefweb.int/report/iran-islamic-republic/wfp-iran-country-brief-october-2026) (59,968 people assisted in September) and [Iraq](https://reliefweb.int/report/iraq/wfp-iraq-country-brief-october-2026) (8,948 people assisted in September).

**Tunisia.** Thousands marched in Tunis, led by the UGTT union, in the largest protest against President Saied in years over living conditions and restrictions on freedoms. [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/17/thousands-rally-for-biggest-protest-in-years-against-tunisias-saied)

## 🇦🇺 Australian Priority Alerts

A Fijian parliamentary committee has advised against immediate ratification of the Fiji–Australia Vuvale Union and the Ocean of Peace Alliance (Veitacini Treaty), both signed in July. The Standing Committee on Foreign Affairs and Defence recommended further negotiations with Australia, citing concerns about mutual defence obligations, visiting forces, intelligence sharing and the transparency of a ten-year work plan. Prime Minister Sitiveni Rabuka said he was "very disappointed" and would pursue "another route" in the House ([RNZ Pacific](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/pacific/2349209/parliamentary-committee-puts-ratification-of-fiji-australia-security-treaty-on-hold)).

Australia and Malaysia have advanced defence relations through new agreements (headline only; see [Indo-Pacific Defense Forum](https://ipdefenseforum.com/2026/11/agreements-advance-australia-malaysia-defense-relations/)).

## 🌍 Tier 2 — Middle East & Africa

WHO has approved clinical trials of two new vaccines designed for the Bundibugyo strain of Ebola. The DRC's National Institute for Biomedical Research is leading the trial, with results expected in early 2027. Al Jazeera reports more than 5,300 deaths since May, mostly in the DRC, and 10,444 cases confirmed by the African CDC, with cases also in Kenya (nine, including two deaths) and Nigeria (one) ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/4/who-approves-trials-for-two-new-ebola-vaccines)).

OCHA's daily top news covers Yemen, the occupied Palestinian territory, DRC, Sudan and Somalia (headline only; [OCHA](https://www.unocha.org/news/todays-top-news-yemen-occupied-palestinian-territory-democratic-republic-congo-sudan-somalia)). The ICRC reports delivering a shipment of medical supplies to health facilities in Taiz city, Yemen (headline only; [ICRC](https://www.icrc.org/en/news-release/yemen-icrc-delivers-shipment-vital-medical-supplies-health-facilities-taiz-city)).

## 🌐 Tier 3 — Rest of the World

Ukraine raised the death toll from a Russian strike on Komyshuvakha, Zaporizhia region, to 40. The strike hit a school building used to distribute aid, and a follow-up strike hit an underground shelter. Zelenskyy said more than 30 people may remain under rubble; Russian drones have interrupted the search ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/4/ukraine-raises-death-toll-from-russian-strike-on-zaporizhia-to-40-people)).

OCHA published "Haiti: Nationwide Response – Situation Report No. 3 (as of 30 September 2026)" (headline only; [ReliefWeb/OCHA](https://reliefweb.int/report/haiti/haiti-nationwide-response-situation-report-no-3-30-september-2026)). OCHA also covers El Niño drought response in Guatemala (headline only; [OCHA](https://www.unocha.org/news/race-against-time-guatemala-saving-lives-el-nino-drought)), and the UN country team in Ecuador issued a flash update on El Niño rains and floods as of 1 November (headline only; [ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/ecuador/ecuador-flash-update-01-enos-lluvias-y-inundaciones-al-1-de-noviembre)).

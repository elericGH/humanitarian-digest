*Produced Wednesday, 25 November 2026 | Australian Interest Priority Edition*

## 🇦🇺 Australian Priority Alerts

Australia joined the foreign ministers of Belgium, Canada, Cyprus, Czech Republic, Estonia, Iceland, Latvia, Luxembourg, New Zealand, Portugal, Spain, The Gambia and the United Kingdom in a joint statement on the International Court of Justice decision on Myanmar (23 November). The statement says the Court found Myanmar violated its obligations under the Genocide Convention, calls the judgment binding, and calls it "an important step toward accountability and justice for Rohingya victims and survivors". It also stresses that conditions for the voluntary, safe, dignified and sustainable return of Rohingya refugees are essential. [ReliefWeb – Joint Statement on the ICJ decision on Myanmar](https://reliefweb.int/report/myanmar/joint-statement-international-court-justice-decision-myanmar-23-november-2026)

Australia's diphtheria outbreak continues in the latest Pacific epidemic alert map (as of 24 November), which cites the Australian Centre for Disease Control update as at 16 November: 653 cases notified in 2026 against an average of 17 for the same period in 2022–2025. Fourteen cases were notified in the last fortnight (Western Australia 7, Northern Territory 5, South Australia 1, Queensland 1). Aboriginal and/or Torres Strait Islander people account for 95.3% of cases, 120 people (18.4%) have been hospitalised, and one death was notified in April 2026. Activity remains below the late May–early June peak. [ReliefWeb – Epidemic and emerging disease alerts in the Pacific as of 24 November 2026](https://reliefweb.int/map/world/epidemic-and-emerging-disease-alerts-pacific-24-november-2026)

No new items were returned by ACFID (newest item 18 November) or Emergency Action Alliance (newest item 12 November) in the last 24 hours.

## 🌏 Tier 1 — Pacific & Asia-Pacific

**El Niño drought deepens across the Pacific.** Fiji has declared a national state of natural disaster; 11 of 14 water sources in the Western Division are at critical levels, the government has allocated FJ$20 million, and school will finish four days early (27 November). Energy Fiji warns its hydro dams could be forced to shut by the end of January 2027 if dry conditions persist. Papua New Guinea is preparing drought assistance for 680,000 people, and Tonga's 27-day state of emergency covers all land and sea areas. A new analysis by seven UN agencies says El Niño is likely to continue over the next six months, peaking in December, and names PNG and Tonga as countries of particular concern. [RNZ Pacific](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/pacific/2768558/pacific-governments-step-up-el-nino-response-as-drought-and-water-crisis-deepen)

**PNG: five women allegedly buried alive over sorcery accusations.** Five women, one of them pregnant, were allegedly buried alive over the weekend in Kaa village, Southern Highlands Province. Police Commissioner David Manning reportedly confirmed that three suspects are in custody; Police Minister John Pundari said those responsible would face "the full force of the law". The Minister for Community Development, Religion and Youth issued a statement with UN Women PNG condemning the killings. [RNZ Pacific](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/pacific/2768470/agents-of-the-devil-outrage-in-png-after-five-women-allegedly-buried-alive-over-sorcery-claims)

**Pacific disease alerts.** The latest alert map shows PNG's measles blue alert in place, with acute fever and rash cases rising from 11 (EpiWeek 44) to 24 (EpiWeek 46) and 505 cases year to date. French Polynesia remains in an influenza A epidemic phase (red alert): 142 confirmed cases and 56 hospitalisations in EpiWeek 46, and six deaths since 5 October. Yap State (FSM) is back at the diarrhoea alert threshold. [ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/map/world/epidemic-and-emerging-disease-alerts-pacific-24-november-2026)

**West Papua.** Calls for an independent probe after Cenderawasih University student leader Wialo Silak died in hospital in Jayapura weeks after an alleged assault by police intelligence officers; his family, supported by the Legal Aid Institute in Papua, is demanding an investigation. [RNZ Pacific](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/pacific/2768585/call-for-probe-after-west-papuan-student-leader-dies-weeks-after-alleged-police-assault)

**Myanmar.** The UK announced fresh Myanmar sanctions following the ICJ genocide ruling and said it would work with international partners "to ensure that this judgment leads to meaningful action and renewed international pressure" (feed excerpt only). [The Diplomat](https://thediplomat.com/2026/11/uk-announces-fresh-myanmar-sanctions-following-icj-genocide-ruling/) The Landmine Monitor 2026 found global landmine casualties at a five-year high in 2025 (6,929 casualties, 84% civilians, half of them children), with Myanmar recording 1,891 deaths, the highest of any country for a third year. [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/24/global-landmine-casualties-hit-a-five-year-high-driven-by-myanmar-syria)

**Nepal.** Three months after the August floods, WFP called for sustained support as winter sets in; it has provided food assistance to more than 20,000 people and multipurpose cash to 22,400. The preliminary PDNA estimates recovery needs at NPR 706.65 billion (USD 4.76 billion) and the disaster at 1,464 deaths and 5,285 missing. [WFP](https://www.wfp.org/news/three-months-after-nepal-floods-wfp-calls-sustained-support-affected-communities-bitter) · [ReliefWeb – Rasuwa Flood Situation Report No. 13](https://reliefweb.int/report/nepal/nepal-rasuwa-flood-situation-report-no-13-20-november-2026)

**Defence.** Japan and Vietnam are discussing joint defence asset production (headline only). [IPDF](https://ipdefenseforum.com/2026/11/japan-vietnam-discuss-joint-defense-asset-production/)

## 🌍 Tier 2 — Middle East & Africa

**Yemen.** More than 360,000 people have been newly displaced since 1 August (up from about 321,000), with Taiz accounting for 64%. About 350,000 children in Ta'iz missed a national measles and rubella vaccination campaign because insecurity blocked vaccine delivery. [UNHCR via ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/yemen/yemen-emergency-unhcr-external-update-8-23-november-2026) · [OCHA](https://www.unocha.org/news/todays-top-news-ukraine-yemen-sudan-occupied-palestinian-territory-democratic-republic-congo)

**Sudan.** OCHA reports renewed fighting driving displacement: more than 6,700 newly displaced families in two sites near Ed Damazine (Blue Nile), families sheltering under trees in North Darfur, and more than 1,100 people displaced in Abu Zabad (West Kordofan) on top of 6,400 since Thursday. [OCHA](https://www.unocha.org/news/todays-top-news-ukraine-yemen-sudan-occupied-palestinian-territory-democratic-republic-congo)

**DR Congo.** OCHA's Flash Update #2 says more than 447,000 people were affected by successive displacement and return movements in under two weeks in Kalehe and Kabare territories (South Kivu). At least seven health centres and the Bunyakiri General Reference Hospital are closed or relocated, and humanitarian access remains highly constrained. [OCHA via ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/democratic-republic-congo/rd-congo-sud-kivu-flash-update-2-la-poursuite-des-violences-kalehe-aggrave-la-vulnerabilite-de-centaines-de-milliers-de-civils-dans-les-territoires-de-kalehe-et-de-kabare)

**Lebanon and Israel/Palestine.** Lebanese NGO forums report civilians continue to face harm despite cessation-of-hostilities arrangements. [ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/lebanon/violations-international-humanitarian-law-lebanon-harm-continues-despite-cessation-hostilities-arrangements) OCHA reports strikes continuing in populated areas of Gaza and calls for better protection of aid workers. [OCHA](https://www.unocha.org/news/todays-top-news-ukraine-yemen-sudan-occupied-palestinian-territory-democratic-republic-congo)

## 🌐 Tier 3 — Rest of the World

**Ukraine.** OCHA facilitated an inter-agency convoy to a front-line community in Kherson where 600 people remain after nearly a year of severe power disruption. In the past day at least seven civilians were killed and nearly 80 injured; in Odesa some 80,000 households and businesses remain without heating. [OCHA](https://www.unocha.org/news/todays-top-news-ukraine-yemen-sudan-occupied-palestinian-territory-democratic-republic-congo)

**Global.** Five women are killed every hour globally by a partner or relative, according to UN figures reported by Al Jazeera (headline only). [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/24/five-women-killed-every-hour-globally-by-partner-relative-in-2025-un)

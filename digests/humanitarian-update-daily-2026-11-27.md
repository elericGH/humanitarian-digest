## 🌏 Tier 1 — Pacific & Asia-Pacific

**Afghanistan winterisation.** The Shelter Cluster dashboard (as of 15 Nov) shows 61.1K households identified as needing winterisation assistance, with 28.4K targeted across 102 priority districts. 20.8K households are committed for heater and fuel assistance, but only 311 households had received winter clothing and 431 blankets so far. [ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/afghanistan/afghanistan-winterization-response-dashboard-15-november-2026)

**Rohingya after the ICJ genocide ruling.** The New Humanitarian reports the landmark ruling raises hopes for over a million people in Bangladesh camps, yet civil war and denied citizenship still block their return; an expert quoted calls the verdict largely symbolic without political action. ## 🌍 Tier 2 — Middle East & Africa

**Yemen.** WHO reports hostilities have intensified since the 6 Aug escalation; by 16 Nov 12,779 conflict-related casualties (2,260 deaths) were recorded, and IOM counted 347,321 people displaced between 1 Aug and 14 Nov. Frontlines have moved through Lahj toward Aden, and Taiz faces severe fuel and surgical supply shortages. [ReliefWeb/WHO](https://reliefweb.int/report/yemen/who-and-partners-sustain-lifesaving-surgical-care-conflict-escalates-yemen) The ICRC facilitated transfer of human remains across front lines (Dhamar–Lahj–Sana'a) between 24 and 26 Nov, under the Dec 2025 Muscat declaration. [ICRC](https://www.icrc.org/en/news-release/yemen-icrc-facilitates-transfer-human-remains-across-front-lines) NRC warns of "catastrophic civilian suffering as donors and media look elsewhere" (headline only). [ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/yemen/yemen-catastrophic-civilian-suffering-donors-and-media-look-elsewhere-nrc)

**Gaza / oPt.** OCHA's 26 Nov situation report notes at least 600 aid workers killed since Oct 2023 (13 since the ceasefire announcement), 692 suspected meningitis cases (1 Oct–23 Nov), and only 35% of minimum winter shelter requirements funded. [ReliefWeb/OCHA](https://reliefweb.int/report/occupied-palestinian-territory/opt-humanitarian-situation-report-26-november-2026) Al Jazeera reports about 1.36 million people (65% of Gaza's population) live in tents; an early-November storm flooded about 70,000 tents per Gaza's Government Media Office. [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/26/why-are-winter-rains-forcing-palestinians-in-gaza-to-flee-again)

**Gulf attacks.** Kuwait says two Italian technicians died after an Iranian drone attack on a "vital site"; Iran denies involvement. [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/26/two-italians-killed-after-iranian-drones-strike-facility-says-kuwait) A separate attack on Saudi Arabia's Jazan refinery killed one person (headline only beyond summary). [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/26/attack-on-saudi-aramcos-jazan-refinery-kills-worker-disrupts-operations)

**DR Congo Ebola.** MSF says close to 12,000 people have been infected since the outbreak was declared in May, with about half of those infected dying; many arrive at treatment centres late. [ReliefWeb/MSF](https://reliefweb.int/report/democratic-republic-congo/ebola-disease-survivors-share-their-stories)

**Other (headlines only).** UNHCR Syria Operational Update, October 2026 [ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/syrian-arab-republic/unhcr-syria-operational-update-october-2026); Chad: Adré and Dougui site profiling [ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/chad/profilage-du-site-spontane-dadre-et-du-site-amenage-de-dougui-province-du-ouaddai-tchad-septembre-2026); Zimbabwe cholera response final report [ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/zimbabwe/zimbabwe-cholera-response-final-report-mdrzw021-26-november-2026); Guinea-Bissau military leaders delay presidential election until 2028 [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/26/guinea-bissau-presidential-election-delayed-by-two-years).

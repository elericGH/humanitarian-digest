## 🌏 Tier 1 — Pacific & Asia-Pacific
Japan's Kumamoto earthquake response continues, with AMDA publishing its eighth emergency relief update on 25 August covering ongoing medical and shelter support in quake-hit Kumamoto Prefecture, more than three weeks after the magnitude-6.8 quake killed at least 39 people ([ReliefWeb/AMDA](https://reliefweb.int/report/japan/amda-emergency-relief-8-kumamoto-earthquake-japan-25-august-2026)).

Tropical Storm Moke passed south of Hawai'i over the weekend, dumping up to 15 inches of rain on the Big Island and triggering flood and mudslide warnings in areas already hit by Hurricane Lala; the system weakened to a remnant low and dissipated by 25 August without a direct landfall ([NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/08/23/nx-s1-5941769/tropical-storm-moke-hawaii), [Honolulu Star-Advertiser](https://www.staradvertiser.com/2026/08/24/breaking-news/flood-watch-remains-for-big-island-as-downgraded-moke-heads-west/)).

## 🌍 Tier 2 — Middle East & Africa
Israeli forces raided and seized UNRWA's Qalandiya Training Centre in Kafr Aqab, occupied East Jerusalem, on 25 August, expelling staff and beginning conversion of the site — which served roughly 16,000 Palestinian refugees — into a municipal complex. UNRWA and human rights lawyers say the takeover breaches international law and the ICJ's December 2025 advisory opinion ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/25/israeli-forces-raid-unrwa-training-centre-in-occupied-east-jerusalem)).

The DRC's Ebola outbreak — now the country's deadliest on record — reached 5,515 confirmed cases and 2,642 deaths (a nearly 48% case-fatality rate) as of 24 August, with Pope Leo appealing for greater global support ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/24/ebola-cases-in-drc-top-5515-as-pope-leo-urges-global-action-to-save-lives)).

The Red Cross Movement condemned a wave of attacks on Ebola responders in DRC and Uganda: 12 volunteers injured and ambulances damaged or torched across 11 incidents in Ituri, Haut-Uele and North Kivu provinces in the week to 24 August, complicating the outbreak response ([ICRC](https://www.icrc.org/en/news-release/drc-international-red-cross-and-red-crescent-movement-condemns-attacks-its-volunteers)).

## 🌏 Tier 1 — Pacific & Asia-Pacific

**Fiji declares national disaster over El Niño drought (RNZ, 18 Nov).** Cabinet declared a nationwide disaster, enabling mandatory evacuations, curfews, travel restrictions, water rationing and land-use rules, with government centrally directing response teams. Greater Suva has night-time household water restrictions (9pm–4am) after the Tamavua Water Treatment Plant lost over 15 percent of its raw water inflow, and wildfires are occurring daily. FJ$20 million has been reallocated, and more than two million litres of emergency water went to maritime communities over the past month. [RNZ Pacific](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/pacific/2660183/fiji-declares-state-of-national-disaster-over-el-nino-drought)

**Cyclone Xavier, Northern Cook Islands (RNZ, 19 Nov, 07:08 AEDT).** Xavier was downgraded to category 4 (about 175 km/h) after reaching category 5 earlier, and is expected to weaken to category 3 within 12 hours. Pukapuka and Nassau remain under a gale warning, and more than 300 people sheltered on Pukapuka. Two NZ Rapid Response Team personnel arrived to support humanitarian coordination. See also Sudden Onset Hazards. [RNZ Pacific](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/pacific/2677188/xavier-downgraded-to-category-4-as-nz-rapid-response-team-arrives-in-cook-islands) · [earlier report](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/pacific/2662253/cyclone-shelters-activated-as-category-5-xavier-threatens-northern-cook-islands) (headline only)

**Rio Tinto commits US$250m for Panguna mine legacy (RNZ, 19 Nov).** Rio Tinto agreed with Bougainville Copper Limited, the Autonomous Bougainville Government and community leaders to set up a fund for environmental and human rights harm identified in the Panguna Mine Legacy Impact Assessment, plus social projects. The mine has been closed since 1989. [RNZ Pacific](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/pacific/2674427/rio-tinto-commits-us-250m-to-address-damage-from-bougainville-s-panguna-mine)

## 🌍 Tier 2 — Middle East & Africa

**Gaza: new Israeli ground operation (Al Jazeera, 18 Nov).** Israeli forces launched a ground operation in central Gaza, advancing west of the "Yellow Line" and forcing residents of Nuseirat and Bureij to flee again. Israel controls roughly two-thirds of the enclave, leaving over two million Palestinians in the remainder, mostly in tents. Gaza's Health Ministry reports 1,658 killed and 6,000 wounded since the October 2025 ceasefire, and about 100,000 tents are stuck outside Gaza. [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/18/what-does-israels-new-ground-offensive-mean-for-palestinians-in-gaza)

**Gulf: renewed Iran–US exchanges (Al Jazeera, 18 Nov).** After a US strike on a missile site on Qeshm Island on 17 November, Iran launched drone attacks on a US-used airbase in Kuwait and several attacks on Bahrain on 18 November. The article reports no civilian casualties; Gulf states and the Arab League condemned the attacks. [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/18/kuwait-bahrain-face-new-iran-attacks-after-us-strike-on-qeshm-island)

**Ethiopia: ICRC in Oromia (18 Nov).** Working mainly with the Ethiopian Red Cross Society, ICRC reports medical donations reaching over 125,000 patients, restored hand pumps giving 90,000 people safe water, agricultural tools for 7,000 farming families and cash for almost 2,900 families. [ICRC](https://www.icrc.org/en/article/ethiopia-helping-those-caught-in-conflict-oromia)

## 🌏 Tier 1 — Pacific & Asia-Pacific

**Pacific fuel crisis cost the region nearly US$1 billion in six months.** NZ briefings reveal that the fuel crisis, triggered in late February by the war between Iran and the United States, has cost the Pacific nearly a billion dollars in its first six months (headline and summary only). ([RNZ Pacific](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/pacific/2558217/pacific-fuel-crisis-cost-region-nearly-1-billion-in-six-months-nz-briefings-reveal))

**COP31 and Pacific climate voices.** Pacific leaders say climate action must continue even though the US has no official presence at the talks in Türkiye ([RNZ Pacific](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/pacific/2558037/cop31-climate-action-must-continue-even-if-us-is-not-here-pacific-leaders-say)). A Kiribati minister is calling for faster access to climate funds for vulnerable Pacific nations ([RNZ Pacific](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/pacific/2551214/kiribati-minister-calls-for-faster-access-to-climate-funds-for-vulnerable-pacific-nations)), and a Solwata virtual-reality documentary about a Solomon Islands island that "no longer exists" is heading to COP31 ([RNZ Pacific](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/pacific/2551541/our-island-no-longer-exists-solomon-islands-climate-documentary-heads-to-cop31)) (headlines and summaries only).

**Nepal: survivors race to rebuild before winter after floods.** Canadian Lutheran World Relief reports classrooms in a northern Nepal mountain village that had become shelters for displaced families are reopening; a community member says his father died in the disaster and his sister remains missing. ([ReliefWeb / Canadian Lutheran World Relief](https://reliefweb.int/report/nepal/after-floods-nepals-survivors-race-rebuild-winter))

**Regional security and preparedness.** Defence ministers from the US, Japan, Australia and the Philippines, meeting in Cebu on 28 October, agreed that "Squad" nations will establish a coordination hub in the Philippines (headline and summary only) ([The Diplomat](https://thediplomat.com/2026/11/squad-nations-to-establish-coordination-hub-in-the-philippines-defense-chiefs-say/)). Indonesia's Vice President has submitted his resignation amid a school credentials controversy (headline and summary only) ([The Diplomat](https://thediplomat.com/2026/11/indonesian-vice-president-submits-resignation-amid-school-credentials-controversy/)). An Indo-Pacific Defense Forum piece argues disaster preparedness bolsters civilian and military response to typhoons, earthquakes and tsunamis ([IPDF](https://ipdefenseforum.com/2026/11/disaster-preparedness-bolsters-civilian-military-response/)).

## 🌍 Tier 2 — Middle East & Africa

**Gaza: air strike on Nasser Hospital.** Medical Aid for Palestinians, citing the Palestinian Health Ministry, says an Israeli airstrike on Nasser Hospital in Khan Younis overnight on 12 November killed at least six people and injured more than 30, including patients and staff; surgeon Dr Rizq Abu Shaeira was among those killed, and one unexploded missile remains inside the building ([ReliefWeb / MAP](https://reliefweb.int/report/occupied-palestinian-territory/we-lost-some-patients-i-had-been-caring-uk-must-impose-sanctions-after-nasser-hospital-surgeon-killed)). Al Jazeera separately reports a Palestinian baby died from cold amid Israeli restrictions on aid (headline and summary only) ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/12/palestinian-baby-dies-from-cold-in-gaza-amid-israeli-restrictions-on-aid)), and The New Humanitarian carries a first-person piece on Gaza's winter ([The New Humanitarian](https://www.thenewhumanitarian.org/opinion/first-person/2026/11/12/how-many-winters-must-palestinians-gaza-survive-they-are-allowed-live)).

**Sudan: WFP chief calls for end to fighting.** WFP Executive Director Luke Lindberg said "we cannot outfeed the war". WFP was supporting more than four million people in August, including 880,000 in areas at risk of famine; funding constraints forced caseload cuts from September, with an estimated 900,000 people losing food assistance. ([ReliefWeb / WFP](https://reliefweb.int/report/sudan/world-food-programme-executive-director-calls-end-fighting-sudan))

**Eastern Africa: El Niño flash update.** OCHA reports heavy rains intensifying across Somalia, Ethiopia, Kenya, Uganda, South Sudan, Rwanda and Burundi, while parts of Ethiopia and South Sudan face worsening drought. Flooding is causing displacement and disease risk; Somalia has nearly 170,000 people affected. ([ReliefWeb / OCHA](https://reliefweb.int/report/somalia/eastern-africa-el-nino-status-and-humanitarian-impact-flash-update-no-1-12-november-2026))

**DR Congo: Ebola outbreak "out of control" six months on.** MSF says the second-largest Ebola outbreak on record remains out of control, with a disconnect between resources mobilised and what reaches patients and communities. ([ReliefWeb / MSF](https://reliefweb.int/report/democratic-republic-congo/msf-drc-ebola-persistent-gaps-between-resources-and-action-remain-six-months))

**OCHA headlines.** OCHA's daily top news covers the occupied Palestinian territory and Yemen ([OCHA](https://www.unocha.org/news/todays-top-news-occupied-palestinian-territory-yemen)); a feature covers recovery from malnutrition in Uganda's Karamoja (headline only) ([OCHA](https://www.unocha.org/news/uganda-helping-families-recover-malnutrition-karamoja)).

## 🌐 Tier 3 — Rest of the World

**Panama: one month after the magnitude 7.5 earthquake.** The UN Country Team's Situation Report No. 09 says around 2,700 people remain in 100 shelters (almost a fifth fewer than a week earlier); more than 8,000 households have been surveyed for assistance, with WFP cash assistance planned from early December; sanitation gaps persist at displacement sites. ([ReliefWeb / OCHA](https://reliefweb.int/report/panama/panama-earthquake-azuero-veraguas-situation-report-no-09-11-november-2026-1230))

**UN launches Disaster Risk Financing Platform at COP31** to make humanitarian responses faster and more predictable (headline only). ([ReliefWeb / UN](https://reliefweb.int/report/world/un-launches-disaster-risk-financing-platform-cop31-make-humanitarian-respons))

## 🌏 Tier 1 — Pacific & Asia-Pacific
Flooding continues along the Mu River in Myanmar's Sagaing Region, downstream from the Thaphanseik Dam, with nearly 15,000 people from about 3,000 households affected in Myinmu Township and rising water threatening Taze, Kanbalu, Ye-U, Khin-U and Depayin townships as monsoon rains persist ([GDACS flood alert, updated through 6 Aug](https://www.gdacs.org/media.aspx?eventid=1104011&episodeid=9&eventtype=FL)).

A magnitude 5.6 earthquake struck the Kermadec Islands Region, north of New Zealand, on 13 August at 16:11 UTC. No damage or casualties have yet been reported, and the event is being monitored by the Global Disaster Alert and Coordination System ([GDACS](http://gdacs.org/report.aspx?eventid=1557839&episodeid=1724872&eventtype=EQ)).

Five active tropical cyclones — Peilou, Nangka, Cristobal, One-C and Hernan — were being tracked across the Western Pacific and Indian Ocean basins as of 13 August 18:00 UTC, per GDACS cyclone monitoring ([GDACS](https://gdacs.org/)); none had reached a red alert level at time of writing.

## 🌍 Tier 2 — Middle East & Africa
OCHA's daily top-news roundup for 12 August covered continuing crises in Colombia, the Democratic Republic of the Congo, Sudan, the Occupied Palestinian Territory and Lebanon, underscoring the scale of concurrent emergencies aid agencies are managing ([OCHA, 12 Aug](https://www.unocha.org/news/todays-top-news-colombia-occupied-palestinian-territory-democratic-republic-congo-west-africa)).

In Gaza, OCHA reports a 33 percent increase in the volume of supplies the UN and partners were able to bring in during July compared with June, though military operations continue to put civilians at risk and humanitarian needs remain severe ([OCHA](https://www.unocha.org/news/todays-top-news-colombia-occupied-palestinian-territory-democratic-republic-congo-west-africa)).

In eastern DRC's North Kivu, insecurity continues to disrupt humanitarian operations, including the response to an ongoing Ebola outbreak, as the six-month July–December roadmap for the peace process struggles to translate into improved access on the ground ([OCHA](https://www.unocha.org/news/todays-top-news-colombia-occupied-palestinian-territory-democratic-republic-congo-west-africa); [UN News](https://news.un.org/en/story/2026/02/1166899)).

## 🌐 Tier 3 — Rest of the World
Colombia's earthquake death toll has risen to 273, with 3,284 injured and roughly 496 people still reported missing, four days after the magnitude 7.4 quake struck near San José del Palmar in Chocó department on 10 August. Officials also reported roughly 12,600 homes destroyed and more than 74,800 damaged, with authorities separately monitoring the Puracé volcano for signs of triggered activity ([Local10/AP, 13 Aug](https://www.local10.com/news/world/2026/08/13/earthquake-in-colombia-at-least-273-dead-3284-injured/); [ColombiaOne, 13 Aug](https://colombiaone.com/2026/08/13/colombia-earthquake-death-toll-hits-265-with-496-still-missing/)).

The IFRC and Colombian Red Cross remain in full nationwide response mode, running search-and-rescue, evacuation, first aid and needs assessments across affected departments, while the UN and bilateral donors including the US ($15.5 million) continue to scale up shelter, health, food and protection assistance ([IFRC](https://www.ifrc.org/press-release/colombia-earthquake-red-cross-mobilizes-nationwide-response-saving-lives-top-priority); [US State Department](https://www.state.gov/releases/office-of-the-spokesperson/2026/08/united-states-mobilizes-life-saving-response-to-colombia-earthquake/)).

## 🚨 Sudden Onset Hazards
Guatemala's Fuego volcano remains under close watch after its early-August eruptive phase; a Copernicus EMS rapid-mapping activation (EMSR912) was triggered on 13 August at 12:20 UTC to support ongoing monitoring and damage assessment ([GDACS/Copernicus EMS](https://rapidmapping.emergency.copernicus.eu/EMSR912/download)).

Colombia's magnitude 7.4 earthquake (10 August) continues to generate aftershocks and has prompted precautionary monitoring of the Puracé volcano; a Copernicus EMS activation (EMSR916) and UNITAR-UNOSAT damage assessment remain active for the affected region ([GDACS/Copernicus EMS](https://rapidmapping.emergency.copernicus.eu/EMSR916/download)).

A magnitude 5.6 earthquake was recorded in the Kermadec Islands Region on 13 August at 16:11 UTC; a magnitude 6.0 event also struck the South Sandwich Islands Region on 12 August. Neither has triggered a red alert ([GDACS](http://gdacs.org/report.aspx?eventid=1557839&episodeid=1724872&eventtype=EQ)).

Five tropical cyclones (Peilou, Nangka, Cristobal, One-C and Hernan) were active across Pacific and Indian Ocean basins as of 13 August, alongside continuing flood alerts in China, Myanmar, India and Vietnam ([GDACS](https://gdacs.org/)).

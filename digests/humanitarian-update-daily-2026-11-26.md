## 🇦🇺 Australian Priority Alerts

**Australia–Solomon Islands treaty.** Solomon Islands has signed a sweeping new treaty with Australia that will ensure there is "no foreign military or security base" in the island nation, per RNZ Pacific (25 Nov, 12:57 Sydney). Only the summary line was available. [RNZ Pacific](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/pacific/2771868/solomon-islands-australia-sign-new-treaty-to-keep-foreign-military-bases-out)

**ACFID's Weekly (25 Nov, 12:02 Sydney).** ACFID flags a speech by Foreign Minister Wong on 24 Nov outlining DFAT priorities into 2027, and expects the reports from the parliamentary inquiries into Australia's international development program and conflict prevention and into gender equality as a national and economic security imperative. It describes COP31's outcome as disappointing for Australian and Pacific civil society, since no final agreement was reached. It also notes the new DFAT First Assistant Secretary for the Humanitarian and Civil Society Division (Anita Dwyer) and the ICJ finding that Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya, with nearly 1.2 million Rohingya refugees still in Bangladesh. [ACFID The Weekly](https://acfid.asn.au/the_weekly/wednesday-25-11-2026/)

## 🌍 Tier 2 — Middle East & Africa

**Yemen.** UN Relief Chief Tom Fletcher arrived in Yemen on 25 Nov. He said renewed fighting has displaced over 364,000 people since August, including tens of thousands in the past week, that hospitals and first responders are under attack, and that the response is at breaking point from underfunding. He demanded release of detained UN staff and access to Taiz. [OCHA](https://www.unocha.org/news/un-relief-chief-we-will-not-allow-yemens-people-slip-worlds-radar) The HALO Trust reports landmine and explosive-hazard casualties in Yemen rose to 98 in October, over five times the 2026 monthly average; two incidents caused 34 civilian casualties. [ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/yemen/sharp-spike-landmine-casualties-renewed-violence-intensifies-yemen) Al Jazeera asks whether renewed strikes on Hodeidah port could worsen the crisis (headline and summary only). [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/25/could-renewed-strikes-on-hodeidah-port-worsen-yemens-humanitarian-crisis)

**Somalia.** International Medical Corps' Baidoa situation report #2 says the conflict has damaged civilian infrastructure, displaced residents and disrupted healthcare. [ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/somalia/conflict-response-baidoa-somalia-situation-report-2-november-25-2026)

**DR Congo Ebola (Bundibugyo).** UNICEF reports nearly 6 in 10 surveyed women and adolescent girls in Ituri report new or increased violence since the outbreak began ([ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/democratic-republic-congo/casi-6-de-cada-10-mujeres-y-ninas-adolescentes-encuestadas-denuncian-un-aumento-de-la-violencia-y-los-abusos-en-ituri-provincia-afectada-por-el-ebola)). Gavi has signed an advance purchase agreement for up to 1.5 million doses of a candidate vaccine (ChAdOx1 BDBV), subject to trial results ([ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/democratic-republic-congo/gavi-signe-un-accord-avec-le-serum-institute-india-afin-de-garantir-un-acces-rapide-aux-vaccins-contre-le-virus-ebola-bundibugyo)).

**Cameroon cholera.** IMC reports 3,038 cases and 86 deaths across the Far North Region as of 9 Nov; Minawao refugee camp has had no new cases for 29 days, but the regional outbreak is not yet declared over. [ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/cameroon/cameroon-cholera-outbreak-situation-report-8-november-25-2026)

**Egypt.** At least 30 killed and 15 injured when a vehicle carrying petroleum products caught fire, per the health ministry. [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/25/at-least-30-killed-in-egypt-vehicle-fire-health-ministry-says)

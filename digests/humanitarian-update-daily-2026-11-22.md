## 🌏 Tier 1 — Pacific & Asia-Pacific
The Cook Islands government says the state of emergency for Nassau remains in force as Tropical Cyclone Xavier continues to weaken. All 84 residents of Nassau are safe and sheltering in two safe houses; the island has coastal damage from swells but no building damage has been reported so far. In Pukapuka residents are in emergency centres and some seawall damage has been reported on the south-eastern coast, and officials are following up on food supply concerns in Manihiki and Rakahanga. Xavier is moving away from Cook Islands waters to the west-southwest, but strong wind warnings remain in force for Suwarrow, Pukapuka, Nassau, Manihiki and Rakahanga ([RNZ Pacific](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/pacific/2726758/everyone-on-nassau-is-safe-says-cook-islands-pm)).

Earlier RNZ reporting says Xavier was downgraded to a category 1 cyclone and that the government is preparing relief. A New Zealand Air Force C-130 was due to bring humanitarian supplies to Rarotonga, to be loaded onto the vessel Kukupa for the Northern group, with the barge Taunga Nui expected to depart for Pukapuka and Nassau after the weekend once conditions allow. The loss of Pukapuka's traditional wale pola landmark was also reported ([RNZ Pacific](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/pacific/2719268/cook-islands-pm-says-relief-supplies-on-standby-as-tc-xavier-weakens)).

Pacific representatives delivered a stark warning over fossil fuels at COP31 (headline only) ([RNZ Pacific](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/pacific/2709824/stark-warning-over-fossil-fuels-from-pacific-at-cop31)).

Japan is providing coastal radar systems and drones to the Philippines to bolster Manila's coastal defence ([Indo-Pacific Defense Forum](https://ipdefenseforum.com/2026/11/japan-to-provide-coastal-radar-drones-to-philippines/)).

## 🌍 Tier 2 — Middle East & Africa
WFP's Palestine external situation report (reporting period 1–31 October 2026) says displaced families in Gaza face a fourth winter in tents. About 1.4 million people, two-thirds of the population, are acutely food insecure, and funding shortfalls have forced WFP to cut monthly food assistance by one-third in Gaza and by half in the West Bank ([ReliefWeb/WFP](https://reliefweb.int/report/occupied-palestinian-territory/wfp-palestine-external-situation-report-21-november-reporting-period-1-31-october-2026)). Al Jazeera reports an Israeli strike on a food aid vehicle in Gaza that killed three people (video item, headline only) ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/video/newsfeed/2026/11/21/21-11-sv-israel-targets-gaza-food-truck)), and carries a "Jerusalem Daily" segment on deadly strikes and a health crisis in Gaza (headline only) ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/video/newsfeed/2026/11/21/jerusalem-daily-gaza-faces-deadly-strikes-and-health-crisis)). An Education Cluster assessment covered 962 learning spaces across Gaza's five governorates (21 May–12 August 2026), reporting 581,111 enrolments and 15,709 teachers ([ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/occupied-palestinian-territory/gaza-strip-learning-spaces-assessment-report-november-2026)).

OCHA's Lebanon Inter-Agency Response Update (15 November) says about 1,200 IDPs left collective shelters in the first two weeks of the month amid limited alternatives, and that planned shelter closures continue. It also cites an IPC analysis showing 1.05 million people in crisis or worse levels of food insecurity, with further deterioration projected in 2027 ([ReliefWeb/OCHA](https://reliefweb.int/report/lebanon/lebanon-inter-agency-response-update-15-november-2026)).

FAO's Yemen Market and Trade Bulletin for October 2026 says roughly 18.7 million people (over half the population) face acute food insecurity (IPC Phase 3 or worse), with conditions deteriorating amid renewed frontline conflict since July 2026 ([ReliefWeb/FAO](https://reliefweb.int/report/yemen/yemen-market-and-trade-bulletin-october-2026-issued-21st-november-2026)).

In DR Congo, a joint Education and Protection needs assessment reports a 12.5% dropout rate in the 2025–26 school year, linked mainly to household socio-economic hardship, forced displacement, domestic work and early marriage and pregnancy ([ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/democratic-republic-congo/evaluation-conjointe-des-besoins-en-matiere-deducation-et-protection-rdc-2026)).

Al Jazeera reports two tankers struck by unidentified projectiles near Oman in the Strait of Hormuz on Saturday, as Iran continues to restrict shipping, per the UK Maritime Trade Operations centre ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/21/tankers-hit-in-strait-of-hormuz-as-iran-tightens-grip-on-shipping)).

## 🌐 Tier 3 — Rest of the World
A Russian attack on Kryvyi Rih, Ukraine, on Friday night killed two people (a seven-year-old boy and a 20-year-old woman) and wounded 60, including six children, according to local officials. About 25 apartment buildings, a hospital, schools and businesses were damaged, and further missiles reportedly hit rescuers, medics and utility crews who had arrived ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/21/russian-strike-kills-two-wounds-60-in-zelenskyys-hometown)). Al Jazeera also carries a video item on a deadly attack in Kyiv (headline only) ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/video/newsfeed/2026/11/21/aje-onl-nf_deadly-attack-in-kyiv-as-russia-launches-barrage-211126)).

The ICRC has renewed its call for legally binding rules on autonomous weapons after the failure to launch negotiations, in a statement prepared for the Seventh Review Conference of the Convention on Certain Conventional Weapons (16–20 November 2026) ([ICRC](https://www.icrc.org/en/statement/icrc-renews-urgent-call-legally-binding-rules-autonomous-weapons)).

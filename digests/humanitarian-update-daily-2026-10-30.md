## 🌏 Tier 1 — Pacific & Asia-Pacific

The Pacific Cyclone Watch Service has been activated ahead of the South Pacific cyclone season, which officially begins on 1 November and runs to the end of April 2027 ([RNZ Pacific](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/pacific/2231511/cyclone-watch-service-activated-as-south-pacific-cyclone-season-begins)). No further detail was in the fetched feed text.

In the Northern Marianas, tourism and business leaders warn that the United States' suspension of visa-free travel for Chinese tourists could further damage the territory's struggling economy ([RNZ Pacific](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/pacific/2231512/northern-marianas-tourism-industry-warns-us-travel-suspension-could-deepen-economic-crisis)).

The IFRC has published the final report for its Cook Islands dengue DREF operation. A dengue outbreak was declared on 22 May 2025 and spread across Rarotonga, Aitutaki, Atiu, Mauke and Mangaia; the Cook Islands recorded 1,251 cumulative cases, with 1,250 people making a full recovery ([ReliefWeb / IFRC](https://reliefweb.int/report/cook-islands/cook-islands-dengue-outbreak-dref-final-report-mdrck003)).

In Southeast Asia, Singapore and Indonesia say they are ready to address bilateral issues including transboundary haze and energy supplies, after several weeks of growing tension ([The Diplomat](https://thediplomat.com/2026/10/singapore-indonesia-ready-to-address-bilateral-issues-including-haze-minister-says/)).

## 🌍 Tier 2 — Middle East & Africa

Gaza: Israeli strikes continued despite the "ceasefire", and Gaza's Health Ministry says dozens have died awaiting medical treatment abroad while it urges the opening of Rafah ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/29/israeli-strikes-hit-gaza-as-health-ministry-urges-rafah-opening?traffic_source=rss)). The New Humanitarian carries an opinion piece by Nour ElAssy on WFP's ration cuts in Gaza, arguing the funding exists but political will is missing ([The New Humanitarian](https://www.thenewhumanitarian.org/opinion/2026/10/29/who-will-answer-wfps-ration-cuts-gaza-and-winter-misery-ahead)).

Yemen: Human Rights Watch says a long-running crisis in Taizz has drastically worsened since October 2026, when Houthi forces fully encircled the city and blocked and attacked the remaining major supply route, causing severe shortages of fuel, food, water and medicine and restricting humanitarian access ([ReliefWeb / HRW](https://reliefweb.int/report/yemen/yemen-houthis-depriving-taizz-civilians-food-medicine)).

Ethiopia/Kenya: IOM says about 50,000 people have crossed into northern Kenya after violent clashes in Ethiopia. Kenya and inter-agency partners are planning a response for up to 80,000 people ([ReliefWeb / IOM](https://reliefweb.int/report/kenya/50000-people-cross-kenya-fleeing-violence-ethiopia-iom-says)).

Ebola: WHO field stories describe the response to the Bundibugyo virus outbreak in DRC, now in its eighth province according to WFP's latest briefing, and the response in Uganda's Ntoroko District, where a family that crossed from DRC was placed under 21-day home quarantine ([WHO via ReliefWeb, Uganda](https://reliefweb.int/report/uganda/frontlines-ugandas-ebola-response-how-who-supported-district-health-teams-keep-communities-safe); [WHO via ReliefWeb, DRC](https://reliefweb.int/report/democratic-republic-congo/ebola-democratic-republic-congo-niania-dialogue-transforms-mistrust-community-engagement)).

Sudan: a UAE court sentenced eight people to life over arms supplies to Sudan's army, amid tensions between Khartoum and Abu Dhabi over the war and the RSF ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/29/uae-sentences-eight-people-to-life-over-arms-supplies-to-sudans-army?traffic_source=rss)).

## 🌐 Tier 3 — Rest of the World

Haiti: the Norwegian Refugee Council's Secretary General Jan Egeland condemned the killing of two NRC colleagues, Bonaparte Février and Richard Alexandre, in Port-au-Prince on 28 October ([NRC via ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/haiti/statement-killing-nrc-aid-workers-haiti-nrc)). WFP says food insecurity in Haiti is on track to reach record highs ([WFP via ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/haiti/en-haiti-la-faim-atteint-des-niveaux-records-et-les-alertes-du-pam-deviennent-realite-world-food-programme)).

Ukraine: a Russian drone strike killed at least 14 people at Dnipro's main railway station ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/video/newsfeed/2026/10/29/aje-onl-nf_russian-drone-strike-at-dnipro-train-platform-291026?traffic_source=rss)).

The ICRC has issued its call to COP31 for stronger climate action and finance to reach people in conflict settings ([ICRC](https://www.icrc.org/en/article/cop31-icrc-call-strengthen-climate-action-and-finance)). OCHA's daily briefing covers Ukraine, the occupied Palestinian territory, DRC and Haiti ([OCHA](https://www.unocha.org/news/todays-top-news-ukraine-occupied-palestinian-territory-democratic-republic-congo-haiti)).

Panama: the European Commission published a daily map for the 7.7 magnitude earthquake of 9 October (map only; no detail in fetched text) ([ReliefWeb / DG ECHO](https://reliefweb.int/map/panama/panama-77-m-earthquake-9-october-dg-echo-daily-map-29102026)).

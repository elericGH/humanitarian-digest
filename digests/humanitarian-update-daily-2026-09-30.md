# ⚡ Daily Humanitarian Update — Wednesday, 30 September 2026
*Produced Wednesday, 30 September 2026 | Australian Interest Priority Edition*

## 🇦🇺 Australian Priority Alerts

**Nepal flood response expanded to AUD11 million.** The ANU Development Policy Centre's September aid roundup, published this morning, highlights the government's additional AUD3 million and deployment of specialist drone operators to support Nepal's recovery from the August flood disaster, taking Australia's total contribution to AUD11 million. Nepal estimates reconstruction at USD4-5 billion and has requested USD20 million from the new global loss and damage facility, which has yet to disburse funds. ([Devpolicy Blog](https://devpolicy.org/sept-2026-aid-news-20260930/))

**Solomon Islands treaty package and Myanmar oversight.** The same roundup notes Australia will link AUD980 million over ten years to finalising a bilateral treaty with Solomon Islands, including AUD142 million for the free education program and AUD68 million for MPs' local development priorities, with open questions about whether funds come from within the existing aid budget. It also reports the government has rejected a Senate committee recommendation to give parliament regular updates on its Myanmar response, including humanitarian programs. ([Devpolicy Blog](https://devpolicy.org/sept-2026-aid-news-20260930/))

**Sector funding context for Australian NGOs.** Devpolicy draws attention to ALNAP's State of the Humanitarian System 2026 report, which finds international humanitarian funding fell 30% from its 2022 peak as major donors cut support, and that barriers to channelling aid through local actors persist. A joint OECD/UNDP report also finds the share of aid recorded on partner-country budgets has declined since 2014. ([Devpolicy Blog](https://devpolicy.org/sept-2026-aid-news-20260930/))

## 🌏 Tier 1 — Pacific & Asia-Pacific

**Pacific leaders press Indonesia on West Papua access.** Pacific Islands leaders used their UN General Assembly addresses to urge Indonesia to allow the UN High Commissioner for Human Rights to visit West Papua. Solomon Islands Prime Minister Matthew Wale said reports of rights violations "must be independently verified, not left in dispute", and Marshall Islands President Hilda Heine backed the Pacific Islands Forum position. Melanesian Spearhead Group members have said Indonesia's associate membership will be reviewed if no visit occurs before their 2027 summit. ([Asia Pacific Report](https://asiapacificreport.nz/2026/09/29/pacific-leaders-urge-jakarta-to-allow-un-rights-visit-to-west-papua/))

**Fiji HIV emergency and Pacific health pressures.** Devpolicy's roundup flags Fiji's declaration of its HIV outbreak as a national emergency, with one in 60 adults now living with HIV and reported cases up 27% in a year; HIV rates are also rising in Solomon Islands and PNG. It also notes new Pacific pledges at the Pacific Islands Forum in Palau, including almost AUD30 million from Australia for SPREP, a Pacific Renewable Energy Commissioner and ocean economies work, and GBP10 million from the UK to the Pacific Resilience Facility. ([Devpolicy Blog](https://devpolicy.org/sept-2026-aid-news-20260930/))

## 🌍 Tier 2 — Middle East & Africa

**Yemen: health system near breaking point.** WHO warned on 29 September that renewed fighting is fuelling fears of cholera and measles spread, with several lifesaving supply lines at zero stock in its Aden warehouse, including trauma kits and essential medicines. Nine health facilities have been damaged or attacked near Taiz, Lahj and Hudaydah, emergency obstetric care is suspended at two hospitals, and more than 180,000 people have been displaced. Authorities in Aden report more than 4,481 casualties since 6 August. Djibouti has received more than 3,600 Yemenis since 10 September, with 5,000 to 10,000 more arrivals anticipated. ([UN News](https://news.un.org/en/story/2026/09/1168480))

**DRC: Ebola confirmed cases pass 8,000.** WHO reported 8,067 confirmed cases and 3,901 deaths (case fatality above 48%) across 63 health zones in seven provinces in DRC's largest-ever Ebola outbreak, caused by the Bundibugyo virus. Treatment capacity has grown to more than 1,600 beds across 50 centres, but many people still die at home before reaching care and oxygen supply remains unreliable. ([UN News](https://news.un.org/en/story/2026/09/1168481))

**UNHCR funding cuts hit Chad, Ethiopia and Sudan.** UNHCR's new report details that 319,000 refugees in Chad face registration delays, GBV safe houses in Ethiopia have fallen from 29 to 13 in a year, and more than 50,000 survivors of sexual violence in Darfur and Kordofan could lose access to post-rape care. ([UN News](https://news.un.org/en/story/2026/09/1168486))

## 🌐 Tier 3 — Rest of the World

**UNHCR: 8.3 million at risk of losing assistance.** UNHCR's report *Protection Eroded: The Human Cost of Underfunding*, launched in Geneva on 29 September, warns that nearly 8.3 million refugees, displaced and stateless people are on the brink of losing vital assistance. Funding fell from USD5.18 billion to USD3.93 billion between 2024 and 2025, and by July UNHCR had received only 32% of the USD8.5 billion needed for 2026. In Afghanistan, where more than a million people have returned this year, arrival cash grants have been cut from USD375 per person to USD170 per household. ([UN News](https://news.un.org/en/story/2026/09/1168486); [UN Geneva briefing](https://www.unognewsroom.org/story/en/3281/un-geneva-press-briefing-29-september-2026))

## 🚨 Sudden Onset Hazards

**Hurricane Polo, Mexico.** Polo made landfall in Baja California Sur as a Category 2 hurricane near Las Barrancas late on 28 September (local time), bringing 6 to 12 inches of rain, toppled trees and coastal flooding, before making a second landfall in Sonora on 29 September. The US Consulate General in Hermosillo suspended services due to expected flooding and road closures, and Polo's remnants are driving flood watches across 11 US states. ([PBS News](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/science/hurricane-polo-makes-landfall-in-baja-california-toppling-trees-and-flooding-coastal-communities); [UPI](https://www.upi.com/Top_News/World-News/2026/09/29/latam-mexico-hurricaner-polo/1651790707059); [US Consulate Hermosillo](https://mx.usembassy.gov/weather-alert-suspension-of-in-person-routine-and-emergency-services-u-s-consulate-general-hermosillo-september-29-2026/))

No other active sudden-onset hazards of note were identified in the Asia-Pacific in this period.

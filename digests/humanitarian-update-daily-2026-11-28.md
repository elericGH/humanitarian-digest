## 🌏 Tier 1 — Pacific & Asia-Pacific
**PNG parliament nears Bougainville vote.** RNZ Pacific reports PNG MPs are expected to vote on Wednesday 2 December on whether to ratify the result of Bougainville's 2019 non-binding referendum, in which 97.7 percent voted for independence. A three-quarters majority is required, and the deputy prime minister has urged all MPs to attend and not abstain. [RNZ Pacific](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/pacific/2825651/png-parliament-nears-crucial-vote-on-bougainville-s-future)

**PNG has the Pacific's largest HIV epidemic.** A new UNAIDS report cited by RNZ estimates 110,000 people living with HIV in Papua New Guinea (1.5 percent adult prevalence). Pacific-wide, new infections are up 65 percent since 2010; Fiji's new infections rose roughly twelvefold to an estimated 1,700 in 2025, and increases in diagnoses in Palau, Solomon Islands and Vanuatu were flagged. [RNZ Pacific](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/pacific/2815173/png-has-largest-hiv-epidemic-in-the-pacific-report)

**Oceania epidemic alerts map (headline only).** A map of epidemic and emerging disease alerts in Oceania dated 24 November was posted on ReliefWeb. [ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/map/world/carte-des-alertes-de-maladies-epidemiques-et-emergentes-en-oceanie-24-novembre-2026)

## 🌍 Tier 2 — Middle East & Africa
**Yemen: aid response "at breaking point".** OCHA reports Emergency Relief Coordinator Tom Fletcher ended a two-day Yemen mission on 26 November. Nearly 380,000 people have been displaced since August, and the UN Human Rights Office has verified nearly 350 civilian casualties (95 killed, 252 injured), half of those killed being children. He called for funding, civilian protection, access to Ta'iz and release of detained UN and aid personnel. [OCHA](https://www.unocha.org/news/todays-top-news-yemen-democratic-republic-congo-0) Al Jazeera separately carries a headline that Mecca pact allies condemned Houthi attacks on Saudi Arabia (headline only). [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/27/mecca-pact-allies-condemn-heinous-houthi-attacks-on-saudi-arabia?traffic_source=rss)

**DR Congo: violence disrupting aid and Ebola response.** OCHA says more than 10,000 people displaced by attacks in Djugu territory, Ituri, have reached Bunia over the past week, with intensified fighting in North and South Kivu; displacement and access limits are disrupting the Ebola response. [OCHA](https://www.unocha.org/news/todays-top-news-yemen-democratic-republic-congo-0)

**Sudan: Blue Nile displacement.** A Sudan Protection Cluster alert dated 27 November says hostilities since early November have displaced about 47,460 people, with a further 22,467 reported pending verification, amid explosive-ordnance contamination and constrained access. [ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/sudan/protection-cluster-alert-escalating-conflict-mass-displacement-and-protection-risks-blue-nile-state-27-november-2026)

**FAO–WFP Hunger Hotspots.** El Niño has added Southern Africa and the Central American Dry Corridor to the list, now 16 hotspots (up from 13). Sudan, South Sudan, Somalia, parts of Nigeria and Yemen remain of highest concern, with famine risk in parts of Sudan and South Sudan; up to 3 million people in Zimbabwe, 2.6 million in Malawi and 1.8 million in Mozambique are projected to face high acute food insecurity. [ReliefWeb (WFP/FAO)](https://reliefweb.int/report/world/hunger-hotspots-fao-wfp-early-warnings-acute-food-insecurity-december-2026-may-2027-outlook)

**Gaza.** Al Jazeera reports Israeli attacks killed four Palestinians on Friday despite the ceasefire agreed over a year ago, and that a son of Hamas leader Khalil al-Hayya survived a strike. [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/27/israel-kills-four-in-gaza-as-hamas-chief-al-hayyas-son-survives-strike) Also headline only: funeral prayers for 31 victims of an Egypt tanker crash. [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/video/newsfeed/2026/11/27/funeral-prayers-held-for-31-victims-of-egypt-tanker-crash?traffic_source=rss)

**The New Humanitarian (headline only):** "Houthi escalation, Israeli threats, and the USAID Files: The Cheat Sheet." [TNH](https://www.thenewhumanitarian.org/news/2026/11/27/houthi-escalation-israeli-threats-usaid-files-cheat-sheet)

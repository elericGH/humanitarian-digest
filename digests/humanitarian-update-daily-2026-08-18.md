## 🌏 Tier 1 — Pacific & Asia-Pacific

Indonesia's death toll from Saturday's magnitude 7.7 Flores earthquake continued climbing, with thousands still awaiting aid on the country's Independence Day. Al Jazeera reports rescue work is ongoing as the confirmed toll approaches 70, with about 12,800 people displaced ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/17/thousands-await-aid-after-deadly-indonesia-quake-as-rescue-work-conducted)). See the Sudden Onset Hazards section below for full details.

Pacific leaders are finalising preparations for the 55th Pacific Islands Forum Leaders Meeting, to be hosted in Palau from 30 August to 4 September under the theme "B.E.L.A.U." Regional divisions over how to respond to China's July missile test, and Taiwan's expected participation despite Beijing's objections, are shaping the lead-up ([PINA/Island Times](https://pina.com.fj/2026/08/17/pif-2026-palau-prepares-for-a-pacific-meeting-shaped-by-regional-divisions/)).

Pacific geoscientists and disaster managers wrapped a week of meetings in Honiara (10–14 August) aimed at securing durable, non-donor-cycle funding for the Oceania Regional Seismic Network (ORSNET), which links more than 50 seismic stations across the region to underpin earthquake monitoring and tsunami early warning ([Islands Business/PACNEWS](https://islandsbusiness.com/pacnews/pacnews-three-17-august-2026/)).

Metro Manila and surrounding Luzon provinces remained under Red Warning heavy-rainfall alerts on 17 August as the Southwest Monsoon (Habagat) continued to trigger flooding on top of the earlier Kujira/Dolphin weather system, with several roads submerged ([Philippine Daily Inquirer](https://newsinfo.inquirer.net/2285861/list-flooded-metro-manila-roads-on-monday-aug-17)).

Papua New Guinea nationals will begin serving in the Australian Defence Force under a new bilateral arrangement, a development with direct implications for Australia-Pacific security cooperation ([PINA](https://pina.com.fj/2026/08/17/papua-new-guineans-to-start-serving-in-australian-defence-force/)).

## 🌍 Tier 2 — Middle East & Africa

UN High Commissioner for Human Rights Volker Türk warned that Yemen risks a return to full-scale conflict, expressing alarm at the rising civilian toll from escalating Houthi strikes and urging restraint from all parties ([OHCHR](https://www.ohchr.org/en/press-releases/2026/08/yemen-turk-alarmed-civilian-casualties-hostilities-escalate-urges-restraint); [Euronews](https://www.euronews.com/2026/08/17/yemen-risks-return-to-full-scale-conflict-if-houthi-strikes-continue-un-rights-chief-warns)).

The Ebola outbreak in eastern Democratic Republic of the Congo has become the deadliest in the country's history, surpassing the 2018-2020 outbreak's toll, with the virus reported to be killing roughly one person every 30 minutes across six provinces ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/17/ebola-outbreak-in-dr-congo-becomes-deadliest-in-countrys-history)).

## 🚨 Sudden Onset Hazards

Indonesia's magnitude 7.7 earthquake off Flores Island (East Nusa Tenggara), which struck 15 August, is the region's dominant active hazard. The confirmed death toll has climbed past 54 toward 68, with roughly 12,800–13,000 people displaced, over 1,300 homes damaged (around 250 destroyed), and nearly 1,600 aftershocks recorded. Initial tsunami warnings for East Nusa Tenggara, West Nusa Tenggara, South Sulawesi and Southeast Sulawesi have since been lifted. More than 3,500 military and police personnel are deployed, though logistics are slowing aid delivery to hard-hit communities ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/17/thousands-await-aid-after-deadly-indonesia-quake-as-rescue-work-conducted); [ABC News Australia](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-08-15/earthquake-strikes-off-coast-of-indonesia/107041680)).

The Philippines remains under active Red Warning heavy-rainfall alerts (Bataan, Metro Manila, Zambales, Pampanga) from the enhanced Southwest Monsoon as of 17 August, continuing the flood impact already affecting roughly 586,000 people this month ([Inquirer](https://newsinfo.inquirer.net/2285861/list-flooded-metro-manila-roads-on-monday-aug-17)).

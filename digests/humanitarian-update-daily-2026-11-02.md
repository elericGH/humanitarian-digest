## 🌍 Tier 2 — Middle East & Africa
Al Jazeera reports Israeli forces have maintained a siege on Hebron in the occupied West Bank for a 10th consecutive day, imposed after an attack on 23 October near Hebron in which two Israeli soldiers were killed. The municipality says more than 3,300 Palestinians have been displaced or affected, and the mayor puts daily economic losses above 12 million shekels (US$3.8m) with 15 homes and other structures demolished: [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/1/hebron-siege-continues-for-10th-day-as-israel-threatens-wider-offensive).

OCHA's Somalia Monthly Humanitarian Update for October (posted 1 November) says at least 175,000 people have been affected by El Niño-linked heavy rains and flash floods. At least 3.4 million people are projected to face flood risks from October to December, with Hiraan, Middle Juba and Lower Juba most exposed. Partners activated a floods anticipatory action plan backed by US$7 million from CERF and US$1 million from the Somalia Humanitarian Fund. The update also notes renewed conflict and insecurity in Bay region causing new displacement: [ReliefWeb/OCHA](https://reliefweb.int/report/somalia/somalia-monthly-humanitarian-update-october-2026).

UNHCR's Operational Data Portal lists new items dated 1 November: a map of new displacement due to escalation on the West Coast (Yemen situation) and "UNHCR Flash Update #1 – Blue Nile State" (headlines only): [UNHCR Data Portal](https://data.unhcr.org/).

## 🌐 Tier 3 — Rest of the World
WFP is providing emergency food assistance to more than 400 people in Kyiv who left Oleshky, in Ukraine's Kherson region, during a brief localised ceasefire. Many arrived at medical facilities on 31 October after months of severe shortages of food and medical supplies. WFP is providing hot meals with its partner Iskra Dobra for an initial two weeks and calls for safe, sustained humanitarian access: [WFP](https://www.wfp.org/news/world-food-programme-ground-providing-emergency-food-assistance-oleshky-evacuees-ukraine).

Al Jazeera carries video of a house collapsing into a swollen river in Ecuador, where torrential rains and floods linked to El Niño have hit parts of the country (video item only, no further detail): [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/video/newsfeed/2026/11/1/video-captures-house-collapsing-due-to-ecuador-floods).

## 🌏 Tier 1 — Pacific & Asia-Pacific
Pacific meteorological leaders opened the Eighth Pacific Meteorological Council (PMC-8) in Tonga, with Deputy Prime Minister Dr Viliami Uasike Latu calling for early-warning and multi-hazard systems to be properly financed and maintained long after donor-funded projects end, pointing to Tonga's repeated exposure to cyclones, droughts, volcanic eruptions and tsunamis. ([PINA](https://pina.com.fj/2026/09/11/pacific-needs-stronger-early-warnings-as-climate-risks-grow/), [Islands Business](https://islandsbusiness.com/news-break/no-community-too-remote-for-an-effective-warning-tongas-deputy-pm/))

In Suva, Fiji's Acting Attorney-General Siromi Turaga opened the Pacific Exchange on Transnational Organised Crime and Correction Conference, warning that criminal networks are exploiting the region's borders, financial systems and institutions, and urging Pacific governments to share intelligence and coordinate corrections and justice responses rather than act in isolation. ([Islands Business](https://islandsbusiness.com/news-break/pacific-justice-leaders-urged-to-unite-against-organised-crime/))

The second Pacific Conference on Health and Climate Change closed in Nadi, Fiji (9–11 September), where representatives from Fiji, Vanuatu, Solomon Islands, Wallis and Futuna, and New Caledonia shared adaptation measures including reforestation, mangrove restoration, agroforestry and water security work aimed at reducing climate-driven health risks. ([Islands Business — PACNEWS ONE, 11 September 2026](https://islandsbusiness.com/pacnews/pacnews-one-11-september-2026/))

## 🌍 Tier 2 — Middle East & Africa
More than 46,000 people have been displaced in Yemen as fighting between Houthi forces and government-aligned troops escalates across Taiz, Hodeidah, Marib, al-Jawf and al-Bayda provinces, with over 11,400 households driven from their homes since 3 September. The UN's migration agency (IOM) says the displacement toll is rising "by the hour" and is calling for safe humanitarian access. ([Al Jazeera, 11 September 2026](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/9/11/at-least-46000-displaced-by-escalating-yemen-fighting-un))

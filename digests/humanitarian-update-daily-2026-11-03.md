## 🌏 Tier 1 — Pacific & Asia-Pacific
**Nepal flood recovery.** The Association of Medical Doctors of Asia (AMDA) reports that, following severe flooding and landslides near the Nepal–China border on 26 August 2026, it has dispatched two aid workers, including its president, from Japan to Nepal as of 2 November to assess medium- and long-term recovery support ([ReliefWeb/AMDA](https://reliefweb.int/report/nepal/amda-emergency-relief-2-flash-floods-nepal-2-november-2026)). A UN/OCHA Rasuwa flood situation report (No. 12, as of 30 October) was also published ([ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/nepal/nepal-rasuwa-flood-situation-report-no-12-30-october-2026), headline only).

**Bangladesh / Myanmar refugees.** Qatar Red Crescent Society, with the Bangladesh Red Crescent Society, has launched a shelter project for refugees in Bangladesh ([ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/bangladesh/qrcs-launches-shelter-project-refugees-bangladesh-enar)).

**Afghans in Pakistan.** UNHCR published findings on the protection needs of Afghans remaining in Pakistan, titled "Staying because return is not safe" ([ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/pakistan/staying-because-return-not-safe-protection-needs-afghans-remaining-pakistan-i-ad-hoc-participatory-assessment-findings-and-recommendations-july-2026-assessment-period-27-)). Headline only.

**Regional disasters.** The ASEAN Coordinating Centre for Humanitarian Assistance published its Weekly Disaster Update for Week 44, 26 October – 1 November 2026, covering Indonesia, Malaysia, Thailand and Viet Nam ([ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/indonesia/asean-weekly-disaster-update-week-44-26-october-1-november-2026), headline only).

**Pacific politics.** RNZ reports that Fiji's PM Rabuka has delayed the vote on the Constitution Bill after mounting pressure ([RNZ](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/pacific/2301684/fiji-pm-rabuka-delays-vote-on-constitution-bill-after-mounting-pressure)). The Fiji military commander has urged accountability amid the coup immunity debate ([RNZ](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/pacific/2307642/fiji-military-chief-urges-accountability-to-the-people-amid-coup-immunity-debate)). In Myanmar, The Diplomat reports the new "civilian" government is angered by an unflattering media profile ([The Diplomat](https://thediplomat.com/2026/11/fallout-from-an-interview-in-naypyidaw/)), and Myanmar remains on the FATF blacklist ([The Diplomat](https://thediplomat.com/2026/11/myanmar-remains-on-fatf-blacklist-due-to-money-laundering-concerns/)).

## 🌍 Tier 2 — Middle East & Africa
**Yemen.** HALO Trust warns that improvised explosive devices disguised as rocks, bricks and thermos flasks are an emerging threat to civilians. It says escalating conflict since August 2026 has driven large-scale displacement and new mine-laying along transport routes and access corridors ([ReliefWeb/HALO Trust](https://reliefweb.int/report/yemen/ieds-disguised-rocks-bricks-and-thermos-flasks-pose-new-threat-civilians-yemen)). UNHCR mapped new displacement from escalation on the west coast as of 31 October ([ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/map/yemen/map-new-displacement-due-escalation-west-coast-yemen-situation-31-oct-2026), headline only). OCHA's daily briefing covers Yemen, Ukraine, Sudan, the occupied Palestinian territory and insurance ([OCHA](https://www.unocha.org/news/todays-top-news-yemen-ukraine-sudan-occupied-palestinian-territory-insurance), headline only).

**DR Congo.** MSF says the de facto authorities in North Kivu (the FRN1, formerly AFC/M23) are imposing restrictions that obstruct care for sexual violence survivors, and that agreed corrective measures have not been implemented ([ReliefWeb/MSF](https://reliefweb.int/report/democratic-republic-congo/north-kivu-drc-harmful-restrictive-measures-obstruct-care-sexual-violence-survivors)). MSF also says no vaccine is licensed specifically for the Bundibugyo Ebola outbreak in DRC, with cases recently reported in Kenya and Nigeria ([ReliefWeb/MSF](https://reliefweb.int/report/democratic-republic-congo/vaccines-2026-bundibugyo-ebola-disease-outbreak-access-gaps-and-recommendations)).

**Sudan.** Al Jazeera reports drone attacks extending a campaign targeting Sudan's power system, with the army reporting new defections from the RSF ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/2/drone-attacks-extend-campaign-targeting-sudans-power-system)).

**Ethiopia.** WHO says violence in northern Ethiopia is pushing the health system towards collapse. Renewed fighting between Tigray rebels and the government has shattered the 2022 peace deal ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/2/violence-in-northern-ethiopia-pushing-health-system-towards-collapse-who)). A Kenya–Ethiopia Moyale emergency inter-agency situation report (#4) was also published ([ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/kenya/moyale-emergency-inter-agency-situation-report-4), headline only).

**Occupied Palestinian territory / region.** Israeli forces ended their lockdown of Hebron in the West Bank ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/video/newsfeed/2026/11/2/jerusalem-daily-israels-lockdown-of-hebron-comes-to-an-end)). Displaced families are returning to damaged homes ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/2/displaced-in-hebron-picking-up-pieces-after-israeli-siege)). Separately, Iran says it is pushing an accelerated plan for ceasefire talks with the US ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/2/iran-pushing-accelerated-plan-for-ceasefire-talks-with-us)).

**Other.** HRW calls on Zambia to halt a post-election crackdown ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/2/hrw-calls-on-zambia-to-halt-post-election-crackdown)). UNHCR published a Syria protection factsheet for Q3 2026 ([ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/syrian-arab-republic/unhcr-syria-protection-factsheet-q3-2026), headline only).

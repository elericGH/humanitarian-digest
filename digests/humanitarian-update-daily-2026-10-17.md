## 🌏 Tier 1 — Pacific & Asia-Pacific
**Cook Islands cyclone alert.** The Cook Islands Metservice warned that tropical depression TD01F could reach category 1 strength within 24 hours while tracking towards the low-lying atoll of Suwarrow ([RNZ Pacific](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/pacific/1954087/cyclone-alert-in-force-for-cook-islands-as-tropical-depression-intensifies)). Cook Islanders have been urged to prepare ([RNZ Pacific](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/pacific/1958250/cook-islanders-urged-to-be-prepared-for-potential-cyclone)). GDACS lists the system (ONE-26) at Orange level; see Hazards below.

**Marianas/Guam storm preparations.** Guam has been placed in "Condition of Readiness 3" as a tropical storm moves towards the Mariana Islands ([RNZ Pacific](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/pacific/1958607/guam-declares-condition-of-readiness-3-as-tropical-depression-approaches)). RNZ also reports that Tropical Storm Champi is another typhoon threat to the Marianas (headline only) ([RNZ Pacific](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/pacific/1950994/tropical-storm-champi-another-typhoon-threatens-marianas)).

**Indonesia fires and haze.** Haze and smoke from large wildfires in Indonesia, including West Papua, have caused flight disruptions and respiratory problems ([RNZ Pacific](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/pacific/1950440/haze-over-papua-from-forest-fires-causes-mass-disruptions-health-issues)). The Diplomat reports the Indonesian president has replaced the head of the national disaster management agency amid growing scrutiny of the response to forest and peatland fires that have drastically reduced air quality across the region ([The Diplomat](https://thediplomat.com/2026/10/indonesian-president-replaces-head-of-disaster-management-agency/)).

**Cambodia floods.** A Humanitarian Response Forum situation report (DanChurchAid, WFP) says that as of 5 October flooding had affected 109,985 families across 10 provinces, with 9,027 families evacuated, 88,811 houses affected and 313 schools impacted ([ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/cambodia/humanitarian-response-forum-hrf-situation-report-no-2-floods-cambodia-2026)). Separately, The Diplomat runs an analysis on the collapse of Cambodia's NGO sector (headline and standfirst only) ([The Diplomat](https://thediplomat.com/2026/10/cambodias-ngo-sector-is-collapsing-does-it-matter/)).

**Nepal.** UNICEF's situation report no. 7 (13 October) cites government figures of 1,458 deaths and 5,285 people missing, including 195 children, and says UNICEF-supported mental health and psychosocial support reached 16,216 people ([ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/nepal/unicef-nepal-humanitarian-situation-report-no-7-13-october-2026)).

**Other.** Myanmar has set up a new committee to advance normalisation with ASEAN ahead of next month's summit in Manila ([The Diplomat](https://thediplomat.com/2026/10/myanmar-establishes-new-committee-to-advance-normalization-with-asean/)). PNG police have seized more high-grade guns, with calls for criminal groups to be declared terrorists (headline only) ([RNZ Pacific](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/pacific/1952484/call-for-criminal-groups-to-be-declared-terrorists-as-png-police-seize-more-high-grade-guns)). In Fiji, a chief says the coup immunity debate must address inequality, drugs and the HIV crisis ([RNZ Pacific](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/pacific/1949414/fijian-chief-warns-coup-immunity-debate-must-address-inequality-and-social-issues)).

## 🌍 Tier 2 — Middle East & Africa
**Yemen.** UNFPA warns that escalating hostilities in Taiz have severed access to life-saving care for more than 147,600 women and girls of childbearing age ([ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/yemen/escalating-violence-puts-147000-women-and-girls-city-taiz-grave-risk-unfpa-warns)).

**Gaza.** Al Jazeera reports commercial goods such as fresh fruit, vegetables, meat and winter clothing have not entered Gaza for two days ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/video/newsfeed/2026/10/16/food-affordability-fears-plague-gaza-as-israel-halts-commercial-goods)), and that Gaza's Health Ministry describes malnutrition and starvation as "catastrophic", with about 67 percent of the population projected to face crisis-level food insecurity or worse through the end of 2026 ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/16/gaza-malnutrition-and-starvation-at-catastrophic-levels-health-ministry)).

**Kenya Ebola.** Kenya has recorded a third Bundibugyo-strain Ebola case, a 65-year-old woman in quarantine linked to the first patient who died; Nairobi reports no signs of community transmission ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/16/kenya-reports-third-bundibugyo-strain-ebola-case)).

**Sudan, Nigeria, Horn of Africa.** IFRC mourns the killing of a Sudanese Red Crescent volunteer in Blue Nile State on 13 October ([ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/sudan/ifrc-mourns-killing-sudanese-red-crescent-volunteer-blue-nile-state)). ICRC says its supported health facilities in Nigeria have seen an 18 per cent rise in malnutrition cases ([ICRC](https://www.icrc.org/en/news-release/nigeria-icrc-supported-health-facilities-see-rise-malnutrition-cases)). The New Humanitarian's weekly cheat sheet leads with Ethiopia-Eritrea war fears (headline only) ([TNH](https://www.thenewhumanitarian.org/news/2026/10/16/ethiopia-eritrea-war-fears-gaza-base-blocked-kabila-new-coalition-cheat-sheet)). New OCHA South Sudan products were published (headline only) ([ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/south-sudan/south-sudan-humanitarian-update-15-october-2026)).

**Aid worker protection.** The UN relief chief called for concrete action to reverse the collapse in protection for aid workers at an international conference (headline only) ([OCHA](https://www.unocha.org/news/un-relief-chief-calls-concrete-action-reverse-collapse-protection-aid-workers)).

## 🌐 Tier 3 — Rest of the World
**Panama earthquake.** UNICEF's situation report no. 3 (12–13 October) says 619 schools are affected, including 35 with severe damage, according to the Education Ministry ([ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/panama/unicef-panama-situation-report-no-3-earthquake-12-13-october-2026)).

**Ukraine.** ICRC features letters from families to prisoners of war (headline only) ([ICRC](https://www.icrc.org/en/article/letters-families-prisoners-war)).

**Food security.** On World Food Day WFP promoted innovation and early action as shocks to the global food system mount ([ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/world/world-food-day-wfp-promotes-innovation-and-early-action-shocks-global-food-system-mount)). WFP published a Jamaica food security monitoring report, "Ten Months On" from Hurricane Melissa (headline only) ([ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/jamaica/jamaica-food-security-monitoring-impact-hurricane-melissa-ten-months)).

**Health and displacement.** The US has recorded 4,410 measles cases as the outbreak grows ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/16/us-records-4410-measles-cases-as-outbreak-grows)). UNHCR Pakistan published figures on voluntary repatriation of Afghan refugees, 1 January to 30 September 2026 (headline only) ([ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/pakistan/unhcr-pakistan-voluntary-repatriation-afghan-refugees-1-january-30-september-2026)).

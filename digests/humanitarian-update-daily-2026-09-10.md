# ⚡ Daily Humanitarian Update — Thursday, 10 September 2026

## 🇦🇺 Australian Priority Alerts

Australia and Solomon Islands have reached an in-principle agreement on a sweeping new bilateral treaty, with Canberra committing close to A$980 million in support, including funds for constituency development, free education and visa/security arrangements. Pacific Minister Pat Conroy and Solomon Islands ministers Rick Hou and Peter Kenilorea Jnr are continuing negotiations, with a signing date not yet confirmed. [ABC News](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-09-07/australia-strikes-agreement-with-solomon-islands-over-new-treaty/107125924)

Analysts note the treaty is widely read as part of Australia's contest with China for influence in the Pacific, and comes on top of the A$600 million Australia pledged (alongside US$150 million from the United States) on 2 September toward a package helping Pacific nations manage the fallout of the global fuel and energy crisis. [ABC News](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-09-08/solomon-islands-australia-china-pacific-contest/107127740) | [Islands Business — PACNEWS](https://islandsbusiness.com/pacnews/pacnews-two-wednesday-9-september-2026/)

DFAT's 2026–27 Official Development Assistance Budget Summary confirms Australia's humanitarian assistance envelope for the year at an estimated $742 million within a total $5.209 billion ODA budget, with continued emphasis on Indo-Pacific disaster preparedness, health security and climate resilience. [DFAT](https://www.dfat.gov.au/about-us/corporate/portfolio-budget-statements/australias-official-development-assistance-budget-summary-2026-27)

## 🌏 Tier 1 — Pacific & Asia-Pacific

The UN's IOM and UNHCR remain concerned about the ongoing Rohingya maritime crisis off Myanmar's coast, with nearly 300 people reported missing or dead in the Andaman Sea and Bay of Bengal so far in 2026, following the loss of two vessels carrying more than 500 people earlier this year. Departure rates in 2026 remain consistent with prior crisis years. [UNHCR](https://www.unhcr.org/us/news/press-releases/iom-and-unhcr-deeply-concerned-reports-maritime-incidents-myanmar-coast-over)

A new food security analysis from Bangladesh's Ministry of Food, UN agencies and partners projects acute food insecurity will climb from 15.3 million people (May–August 2026) to 18.1 million by the September–December period, including 787,000 people in IPC Phase 4 Emergency, driven by climate shocks, flooding and inflation. [ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/bangladesh/more-15-million-people-facing-acute-food-insecurity-bangladesh-ministry-food-un-agencies-and-partners-launch-new-national-food-security-analysis)

## 🌍 Tier 2 — Middle East & Africa

Sudan's civil war continues to escalate: the Sudanese army seized the town of Hamrat al-Wiz in North Kordofan from the RSF on 6 September, tightening control of the strategic Export Road, while fighting has expanded around Kurmuk and Geisan in Blue Nile state near the Ethiopian border. More than 33.7 million people need humanitarian assistance and 11.8 million remain displaced. The UN Security Council is due to vote before 12 September on extending Sudan's sanctions regime. [Security Council Report](https://www.securitycouncilreport.org/monthly-forecast/2026-09/sudan-41.php)

WFP has halved food assistance in the West Bank because of critical funding shortfalls, and says it urgently needs an additional US$386 million over the next six months to serve roughly two million food-insecure people across Gaza and the West Bank. In Gaza, several health facilities in Gaza City closed in September, cutting off hundreds of thousands of people from lifesaving medical services as the rainy season approaches for 2.1 million people in overcrowded displacement sites. [UN News](https://news.un.org/en/story/2026/09/1168237)

## 🌐 Tier 3 — Rest of the World

Dengue continues to surge across the Americas, with more than 11.7 million cases reported as of 9 September — more than double the 2023 total — and all four dengue serotypes now circulating in the region simultaneously. [ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/updates)

Unprecedented monsoon rains across Nigeria, Mali, Niger and DRC have deepened an education crisis, damaging schools, displacing communities away from education facilities, and prompting occupation of school buildings by internally displaced people. [ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/updates)

## 🚨 Sudden Onset Hazards

No active sudden-onset hazards of note were confirmed via GDACS or Pacific Disaster Center at the time of writing.

## 🌏 Tier 1 — Pacific & Asia-Pacific
A Myanmar military airstrike on a displacement camp in Rakhine state killed 12 people, including a five-year-old child, and wounded 18 others, according to rescuers cited by Al Jazeera ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/video/newsfeed/2026/11/14/myanmar-airstrike-displacement-camp-rakhine)).

Authorities in India's Assam have demolished the homes of 132 Muslim families, citing illegal encroachment on government land. Residents say they settled there after fleeing the 1983 anti-Muslim riots, and researchers say the evictions have disproportionately targeted Bengali-origin Muslims ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/video/newsfeed/2026/11/14/14-11-sv-assam-muslim-families-evicted-demolition-drive)).

At the COP31 climate summit in Antalya, Türkiye, Marshall Islands President Hilda Heine told delegates that "every fraction of a degree we warm brings a catastrophe". A new UN report cited by RNZ says warming beyond 1.5°C is now inevitable and overshoot must be kept as small and short as possible ([RNZ Pacific](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/pacific/2591454/cop31-hears-1-point-5-degreesc-overshoot-not-an-option-for-pacific)).

Bangladesh and Sri Lanka are expanding partnerships to strengthen maritime domain awareness and combat illicit activity at sea (headline and summary only) ([Indo-Pacific Defense Forum](https://ipdefenseforum.com/2026/11/bangladesh-sri-lanka-expand-partnerships-to-strengthen-maritime-security/)).

## 🌍 Tier 2 — Middle East & Africa
The EU has urged Israel to reopen all Gaza border crossings and grant "immediate, unimpeded access" for aid groups, following devastating flooding in Gaza this week. The statement from the office of EU High Representative Kaja Kallas said civilian infrastructure and medical facilities "must be protected". An Israeli air attack on the Nasser Medical Complex on Thursday killed at least six Palestinians, including a surgeon, and an Israeli drone strike in Jabaliya killed two more on Saturday ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/14/eu-calls-for-immediate-humanitarian-access-to-gaza)).

A car bomb in Jaramana, near Damascus, killed at least two people, a man and a girl. Security forces cordoned off the area and opened an investigation ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/video/newsfeed/2026/11/14/deadly-car-bomb-blast-kills-two-near-syrias-capital)).

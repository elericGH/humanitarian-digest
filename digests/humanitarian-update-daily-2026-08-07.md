## 🇦🇺 Australian Priority Alerts

The Australian Council for International Development (ACFID) launched its **State of the Sector Report 2026** on 6 August, the sector's most comprehensive comparable snapshot of Australian development NGOs in nearly a decade, covering 36 indicators and 10 characteristics. The report was launched by Andrew Leigh and highlights that inflation has pushed up delivery costs while real public donations sit around 20% below 2015 levels. [ACFID — State of the Sector 2026](https://stateofthesector.acfid.asn.au/acknowledgements/)

Separately, ACFID's analysis of Australia's international development budget notes it has reached its highest-ever dollar figure at $5.1 billion, even as ODA as a share of GNI and of the Federal Budget sits at its lowest level on record — a gap the sector is flagging ahead of any further disaster response calls this cyclone season. [ACFID Federal Budget Analysis](https://acfid.asn.au/acfids-analysis-of-the-2025-2026-federal-budget/)

## 🌏 Tier 1 — Pacific & Asia-Pacific

Super Typhoon Dolphin passed roughly 131km from Naha, Okinawa, on the afternoon/evening of 7 August after regaining Category 4-equivalent strength, with violent winds, extreme wave heights and storm surge forecast to persist across the Okinawa and Amami island chains through around 9 August. [Weathernews — Typhoon Dolphin Update](https://wxtech.weathernews.com/en/news/20260805-01/)

Dolphin is operating in tandem with Tropical Storm Kujira (locally "Maymay" in the Philippines), which finished crossing Northern Luzon on 6 August before weakening over the Philippine Sea. The combined systems, plus a separate low-pressure area (Invest 96W), are driving widespread heavy monsoon rain across the Philippines, Palau, Micronesia, the Marianas and the Marshall Islands. [PDC Daily Tropical Cyclone Tracker](https://www.pdc.org/weather-wall/)

## 🌍 Tier 2 — Middle East & Africa

Eight Arab and Muslim-majority countries issued a joint statement on 6 August delivering their "strongest condemnation and denunciation" of ongoing Israeli violations in Gaza, with Palestinians reporting that continued strikes and aid restrictions have turned the October 2025 ceasefire into what they describe as "an illusion." ## 🚨 Sudden Onset Hazards

**Super Typhoon Dolphin (Western Pacific):** Passed near Okinawa's main island on 7 August at Category 4-equivalent strength (945 hPa, sustained winds ~85kt, gusts to 120kt at last advisory), tracking toward the East China Sea. Violent winds, dangerous seas and storm surge continue to threaten Okinawa and Amami through 9 August. [Weathernews Typhoon Dolphin Tracker](https://wxtech.weathernews.com/en/news/20260805-01/) | [Al Jazeera Tracker](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/4/typhoon-dolphin-hurtles-towards-japan-tracker-updates-and-what-to-expect)

## 🌏 Tier 1 — Pacific & Asia-Pacific

**Tonga drought response.** An environmental NGO, Live 'n Learn Tonga, supplied water tanks to 47 households on the Ha'apai islands of Mo'unga'one, Felemea, 'Uiha and Lofanga last week; a second phase over the next week will reach six more communities and about 120 households. RNZ reports Tonga's government declared a 27-day state of emergency last week over the drought, which is being felt across Tonga and several neighbouring Pacific Island countries and is linked to El Niño. [RNZ Pacific](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/pacific/2754221/tonga-ngo-supplies-water-tanks-to-drought-hit-ha-apai-households)

**Pacific disease alerts.** The Pacific Community published its epidemic and emerging disease alerts map for Oceania (17 November), covering Australia, French Polynesia, Marshall Islands, New Zealand, Samoa, Vanuatu and Wallis and Futuna (map only; no further detail reviewed). [ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/map/world/carte-des-alertes-de-maladies-epidemiques-et-emergentes-en-oceanie-17-novembre-2026)

**COP31 fails; Pacific reaction.** RNZ reports the Pacific push for stronger climate action failed at the UN climate talks, which collapsed after countries could not agree on language on fossil fuels. [RNZ Pacific](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/pacific/2746441/negotiation-in-bad-faith-pacific-push-for-stronger-climate-action-fails-at-un-climate-talks) Plan International says COP31 ended without agreement, leaving girls facing worsening climate impacts. [ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/world/cop31-ends-without-agreement-leaving-girls-facing-worsening-climate-impacts)

**Rohingya and the ICJ ruling.** The New Humanitarian reports Rohingya refugees welcomed the landmark genocide ruling; it raises hopes for over a million people in Bangladesh camps, but civil war and denied citizenship still block their return. [The New Humanitarian](https://www.thenewhumanitarian.org/news-feature/2026/11/23/rohingya-refugees-welcome-momentous-genocide-ruling-ask-when-they-can-go) The Diplomat says ASEAN's response to the 19 November ICJ judgment against Myanmar has been muted. [The Diplomat](https://thediplomat.com/2026/11/how-asean-responded-to-the-icjs-myanmar-genocide-ruling/)

**Nepal and the wider region.** India announced a $1.6bn (NPR 240bn) development cooperation package for Nepal's reconstruction after August's floods, which killed or left missing thousands of people. [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/23/india-pledges-1-6bn-in-flood-relief-for-nepal) Al Jazeera also reports Bangladesh garment workers rejected a pay offer (headline only). [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/23/protesting-bangladesh-garment-workers-reject-pay-offer) The ASEAN weekly disaster update for 16–22 November (Indonesia, Malaysia, Thailand) was published (document not reviewed). [ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/indonesia/asean-weekly-disaster-update-week-47-16-22-november-2026) The Diplomat notes Thailand will ban exports of mining chemicals believed responsible for cross-border river contamination. [The Diplomat](https://thediplomat.com/2026/11/thailand-to-ban-export-of-mining-chemicals-believed-responsible-for-crossborder-river-contamination/)

## 🌍 Tier 2 — Middle East & Africa

**Gaza.** Israeli air strikes killed at least three people in Gaza on Monday, including UNRWA worker Adel al-Helo, hit near a water well in Jabalia camp; three others were wounded. A later strike on a vehicle in western Gaza City killed two, per medics. [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/23/israeli-drone-strike-kills-unrwa-worker-in-gaza) The ICRC describes work with communities preparing for winter rains after early-November rain flooded tents in southern Gaza. [ICRC](https://www.icrc.org/en/article/gaza-working-alongside-communities-prepare-winter-rains)

**Lebanon.** Medair says that nine months after renewed escalation, more than 300,000 people remain displaced outside their areas of origin and about 11,000 are in collective shelters; despite a 20 June ceasefire, more than 6,000 attacks have been recorded. [ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/lebanon/nine-months-humanitarian-emergency-lebanon-far-over)

**Yemen and Syria.** OCHA's daily briefing covers Yemen among other crises. [OCHA](https://www.unocha.org/news/todays-top-news-ukraine-yemen-sudan-occupied-palestinian-territory) OCHA published Syria Humanitarian Update No. 8 for September–October (headline only). [ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/syrian-arab-republic/syrian-arab-republic-humanitarian-update-no-8-september-october-2026-enar) UNHCR published a map of new displacement from the escalation on Yemen's west coast, dated 19 November (headline only). [ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/map/yemen/map-new-displacement-due-escalation-west-coast-yemen-situation-19-nov-2026)

**DR Congo.** MSF says clashes between FRN and Wazalendo armed groups have intensified in Nyabiondo, Masisi territory, since 18 November; a 15-year-old with bullet wounds died awaiting a blocked referral. [ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/democratic-republic-congo/msf-drc-child-dies-while-awaiting-medical-evacuation-intensified-clashes-disrupt-access-care-nyabiondo-masisi-territory)

**South Sudan and Mali.** MSF suspended all activities in Yei River County for at least three months from 17 November after a staff member and a Ministry of Health midwife were abducted on 12 November (both released unharmed 14 November); it also had to suspend Ebola preparedness support. [ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/south-sudan/msf-south-sudan-abduction-staff-members-forces-msf-suspend-all-activities-yei-river-county) In Mali, MSF condemned 21 November attacks near Niafounké and in Niono; two people died, including a health worker, and five people were wounded inside Niono hospital. [ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/mali/msf-mali-attacks-msf-strongly-condemns-deadly-attacks-against-civilians-and-health-facility)

**Sudan/Chad, Malawi, Nigeria.** UNHCR posted an update on arrivals from Sudan to Chad (22 November; headline only). [ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/chad/core-emergency-situation-chad-update-arrivals-sudan-22-nov-2026) The New Humanitarian reports 2.6 million hungry Malawians face drought and aid cuts ahead of the lean season. [The New Humanitarian](https://www.thenewhumanitarian.org/news-feature/2026/11/23/malawi-fight-drought-aid-cuts-did-cop31-provide-answers) OCHA features clean water access in north-east Nigeria (human-interest piece). [OCHA](https://www.unocha.org/news/my-children-slept-thirsty-bringing-clean-water-closer-people-north-east-nigeria)

## 🌐 Tier 3 — Rest of the World

**Ukraine.** OCHA reports nearly 30 civilians killed and 240 injured between Friday and Monday morning, including 14 killed in strikes on a residential building in Dymer and a supermarket in Vyshneve (Kyiv region). A strike in Kryvyi Rih killed a seven-year-old boy and a paramedic and injured 60. Attacks in Odesa left some 80,000 households and businesses without heat. [OCHA](https://www.unocha.org/news/todays-top-news-ukraine-yemen-sudan-occupied-palestinian-territory)

**Global.** Insecurity Insight published its bi-monthly Attacks on Health Care news brief for 1–15 November (headline only). [ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/world/attacks-health-care-bi-monthly-news-brief-01-15-november-2026) OCHA published a Colombia humanitarian trends and impact report for January–August 2026 (headline only). [ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/colombia/colombia-informe-de-tendencias-e-impacto-humanitario-en-2026-datos-acumulados-entre-enero-agosto-de-2026-fecha-de-publicacion-23-de-noviembre-de-2026)

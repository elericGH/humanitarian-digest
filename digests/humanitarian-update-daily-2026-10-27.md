## 🌏 Tier 1 — Pacific & Asia-Pacific
Papua New Guinea is feeling the full force of El Niño, with impacts reported from the Highlands to the coastal provinces. RNZ Pacific says the prolonged dry spell is escalating into a national humanitarian concern affecting water, food, health and livelihoods, and that bushfires are a growing risk. Provincial Disaster Offices across the Highlands have been urged to carry out rapid assessments and provide immediate relief (26 Oct, 13:05 Sydney). ([RNZ Pacific](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/pacific/2168659/png-communities-stuggle-with-el-nino-drought))

In Cambodia, World Vision's Situation Report #4 (25 Oct) says floodwaters have receded in some areas but flooding continues to affect vulnerable communities, with food shortages, lost livelihoods and damaged household assets. An estimated 171,448 people, including 33,669 children, are affected across its response areas; World Vision had reached 11,578 people with food, WASH, hygiene supplies and shelter support. ([ReliefWeb / World Vision](https://reliefweb.int/report/cambodia/world-vision-situation-report-4-humanitarian-response-flooding-25-october-2026))

Other items (headline only): ASEAN Weekly Disaster Update, Week 43 (19–25 Oct 2026) ([ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/thailand/asean-weekly-disaster-update-week-43-19-25-october-2026)); WFP Sri Lanka Country Brief, October 2026 ([ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/sri-lanka/wfp-sri-lanka-country-brief-october-2026)); The Diplomat, "Myanmar War Crimes Survivors Look to Denmark for Justice" ([The Diplomat](https://thediplomat.com/2026/10/myanmar-war-crimes-survivors-look-to-denmark-for-justice/)).

## 🌍 Tier 2 — Middle East & Africa
The UN says the humanitarian situation in Sudan remains devastating one year after the fall of El Fasher. OCHA reports nearly 900,000 displaced people sheltering in Tawila locality with inadequate food, water, sanitation, shelter and healthcare, and that Sudan's roughly US$2.9 billion response plan is just over 46 per cent funded. Drone strikes reportedly hit power substations in Gedaref State and fuel and transport stations in North Kordofan. ([OCHA](https://www.unocha.org/news/todays-top-news-yemen-occupied-palestinian-territory-sudan-democratic-republic-congo)) Separately, the UK sanctioned 15 Sudanese commanders and officials from both sides over war crimes during the fall of El Fasher, including sexual violence, torture and attacks on civilians, and pledged humanitarian aid and survivor support ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/26/britain-sanctions-sudanese-commanders-over-weaponised-rape)). The New Humanitarian also ran a first-person piece, "Remembering El Fasher" (headline only) ([TNH](https://www.thenewhumanitarian.org/opinion/first-person/2026/10/26/remembering-el-fasher-the-city-i-knew-atrocities-changed-it)).

In the West Bank, OCHA says partners are supporting more than 3,000 Palestinians evacuated from their homes in Hebron by Israeli forces after last week's shooting attack on Israeli soldiers, per the Hebron Municipality. Around 700 schools serving 250,000 students in Hebron governorate have shifted to remote learning, and over 2,000 settler attacks causing casualties or property damage have been recorded in 2026, more than in any previous year on record ([OCHA](https://www.unocha.org/news/todays-top-news-yemen-occupied-palestinian-territory-sudan-democratic-republic-congo)). Oxfam called the operation collective punishment that is destroying livelihoods during the olive harvest ([ReliefWeb / Oxfam](https://reliefweb.int/report/occupied-palestinian-territory/reaction-israeli-large-scale-military-operation-and-mass-displacement-hebron-oxfam-international)). The UN-mandated Commission of Inquiry on the OPT reported that Israel has committed crimes against humanity in the West Bank, including extermination; Israel's mission in Geneva rejected the report ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/26/israel-committing-crimes-against-humanity-in-occupied-west-bank-un)).

In Gaza, OCHA reports tents set alight in Ash Shuja'iyyeh overnight, leaving nine households without shelter, after a previous fire displaced about 200 families; partners are providing tents, tarpaulins, blankets and hygiene kits ([OCHA](https://www.unocha.org/news/todays-top-news-yemen-occupied-palestinian-territory-sudan-democratic-republic-congo)). A joint AIDA and PNGO statement on Israeli restrictions on engine oil in Gaza was also published (headline only) ([ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/occupied-palestinian-territory/joint-aida-pngo-statement-gaza-israeli-restrictions-engine-oil-are-crippling-humanitarian-respo)).

In Yemen, OCHA says more than 280,000 people have been displaced since the start of August, almost half from Ta'iz, where medicine, fuel and water are short and cholera cases continue to be reported nationwide. The UN and partners have reached over 200,000 people with emergency aid ([OCHA](https://www.unocha.org/news/todays-top-news-yemen-occupied-palestinian-territory-sudan-democratic-republic-congo)). Also published (headline only): MSF, "Children in Al-Bayda still face severe malnutrition and limited access to care" ([ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/yemen/msf-yemen-children-al-bayda-still-face-severe-malnutrition-and-limited-access-care)).

In the Democratic Republic of the Congo, the UN Senior Ebola Coordinator condemned the killing of a nurse working on the Ebola response in Bafwasende, Tshopo Province, on 24 Oct; a health centre in Kisangani was also looted. National authorities report confirmed cases approaching 10,000 ([OCHA](https://www.unocha.org/news/todays-top-news-yemen-occupied-palestinian-territory-sudan-democratic-republic-congo); [ReliefWeb statement](https://reliefweb.int/report/democratic-republic-congo/statement-senior-ebola-coordinator-julien-harneis-25-october-2026-enfr)).

Algeria's president has ordered troops to neighbouring Niger to protect shared energy and economic interests, after parliament approved the deployment in an emergency session; the presidency says Niger's authorities requested the assistance ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/26/algerias-president-orders-troop-deployment-to-neighbouring-niger)). Also (headline only): Guterres warns of 'unimaginable' consequences of Iran-Gulf escalation ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/video/newsfeed/2026/10/26/guterres-warns-iran-gulf-escalation-west-bank)).

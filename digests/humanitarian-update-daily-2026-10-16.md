## 🌏 Tier 1 — Pacific & Asia-Pacific
**Cook Islands cyclone alert.** The Cook Islands Meteorological Service has put a tropical cyclone alert in force as tropical depression TD01F intensifies. The system is moving south towards the Southern Cook Islands and may become a category 1 cyclone within 24 hours. It is forecast to pass close to Suwarrow in the next 24–36 hours, with damaging gale-force winds possible from tonight ([RNZ Pacific](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/pacific/1954087/cyclone-alert-in-force-for-cook-islands-as-tropical-depression-intensifies)).

**Typhoon threat to the Marianas.** Tropical Storm Champi is forecast to strengthen into a category 2 typhoon and pass over or near Guam on Sunday, with a typhoon watch in effect for Guam, Rota, Tinian and Saipan. The region is still recovering from earlier typhoons, and the American Red Cross says repeated storms have stretched response capacity ([RNZ Pacific](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/pacific/1950994/tropical-storm-champi-another-typhoon-threatens-marianas)).

**Haze from Indonesian and Papua fires.** Smoke from large wildfires in Indonesia, including West Papua, has caused flight disruptions and respiratory problems and is also affecting Papua New Guinea. RNZ links the fires to land clearance and prolonged dry conditions from an intensifying El Niño ([RNZ Pacific](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/pacific/1950440/haze-over-papua-from-forest-fires-causes-mass-disruptions-health-issues)).

**Afghanistan food insecurity.** A new IPC analysis from FAO and WFP projects about 19.2 million people (38% of the population) in high acute food insecurity during the November–April lean season, up from 17.4 million a year earlier. About five million are expected to face emergency levels of hunger, and malnutrition cases are projected to rise to about four million ([ReliefWeb / FAO-WFP](https://reliefweb.int/report/afghanistan/food-insecurity-and-child-malnutrition-worsen-afghanistan-despite-strong-agricultural-season)).

**Myanmar and Pakistan.** The Diplomat reports on daily survival for displaced people in an IDP camp in Myanmar (headline only) ([The Diplomat](https://thediplomat.com/2026/10/one-day-at-a-time-inside-an-idp-camp-in-myanmar-survival-is-a-daily-challenge/)). In Peshawar, Pakistan, thousands attended funeral prayers after a school roof collapse killed at least 34 children and a teacher and injured more than 50; police attributed it to weak infrastructure ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/video/newsfeed/2026/10/16/peshawar-mourns-children-killed-in-school-roof-collapse)). ReliefWeb also carries a satellite-detected mudflow map for Gorkha District, Nepal (headline only) ([ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/map/nepal/satellite-detected-mudflow-11-october-2026-gorkha-district-gandaki-province-nepal-imagery-analysis-11102026-published-15102026-v1)).

## 🌍 Tier 2 — Middle East & Africa
**Yemen: most serious escalation since the 2022 truce.** The UN Special Representative for Children and Armed Conflict warns that heightened hostilities put children at serious risk. The UN has documented more than 60 grave violations against children since 3 September 2026, at least 200,000 people have been displaced, and access constraints are impeding aid in Houthi-controlled areas ([ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/yemen/yemen-heightened-hostilities-put-children-serious-risk-grave-violations-and-threaten-hard-won-gains)). Al Jazeera's live coverage reports Saudi Arabia calling Houthi attacks "war crimes" (headline only) ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/liveblog/2026/10/16/iran-war-live-saudi-blasts-houthi-war-crimes-us-warns-on-saudi-airports)), and a Conflict Escalation in Yemen Situation Report #7 was published (headline only) ([ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/yemen/conflict-escalation-yemen-situation-report-7-reporting-period-3-11-october-2026-issued-13-october-2026)).

**Gaza: commercial goods halted.** COGAT announced a halt to commercial goods entering Gaza, citing a tax dispute between local merchants and Hamas. Hamas's media office denied the claims. The halt affects food and goods for the 2.1 million people who depend on Israel's border crossings in a territory still suffering famine ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/15/israel-halts-commercial-goods-from-entering-gaza-hamas-denies-tax-claims)). The New Humanitarian carries a first-person piece from ten Palestinians on life in Gaza, headlined "There is no ceasefire here" (headline only) ([The New Humanitarian](https://www.thenewhumanitarian.org/opinion/first-person/2026/10/15/there-no-ceasefire-here-ten-palestinians-life-gaza-today)).

**Ethiopia and DRC.** OCHA reports that many hospitals in Ethiopia's Tigray Region are operational again, including Ayder Hospital in Mekelle, as more aid arrives, though significant gaps remain and security is volatile in parts of Amhara, Afar and Tigray. In eastern DRC, recent hostilities have displaced more than 26,000 people in North and South Kivu, and four civilians were killed near Pimbo in Ituri, forcing the suspension of traffic on a key corridor and the Ebola response ([OCHA](https://www.unocha.org/news/todays-top-news-ethiopia-democratic-republic-congo-yemen-occupied-palestinian-territory-panama)).

**Other Africa and Middle East items.** ReliefWeb lists a dengue epidemic notice for five provinces of Chad ([ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/chad/tcd-epidemie-10-2026-epidemie-de-dengue-dans-5-provinces-du-tchad-2026-10-15)), a WHO Sudan Health Spotlight for September ([ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/sudan/who-sudan-sudan-health-spotlight-september-2026)) and a South Sudan Humanitarian Access Snapshot for September ([ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/south-sudan/south-sudan-humanitarian-access-snapshot-september-2026)) (all headline only). Japan is supporting WFP food assistance for 94,000 conflict-affected people in northern Mozambique ([ReliefWeb / WFP](https://reliefweb.int/report/mozambique/japan-supports-food-assistance-94000-conflict-affected-people-northern-mozambique-world-food-programme)). Somalia says it will hold elections in 2027 ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/15/somalia-says-it-will-hold-elections-in-2027)).

## 🌐 Tier 3 — Rest of the World
**Istanbul floods.** At least three people have died and several are missing after torrential rain flooded homes, businesses and streets across Istanbul, with power and water cuts and a large clean-up under way ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/video/newsfeed/2026/10/15/several-people-dead-or-missing-after-istanbul-floods)). The Red Cross and Red Crescent have issued a flood notice for the Marmara Region ([ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/turkiye/tur-flood-10-2026-marmara-region-2026-10-15)).

**Black Sea.** At least seven crew members are missing after drone attacks on two chemical tankers (headline only) ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/video/newsfeed/2026/10/15/at-least-7-crew-members-missing-after-drone-attacks-on-ships-in-black-sea)).

**Ukraine and Haiti.** WFP published its Ukraine Country Brief for October 2026 ([ReliefWeb / WFP](https://reliefweb.int/report/ukraine/wfp-ukraine-country-brief-october-2026-enuk)), and FEWS NET reports dry conditions persisting in southern Haiti ([ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/haiti/haiti-seasonal-monitor-dry-conditions-persist-southern-haiti-october-15-2026)) (both headline only).

## 🌏 Tier 1 — Pacific & Asia-Pacific
**Tonga declares state of emergency over El Niño drought.** Tonga's government has declared a 27-day state of emergency, effective from 11am on 19 November until 11am on 16 December, covering all land and sea areas. Tonga declared drought across most of its islands last month, with El Niño effects expected to persist until at least June next year. The government is working on immediate measures to support affected communities and will review the declaration before 16 December. ([RNZ Pacific](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/pacific/2683569/a-natural-hazard-tonga-declares-state-of-emergency-as-el-nino-drought-worsens))

**ICJ finds Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya.** The International Court of Justice ruled on Thursday that Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya, with nine of the 15 judges concurring, and voted in favour of a landmark reparations order. Al Jazeera reports it is the first time the court has ruled that a state committed genocide. Rohingya refugees in Bangladesh's Cox's Bazar camps gathered ahead of the verdict. ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/19/what-the-icj-ruling-means-for-the-rohingya))

**Myanmar's military-backed government to "review" its ASEAN relationship.** Per The Diplomat's summary, Min Aung Hlaing's comment came after the 11-nation bloc decided to maintain the bar on Myanmar's participation. ([The Diplomat](https://thediplomat.com/2026/11/myanmars-military-backed-government-to-review-relationship-with-asean/))

**Sri Lanka and Timor-Leste (headline only).** WFP Sri Lanka Country Brief, November 2026 (headline only) ([ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/sri-lanka/wfp-sri-lanka-country-brief-november-2026)); Timor-Leste's ASEAN membership strengthens regional security, diplomacy (headline only) ([IPDF](https://ipdefenseforum.com/2026/11/timor-lestes-asean-membership-strengthens-regional-security-diplomacy/)).

## 🌍 Tier 2 — Middle East & Africa
**Gaza: new displacement after Israeli ground operation.** Thousands of Palestinians were again forced from makeshift shelters after Israeli forces launched a major ground operation in central Gaza, the first major offensive publicly announced by the military since the October 2025 ceasefire. The Israeli military issued forced evacuation orders covering three blocks; families spent the night in a schoolyard without blankets or bedding. The military later told AFP it had finished the operation. ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/19/palestinians-in-gaza-face-new-displacement-after-israeli-army-operation))

**Save the Children: Gaza children face "unmatched" dangers.** The group's annual global report says at least 20,000 children have been killed in Gaza after three years of war, and that almost one third of the attacks and violations against children worldwide were recorded there. ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/19/save-the-children-gazas-children-face-unmatched-dangers))

**Yemen: displacement and water crisis.** OCHA says displacement continues to rise amid hostilities; IOM reports more than 353,000 people have fled since the escalation began, nearly 24,000 this week, including 144,000 in Ta'iz ([OCHA](https://www.unocha.org/news/todays-top-news-yemen-occupied-palestinian-territory-sudan)). In Taiz, UNICEF says water production has nearly halved, and more than 800,000 people are reported cut off from supply under a Houthi siege that began about six weeks ago ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/19/yemens-besieged-city-of-taiz-faces-a-deepening-water-crisis)).

**Southern Ethiopia: almost 700,000 displaced.** Islamic Relief reports fighting between Somali and Oromo communities near Moyale, which began on 22 October, has displaced about 329,000 people in Somali Region's Dawa Zone and about 359,800 in Southern Oromia. Access is difficult as agencies prepare to respond. ([ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/ethiopia/around-700000-displaced-amid-volatile-conflict-southern-ethiopia-islamic-relief-says))

**Malawi: WFP prioritises refugee food aid.** Citing funding shortfalls, WFP will from November prioritise the most vulnerable refugees. In Dzaleka camp (65,000+ refugees), 35,000 people will receive monthly cash of USD 8 per person, covering 50 percent of food needs, down from up to 75 percent previously. ([ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/malawi/funding-shortages-force-wfp-prioritise-food-assistance-refugees-malawi-world-food-programme))

**Somalia expels UAE ambassador.** Somalia ordered the UAE ambassador to leave within 72 hours and withdrew its envoy, accusing the UAE of violations of sovereignty amounting to an "open invasion". ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/19/somalia-expels-uae-ambassador-accuses-abu-dhabi-of-violating-sovereignty))

**DR Congo (headline only).** Humanitarian Key Figures (as of 31 October 2026) published ([ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/democratic-republic-congo/dr-congo-humanitarian-key-figures-31-october-2026)).

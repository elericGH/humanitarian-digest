# 🌍 Weekly Humanitarian Digest — 17 August 2026 – 24 August 2026
*Produced Monday, 24 August 2026 | Australian Interest Priority Edition*

---

## 🇦🇺 Australian Priority Alerts

ACFID's [State of the Sector Report 2026](https://acfid.asn.au/a-new-era-australian-aid-agencies-adapt-partner-and-maintain-accountability/), launched 6 August, continued to reverberate through the sector this week: real public donations to member agencies remain around 20% below 2015 levels, and the share of Australian adults donating fell from 8.3% (2016) to 5.7% (2023), even as ACFID members raised $750 million from more than 793,000 Australians in 2023–24. The report frames a sector under pressure from rising humanitarian need, climate stress and narrowing civic space, at the same time as the Albanese Government's [2026-27 aid programme](https://pina.com.fj/2026/08/10/australia-maintains-5-2b-aid-programme-as-pacific-takes-priority/) holds at $5.2 billion, with more than three-quarters directed to the Pacific ($2.2bn) and Southeast Asia ($1.4bn).

[DFAT](https://www.dfat.gov.au/development/topics/development-issues/building-resilience/australias-humanitarian-assistance) continues to lead Australia's international crisis response under its Humanitarian Policy, with the Indo-Pacific reaffirmed as the priority region for 2026-27 delivery. The [Australian Humanitarian Partnership](https://www.dfat.gov.au/development/topics/development-issues/building-resilience/humanitarian-preparedness-and-response/australian-humanitarian-partnership), the ten-year DFAT–NGO partnership running to 2027, remains the main channel for AHP-funded NGO responses, including CAN DO/ADRA-led activations.

[Emergency Action Alliance](https://emergencyaction.org.au/disasters/), the 15-charity coalition coordinating Australian public appeals for overseas disasters, is running an active appeal for the Myanmar earthquake response; no new EAA appeal launched specifically within the past seven days, but fundraising for Myanmar continues.

## 🌏 Tier 1 — Pacific & Asia-Pacific

The Pacific enters a consequential fortnight ahead of the [55th Pacific Islands Forum Leaders Meeting](https://islandsbusiness.com/pacnews/pacnews-two-19-august-2026/) in Koror, Palau (30 August – 4 September, themed "Building Economies: Life. Action. Unity."). Leaders are expected to endorse a draft Regional Peace and Security Action Plan 2026-2030 and to reassert a common regional voice on climate and sovereignty amid intensifying geopolitical competition. Taiwan's expected attendance has drawn a sharp response from Beijing, which has urged the Forum Secretariat to "strictly adhere to the one-China principle," per [The Diplomat](https://thediplomat.com/2026/08/why-the-pacific-islands-forum-should-watch-taiwans-east-coast/) and [Islands Business PACNEWS](https://islandsbusiness.com/pacnews/pacnews-one-19-august-2026/) reporting this week. China separately dismissed Palau President Surangel Whipps Jr's concerns over a recent missile test as "completely groundless."

Climate and disaster stress continues across Melanesia: [Asia Pacific Report](https://asiapacificreport.nz/2026/08/19/on-another-scale-png-province-unprepared-for-el-nino-impacts-warns-governor/) reported a PNG provincial governor warning his province is "on another scale" unprepared for intensifying El Niño impacts, while a companion report noted [snowfall in West Papua alongside severe drought](https://asiapacificreport.nz/2026/08/20/snowfall-in-west-papua-while-severe-drought-hits-other-parts-of-new-guinea/) in other parts of New Guinea, underscoring the uneven climate pressures facing the region. Fiji continues to manage a domestic fuel crisis affecting its national airline, per [Pacific Islands News Roundup](https://pireport.org/2026/08/20/pacific-islands-news-roundup-august-13-august-19-2026/).

Tropical cyclone activity remained elevated across the Pacific basin this week. [Pacific Disaster Center](https://www.pdc.org/weather-wall/) tracking showed multiple active systems including Tropical Cyclone Lala, Tropical Cyclone 02C, and Typhoon Saudel, while the US National Hurricane Center issued advisories for a system forecast to become Tropical Storm Moke, tracking south of Hawaii with 5–10 inches of rainfall forecast for the Big Island and flash flood/mudslide risk in areas already hit by Hurricane Lala.

## 🌍 Tier 2 — Middle East & Africa

Sudan remains, in IFRC's words, "one of the world's largest humanitarian crises... also one of its most overlooked": 35 million people need assistance, more than 9 million are internally displaced and 4.5 million have fled the country ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/4/red-cross-urges-international-aid-as-sudan-crisis-deepens)). This week's reporting shows the crisis deepening further — 33.7 million now require urgent assistance, over 61.7% of the population is acutely food insecure, and a drone strike hit a UN convoy in North Darfur while an armed attack forced MSF to suspend operations at Zalingei hospital amid a cholera outbreak ([Health Policy Watch / ACAPS reporting](https://www.acaps.org/en/countries/sudan)). The UN Human Rights Office separately reported attacks on markets and civilian infrastructure in North Darfur and Khartoum killing at least 64 civilians in the past week ([ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/updates?view=headlines)).

Gaza's humanitarian situation remains critical and largely unchanged in severity: nearly 2.1 million people remain concentrated in less than half the Strip amid daily strikes, shelling and naval fire; 63% of assessed households access less than six litres of drinking water per person per day ([OCHA oPt](https://www.ochaopt.org/content/humanitarian-situation-report-14-august-2026)). OCHA has warned the food emergency could tip into a renewed famine, with children still bearing the effects of the August–December 2025 famine ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/features/2026/8/9/gazas-children-still-struggling-with-hunger-as-another-famine-threatens)). UNRWA continues to report it has been blocked from directly delivering aid and personnel into Gaza since March 2025.

The DRC's Ebola outbreak (Bundibugyo ebolavirus) has become the fastest-growing and second-largest on record: as of 20 August, confirmed cases reached 5,290 with 2,516 deaths, spread across 54 health zones in six provinces, with 81 new cases and 40 deaths reported 18–19 August alone ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/21/ebola-outbreak-growing-faster-%E2%81%A0%E2%81%A0wider-as-drc-death-toll-passes-2500-un), [WHO](https://www.who.int/emergencies/situations/ebola-outbreak---drc-2026)). No vaccine or specific treatment exists for this Ebola species, complicating an already conflict- and displacement-affected response environment. Separately, the ICRC facilitated the handover of 15 people released by Congolese authorities to AFC/M23 in North Kivu on 7 August under the Doha Mechanism ([ICRC](https://www.icrc.org/en/news-release/democratic-republic-congo-icrc-hands-over-15-people-released-congolese-authorities)).

## 🌐 Tier 3 — Rest of the World

Colombia continues recovery from the 7.4-magnitude Chocó earthquake of 10 August, now with a confirmed toll exceeding 250–329 deaths, over 4,600 injured and 247 still missing; more than 1,500 homes were damaged or destroyed along with health centres and schools ([CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/08/10/world/live-news/colombia-earthquake-san-jose-del-palmar), [Direct Relief](https://www.directrelief.org/2026/08/colombia-earthquake-7-4-magnitude-direct-relief-response/)). OCHA has launched an addendum to the 2026 Colombia Humanitarian Plan to fund the response. Direct Relief is mobilising medical aid.

Venezuela's earthquake response remains active more than two months after the June 24 magnitude 7.2/7.5 events: official figures released 17 August put the toll at 6,438 dead and 86,358 injured or hospitalised, with roughly 19,583 people still living in 108 temporary camps and IOM tracking over 12,000 displaced individuals across four states ([IOM](https://www.iom.int/resources/venezuela-bolivarian-republic-earthquake-response-report-no-22-11-13-august-2026), [OCHA](https://www.unocha.org/publications/report/venezuela-bolivarian-republic/earthquakes-venezuela-situation-report-32-07-august-2026-time-0200-pm)) — a reminder the country was already managing one of the world's largest displacement crises before the quakes struck.

Bangladesh's food security outlook continues to deteriorate: over 15.3 million people face acute food insecurity, a figure ReliefWeb reporting expects to climb to 18.1 million (including 787,000 in Emergency conditions) by year end, driven by climate shocks, food costs and inflation ([ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/updates?view=headlines)). In Lebanon, MAG and the Lebanon Mine Action Centre have deployed teams to assess mine contamination following the recent ceasefire, while the US State Department's halt on global mine-clearing funding has left demining organisations worldwide, including in Lebanon, in limbo.

## 🚨 Sudden Onset Hazards

Tropical cyclone activity dominates this week's hazard picture. PDC's DisasterAWARE tracker recorded Tropical Cyclone Lala, Tropical Cyclone 02C, and Typhoon Saudel active simultaneously, alongside several tropical invests (94W, 96W, 97W) across the Pacific basin. The US National Hurricane Center's Tropical Depression Two-C was forecast to strengthen into Tropical Storm Moke and track south of Hawaii, with 5–10 inches of rain forecast for the Big Island and elevated flash-flood and mudslide risk in areas already affected by Hurricane Lala ([weather.com](https://weather.com/2026/08/20/storms/hurricane/video/tropical-depression-two-c-moke-tropical-threat-hawaii-development)).

Europe's wildfire season has burned roughly 500,000 hectares amid record heat, forcing thousands to flee, while record rainfall in Chiba, Japan, left more than 22,000 households without power during the Bon holiday week ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/tag/humanitarian-crises)). GDACS continues to log a steady stream of moderate seismic activity across Indonesia and the wider Pacific Ring of Fire; no new sudden-onset event this week matched the scale of the Colombia or Venezuela earthquakes.

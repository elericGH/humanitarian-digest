## 🇦🇺 Australian Priority Alerts

**Australia joins Canada, Italy and the UK in urging Israel to let shelter aid into Gaza.** A joint statement by the four Foreign Ministers says the humanitarian situation is dire as winter takes hold, that recent floods were "only the first storm of the winter", and that 150,000 tents and other supplies are being held at the border. They call on Israel to open the crossings, allow the tents in and permit dual-use items needed for humanitarian operations and early recovery. Published 16 Nov 2026 (via ReliefWeb, source: Government of Australia): https://reliefweb.int/report/occupied-palestinian-territory/joint-foreign-ministers-statement-gaza-16-november-2026

**Philippines and Australia sign a new Defence Cooperation Agreement.** The Diplomat reports the agreement builds on a series of bilateral defence accords, including the 2007 Status of Visiting Forces Agreement, and is signed amid pressure from China (feed summary only): https://thediplomat.com/2026/11/philippines-australia-sign-new-defense-agreement-amid-china-pressure/

**Oceania epidemic-alert map lists Australia (headline only).** ReliefWeb posted a map of epidemic and emerging disease alerts in Oceania dated 10 November 2026, covering Australia, New Zealand, Samoa, the Marshall Islands and French Polynesia among others: https://reliefweb.int/map/world/carte-des-alertes-de-maladies-epidemiques-et-emergentes-en-oceanie-10-novembre-2026

## 🌏 Tier 1 — Pacific & Asia-Pacific

**Tropical Cyclone Xavier intensifies to category 2 over the Northern Cook Islands.** The Cook Islands Meteorological Service has issued a tropical cyclone alert for Pukapuka and Nassau, with a storm warning still in force for Manihiki and Rakahanga. The Fiji Meteorological Service says Xavier is likely to become a severe tropical cyclone on Tuesday; damaging gale-force winds, heavy rain and thunderstorms are forecast and residents are advised to secure property: https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/pacific/2633910/cyclone-xavier-intensifies-to-category-2-alert-issued-for-pukapuka-and-nassau (earlier report: https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/pacific/2620097/tropical-cyclone-xavier-to-bring-damaging-winds-to-northern-cook-islands)

**PNG's three-child policy draws mixed response from health experts.** RNZ reports the government announced the policy and experts are divided; one quoted line is "Half our population are children": https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/pacific/2633584/half-our-population-are-children-png-s-three-child-policy-draws-mixed-response-from-health-experts

**Vanuatu starts a second international legal proceeding against France** over a territorial dispute (feed summary): https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/pacific/2629975/vanuatu-initiates-legal-action-on-second-front-in-territorial-dispute-with-france. Separately, Pacific tuna stocks are at the centre of regional fisheries talks in Port Vila: https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/pacific/2626606/pacific-tuna-stocks-at-centre-of-regional-fisheries-talks-in-port-vila

**New Pacific police ministers group targets regional crime.** Per the Indo-Pacific Defense Forum, some 20 Pacific countries, territories and partners are involved (feed summary): https://ipdefenseforum.com/2026/11/new-pacific-police-ministers-group-targets-regional-crime/

**Myanmar and ASEAN.** ASEAN leaders pledged to pursue "calibrated engagement" with Myanmar (https://thediplomat.com/2026/11/asean-leaders-pledge-to-pursue-calibrated-engagement-with-myanmar/). ## 🌍 Tier 2 — Middle East & Africa

**Yemen: over 340,000 people displaced since early August.** OCHA says the UN Human Rights Office has verified 300+ civilian casualties since August (70+ killed, about half of them children). Clashes have reached Kharaz refugee camp in Lahj, putting about 9,600 refugees at risk. Access restrictions affect an estimated 1.3 million people in five districts, and strikes on Al Hodeidah fuel infrastructure including Ras Isa have cut port activity. A measles–rubella campaign launched 7 Nov has vaccinated 600,000+ children: https://www.unocha.org/news/todays-top-news-yemen-occupied-palestinian-territory-syria. Al Jazeera reports Saudi Arabia dismissed the commander of coalition forces fighting the Houthis as government forces withdraw from the Red Sea coast to halt a Houthi approach to Aden: https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/16/saudi-arabia-dismisses-commander-of-coalition-forces-fighting-houthis

**Gaza: strikes and medicine shortages.** OCHA says an Israeli air strike hit very close to a UN-led fuel convoy in Rafah, and that 42% of medicines and about 73% of open-heart surgery supplies are virtually out of stock (Ministry of Health, October data). Response to the latest flooding continues: https://www.unocha.org/news/todays-top-news-yemen-occupied-palestinian-territory-syria

**Syria: nearly four million returns since December 2024.** In the Security Council briefing, OCHA said 60,000 more IDPs and 18,000 refugees returned since October, but 730,000+ people remain in camps and explosive ordnance, shelter and services remain major barriers: https://www.unocha.org/news/syrians-cannot-afford-more-instability-un-deputy-relief-chief-tells-security-council

**Sudan/Chad.** UNHCR posted an update on arrivals from Sudan in Chad (15 Nov; headline only): https://reliefweb.int/report/chad/core-emergency-situation-chad-update-arrivals-sudan-15-nov-2026. Qatar Red Crescent launched a paediatric open-heart surgery convoy in Khartoum State (headline only): https://reliefweb.int/report/sudan/qrcs-launches-pediatric-open-heart-surgery-convoy-khartoum-state-enar

**Nigeria's disappeared.** The New Humanitarian reports on families seeking answers about arbitrary arrests, unlawful detention, extrajudicial executions and enforced disappearances: https://www.thenewhumanitarian.org/news-feature/2026/11/16/nigeria-disappeared-families-left-struggle-answers-labyrinth-secret

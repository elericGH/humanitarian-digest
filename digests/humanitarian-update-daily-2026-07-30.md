## 🇦🇺 Australian Priority Alerts

The Lowy Institute's ninth annual Pacific Aid Map shows Australia remains by far the leading provider of grants and loans to the Pacific, disbursing US$1.5 billion in 2024, more than three times New Zealand's contribution and 37% of all development finance to the region. ACFID chief executive Matthew Maury welcomed Australia's continued leadership but urged that it "shouldn't be viewed only through a strategic or geopolitical lens," calling for effective development cooperation to stay focused on reducing poverty and strengthening resilience as other donors cut aid budgets. [Canberra Times / AAP](https://www.canberratimes.com.au/story/9319546/australia-helps-pacific-islands-dodge-worst-of-aid-cuts/)

Ahead of next week's Pacific Islands Forum Foreign Ministers Meeting in Suva, analysts say Australia will push for a strong collective statement condemning China's recent nuclear-capable missile test over the Pacific, a move several Pacific governments have already criticised but the Forum has yet to formally address due to its consensus rules. The outcome will shape Australia's diplomatic and aid posture in the region heading into the Pacific Islands Forum Leaders Meeting in Palau at the end of August. [PINA / Pacific Media Network](https://pina.com.fj/2026/07/29/pacific-leaders-play-both-sides-as-china-divides-forum-over-missile-test-expert/)

## 🌏 Tier 1 — Pacific & Asia-Pacific

Regional diplomacy is in flux ahead of the Suva Foreign Ministers Meeting, with New Zealand's Foreign Minister Winston Peters separately reaffirming support for stronger Pacific parliamentary cooperation and women's leadership in the lead-up to the talks. [PINA](https://pina.com.fj/2026/07/29/nz-foreign-minister-backs-stronger-pacific-womens-leadership-ahead-of-forum-meeting/)

Papua New Guinea's LNG exports have been disrupted after Taiwan's state-owned CPC Corp temporarily suspended spot-market purchases following a diplomatic dispute over PNG's closure of an unofficial Taiwanese office, a development regional observers are watching for its potential flow-on effects to PNG's economy. [PINA](https://pina.com.fj/2026/07/29/taiwan-halts-lng-spot-purchases-from-png-over-office-closure/)

Aftershocks continue in Kyushu, Japan, following the magnitude 7.1 earthquake centred on Kumamoto Prefecture on 28 July, with authorities warning residents that further strong quakes remain possible; a tsunami advisory issued for the Ariake and Yatsushiro Seas has since been lifted. [U.S. Embassy Japan](https://jp.usembassy.gov/alert-kyushu-earthquake-2026/)

## 🌍 Tier 2 — Middle East & Africa

Escalating violence across Sudan's Darfur region has displaced more than 20,000 people in recent weeks, the UN said, as Sudan's US$2.9 billion humanitarian appeal remains only 40% funded (US$1.1 billion received). Fighting between RSF-allied tribal militias and targeted attacks on the Zaghawa community in North Darfur are compounding a cholera outbreak that has recorded more than 2,000 suspected cases and 170 deaths since May, mostly in Kordofan. [Sudan Tribune](https://sudantribune.com/article/316716)

Dozens of civilians were killed in a wave of air and drone strikes across North Kordofan State between 24 and 26 July, a legal rights group reported, including attacks on a water station, a health centre and a school, alongside an Antonov strike on a civilian fuel vehicle. Emergency Lawyers said the pattern of strikes on civilian infrastructure may amount to war crimes and is driving fresh displacement. [Sudan Tribune](https://sudantribune.com/article/316727)

The Blue Nile Civil Society Initiative issued an urgent appeal for international intervention on 28 July, warning that heavy seasonal rains have severely degraded conditions for displaced people around Ed Damazin, triggering disease outbreaks amid acute shortages of shelter, clean water and medical care. [Sudan Tribune](https://sudantribune.com/article/316760)

Seven cholera cases were confirmed across Darfur states between 5 and 25 July, according to Darfur's General Coordination for Refugees and Displaced Persons, alongside seven measles cases near Golo in Jebel Marra; the group has appealed to the UN, African Union and international agencies for emergency food, medicine and clean water supplies. [Sudan Tribune](https://sudantribune.com/article/316707)

## 🚨 Sudden Onset Hazards

GDACS is tracking three active tropical cyclones as of 29 July: Fausto (167 km/h), Genevieve (259 km/h) and Dolphin (278 km/h). Wildfires continue to burn across Spain and France amid an ongoing heatwave, with a new Copernicus EMS activation (EMSR903) triggered for a wildfire in Le Kef and Beja governorates, Tunisia, and earlier reporting indicating more than 360,000 people evacuated across Spain and France this week. Aftershocks continue following Japan's magnitude 7.1 Kyushu earthquake of 28 July. [GDACS](https://www.gdacs.org/)

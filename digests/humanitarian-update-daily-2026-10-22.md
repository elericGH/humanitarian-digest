## 🌏 Tier 1 — Pacific & Asia-Pacific
**El Niño warning covers Asia and the Pacific.** At a joint press conference with the WMO in Geneva on 21 October, UN Emergency Relief Coordinator Tom Fletcher called the current El Niño "a wrecking ball" that will hit Latin America, Eastern and Southern Africa, Asia and the Pacific with extreme heat, droughts and floods, and said 50 million people could face increased hunger. He said he has released over $50 million from the Central Emergency Response Fund ([OCHA](https://www.unocha.org/news/un-relief-chief-warns-el-nino-wrecking-ball-calls-urgent-global-action)).

**West Papua: civil society seeks UN and Pacific access.** West Papuan civil society organisations have called on Indonesia to allow the UN High Commissioner for Human Rights to visit and to open inclusive dialogue. RNZ reports violent conflict has deepened in the interior of Papua in recent months, causing an increasing number of internally displaced people. Pacific countries have recently echoed the 2019 Pacific Islands Forum call for a UN rights office visit ([RNZ Pacific](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/pacific/2070620/west-papuan-civil-society-calls-for-un-and-pacific-access-to-region)).

## 🌍 Tier 2 — Middle East & Africa
**Sudan: children among hundreds reportedly killed in drone and air attacks.** Save the Children says at least 18 children are among hundreds of people reportedly killed in a wave of drone attacks and airstrikes across Sudan in the past week. The UN reports at least 10 children killed in drone attacks over the weekend and three more on Monday in Khartoum ([UNHCR proxy via ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/sudan/sudan-least-18-children-killed-wave-airborne-attacks)).

**Ethiopia: ICRC responds to renewed hostilities in the north.** The ICRC reports drone strikes and fighting in Tigray and continued armed violence in Amhara affecting communities, health facilities and infrastructure. It facilitated the evacuation of 161 people from Mekelle to Semera on 28 September and, with the Ethiopian Red Cross Society, delivered medical equipment and medicines to health facilities ([ICRC via ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/ethiopia/ethiopia-icrc-responds-humanitarian-needs-amid-renewed-hostilities-north)).

**Occupied Palestinian Territory.** An ACAPS thematic report on deteriorating humanitarian conditions in Area C of the West Bank was posted (headline only) ([ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/occupied-palestinian-territory/acaps-thematic-report-palestine-deteriorating-humanitarian-conditions-area-c-west-bank-21-september-2026)). A related item, "Urgent Action Needed to Protect Palestinian Olive Harvest", was also posted (headline only) ([ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/occupied-palestinian-territory/urgent-action-needed-protect-palestinian-olive-harvest)).

**Yemen (headline only).** Al Jazeera: "Yemen holds mass funeral for 75 recruits killed in Houthi strike" ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/video/newsfeed/2026/10/21/aje-onl-nf_funeral-in-yemen-for-recruits-killed-by-houthis-211026)).

## 🌐 Tier 3 — Rest of the World
**Panama: earthquake response.** Ten days after the magnitude 7.5 earthquake on the Azuero peninsula, UNHCR's 20 October situation report cites national civil protection figures of 2,318 homes with total damage and 5,546 with partial damage, damage to more than 600 schools and 120 health facilities, and 55 temporary shelters hosting 3,827 people. Authorities report 27 injured and 917 aftershocks as of 19 October ([UNHCR via ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/panama/acnur-respuesta-de-emergencia-al-terremoto-en-panama-octubre-20-2026)).

**Ukraine: ICRC on care for prisoners of war.** The ICRC published a field article on improving continuity of care for prisoners of war through coordination in Dnipro (headline only) ([ICRC](https://www.icrc.org/en/article/dnipro-improving-continuity-care-prisoners-war-through-coordination)).

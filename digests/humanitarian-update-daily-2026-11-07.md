## 🌏 Tier 1 — Pacific & Asia-Pacific

IFRC says National Red Cross and Red Crescent Societies across Asia Pacific are strengthening preparedness as El Niño deepens, issued from Kuala Lumpur on 6 November as world leaders gather at COP31: [ReliefWeb/IFRC](https://reliefweb.int/report/world/el-nino-deepens-across-asia-pacific-highlighting-future-climate-risks-and-need-greater-preparedness).

Taiwan says it is closing its representative office in Papua New Guinea and will stop buying PNG liquefied natural gas, blaming Chinese political pressure; PNG had earlier (July) moved to shutter the office: [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/6/taiwan-to-close-representative-office-in-papua-new-guinea-blames-china).

RNZ reports the Pacific Islands Forum is expanding partnerships with Middle East countries (headline only): [RNZ Pacific](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/pacific/2394866/shared-values-see-pacific-forum-increase-partnerships-with-middle-east-countries). RNZ also reports Pacific warnings of debt pressure as the Commonwealth looks to strengthen climate finance (headline only): [RNZ Pacific](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/pacific/2380049/pacific-warns-of-debt-pressure-as-commonwealth-looks-to-strengthen-climate-finance).

World Vision published Situation Report #5 on its humanitarian response to flooding in Cambodia (31 October) (headline only): [ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/cambodia/world-vision-situation-report-5-humanitarian-response-flooding-31-october-2026). UNICEF Cambodia issued Situation Report No.1 on El Niño 2026-2027 preparedness (headline only): [ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/cambodia/unicef-cambodia-situation-report-no1-el-nino-2026-2027-preparedness-october-2026).

India: a 6 Nov situation report on Cyclones Arnab and Murasu says Andhra Pradesh's toll from Murasu (landfall near Ongole on 3 Nov) has risen to 8 deaths; Arnab caused 3 deaths in a Kandhamal landslide in Odisha, and 4 Chennai fishers were found dead on the Prakasam coast: [ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/india/cyclones-arnab-murasu-andhra-pradesh-odisha-telangana-tamil-nadu-and-yanam-date-6-november-2026-time-1800-ist).

## 🌍 Tier 2 — Middle East & Africa

OCHA reports more than 313,000 people displaced in Yemen since the beginning of August, with Ta'iz Governorate taking the largest share; the UN Human Rights Office has verified 281 civilian casualties (69 killed, 212 injured): [OCHA](https://www.unocha.org/news/todays-top-news-yemen-democratic-republic-congo).

DRC: armed men thought to be linked to the ADF raided Cantine, about 50 km west of Beni in North Kivu, overnight; local sources counted at least 33 bodies and more than 50 houses burned, and a health post was set alight, forcing about 180 patients, including suspected and one confirmed Ebola case, to flee. OCHA says the Ebola response was disrupted: [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/6/armed-men-kill-at-least-33-in-village-in-ebola-hit-eastern-drc); [OCHA](https://www.unocha.org/news/todays-top-news-yemen-democratic-republic-congo).

Sudan: IOM and UNHCR warn millions risk losing emergency shelter as funding runs out (headline only): [ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/sudan/iom-and-unhcr-warn-millions-sudan-risk-losing-emergency-shelter-funding-runs-out); [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/6/millions-fleeing-sudans-war-risk-losing-shelter-as-funds-run-low-un-warns).

Lebanon: Save the Children says child malnutrition and hunger are rising, with Israeli attacks having destroyed almost one quarter of agricultural land in the south: [ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/lebanon/childrens-hunger-rising-southern-lebanon-farms-struggle-recover). Al Jazeera reports Israeli settlers seized Palestinian homes in Jericho, displacing dozens (headline only): [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/video/newsfeed/2026/11/6/israeli-settlers-seize-palestinian-homes-in-jericho-displacing-dozens).
